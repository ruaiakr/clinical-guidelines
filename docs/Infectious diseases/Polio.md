<h1 class="pccm-page-title">Polio</h1>

<div class="pccm-guideline-panel">
<h2 class="pccm-section-heading">Recommend</h2>
<ul class="pccm-content-list">
<li>Ensure high vaccination coverage through the use of inactivated polio vaccine (IPV).</li>
<li>Early identification of polio cases is essential to prevent outbreaks and provide supportive care.</li>
<li>In endemic regions, active surveillance and immunization campaigns should be ongoing.</li>
</ul>
<h2 class="pccm-section-heading">Background</h2>
<ul class="pccm-content-list">
<li>Polio (poliomyelitis) is a viral infection caused by the poliovirus, which can lead to paralysis, respiratory failure, or death. While it has been largely eradicated worldwide through vaccination, sporadic cases still occur in areas with low vaccination coverage.</li>
</ul>
</div>

<h2 class="pccm-section-heading-blue" id="may-present-with">1. May present with</h2>
<ul class="pccm-content-list">
<li>Fever, fatigue, headache</li>
<li>Muscle stiffness and pain, particularly in the back and legs</li>
<li>Progressive muscle weakness or paralysis (often asymmetric)</li>
<li>Difficulty breathing or swallowing in severe cases</li>
</ul>

<h2 class="pccm-section-heading-blue" id="immediate-management">2. Immediate management</h2>
<ul class="pccm-content-list">
<li>Provide supportive care for symptoms such as pain and fever.</li>
<li>Monitor for signs of respiratory distress and prepare for potential ventilation support if necessary. See <a href="/docs/Emergency/Oxygen-delivery">Oxygen delivery</a>.</li>
<li>Ensure proper hydration and nutrition.</li>
</ul>

<h2 class="pccm-section-heading-blue" id="clinical-assessment">3. Clinical assessment</h2>
<ul class="pccm-content-list">
<li>Assess for muscle weakness or paralysis, particularly in the legs.</li>
<li>Evaluate for respiratory involvement, which can be life-threatening.</li>
<li>Check immunization history to confirm vaccination status.</li>
</ul>

<h2 class="pccm-section-heading-blue" id="management">4. Management</h2>
<ul class="pccm-content-list">
<li>Initiate supportive care including hydration, nutrition, and pain management.</li>
<li>Monitor for complications such as respiratory failure and provide appropriate interventions.</li>
<li>Refer for specialized care if respiratory support is needed.</li>
</ul>

<h2 class="pccm-section-heading-blue" id="follow-up">5. Follow-up</h2>
<ul class="pccm-content-list">
<li>Regular monitoring for any signs of muscle recovery or worsening paralysis.</li>
<li>Follow-up visits to assess for any long-term effects such as post-polio syndrome.</li>
</ul>

<h2 class="pccm-section-heading-blue" id="referral-consultation">6. Referral/Consultation</h2>
<ul class="pccm-content-list">
<li>Refer to a specialist of any suspected case due to risk of respiratory failure or severe paralysis that may develop.</li>
<li>Consider referral and education for physical therapy and rehabilitation to manage long-term effects.</li>
</ul>
