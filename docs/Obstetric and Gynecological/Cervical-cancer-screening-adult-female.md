<h1 class="pccm-page-title">Cervical cancer screening adult female (Kiribati Original Protocol)</h1>

<div class="pccm-guideline-panel">
<h2 class="pccm-section-heading">Recommend<sup>1–5</sup></h2>
<ul class="pccm-content-list">
<li>Cervical cancer is preventable through regular screening and early treatment of precancerous lesions</li>
<li>Screen all eligible women aged 30–49 years, or earlier if HIV-positive</li>
<li>Visual Inspection with Acetic Acid (VIA) is the recommended screening method</li>
<li>HPV vaccination and safe sexual practices reduce cervical cancer risk</li>
<li>Encourage community education and reduce stigma around screening</li>
</ul>
<h2 class="pccm-section-heading">Background<sup>1–5</sup></h2>
<ul class="pccm-content-list">
<li>Cervical cancer is the fourth most common cancer among women globally, but highly preventable</li>
<li>In Kiribati, cervical cancer is a leading cause of cancer-related deaths in women</li>
<li>Most cases are linked to persistent infection with high-risk human papillomavirus (HPV) types</li>
<li>Screening allows early detection of precancerous changes and timely treatment</li>
<li>VIA is a simple, low-cost screening method suitable for use in low-resource settings</li>
</ul>
</div>

<h2 class="pccm-section-heading-blue" id="may-present-with">1. May present with</h2>
<p>(Screening may be initiated even if asymptomatic)</p>
<ul class="pccm-content-list">
<li>Woman aged 30–49 years, or sexually active woman requesting screening</li>
<li>History of multiple sexual partners, early sexual debut, or STI</li>
<li>HIV-positive status (start screening earlier and repeat more frequently)</li>
<li>No prior cervical cancer screening</li>
</ul>

<h2 class="pccm-section-heading-blue" id="immediate-management">2. Immediate management</h2>
<ul class="pccm-content-list">
<li>Ensure privacy and informed consent</li>
<li>Offer VIA screening if trained personnel and supplies are available</li>
<li>Refer if VIA not available or provider not trained</li>
</ul>

<h2 class="pccm-section-heading-blue" id="clinical-assessment">3. Clinical assessment</h2>
<ul class="pccm-content-list">
<li>Confirm age and sexual history</li>
<li>Ask about HIV status (if known), previous screening, and HPV vaccination</li>
<li>Assess for symptoms of invasive cancer (e.g. abnormal bleeding, discharge)</li>
<li>Conduct a pelvic exam and VIA (if trained and available)</li>
<li>Document findings on screening record</li>
</ul>

<h2 class="pccm-section-heading-blue" id="management">4. Management</h2>

<h2 class="pccm-section-heading-orange" id="via-negative">If VIA negative</h2>
<ul class="pccm-content-list">
<li>Reassure patient</li>
<li>Advise next VIA in 3–5 years (or sooner if HIV-positive)</li>
<li>Provide HPV vaccination advice if eligible</li>
</ul>

<h2 class="pccm-section-heading-orange" id="via-positive">If VIA positive (precancerous lesion suspected)</h2>
<ul class="pccm-content-list">
<li>Offer cryotherapy (if available and eligible)</li>
<li>Refer to gynaecology if not eligible for cryotherapy or no cryotherapy available</li>
<li>Counsel on follow-up and possible further treatment</li>
</ul>

<h2 class="pccm-section-heading-orange" id="invasive-cancer">If invasive cancer suspected</h2>
<ul class="pccm-content-list">
<li>Refer urgently to gynaecology or surgical/oncology care</li>
<li>Provide emotional support and ensure follow-up</li>
</ul>

<h2 class="pccm-section-heading-blue" id="follow-up">5. Follow up</h2>
<ul class="pccm-content-list">
<li>VIA-negative: repeat screening in 3–5 years (or every 3 years if HIV-positive)</li>
<li>VIA-positive and treated: follow-up in 1 year</li>
<li>VIA-positive and referred: ensure referral completed and tracked</li>
<li>Monitor program performance and coverage rates</li>
</ul>

<h2 class="pccm-section-heading-blue" id="referral-consultation">6. Referral/consultation</h2>
<ul class="pccm-content-list">
<li>Refer to:
<ul>
<li>Gynaecology or surgical specialist for:
<ul>
<li>Suspected invasive cancer</li>
<li>VIA-positive and not suitable for cryotherapy</li>
<li>Recurrence or treatment failure</li>
<li>HIV clinic if not already in care</li>
</ul>
</li>
<li>MHMS to advise on referral pathways and eligibility for public sector HPV vaccination programs</li>
</ul>
</li>
</ul>
</div>
