<h1 class="pccm-page-title">Oxygen delivery - Adult/Child</h1>

<div class="pccm-guideline-panel">
<h2 class="pccm-section-heading">Recommend</h2>
<ul>
<li>Oxygen is a therapeutic intervention + should only be given if clinically indicated</li>
</ul>
</div>

<div class="pccm-assessment-table-wrap">
<table class="pccm-assessment-table">
<tr class="pccm-table-banner"><td colspan="3">OXYGEN THERAPY BY SCENARIO</td></tr>
<tr>
<th>SCENARIO</th>
<th>RECOMMENDATIONS</th>
<th>TARGET SpO<sub>2</sub></th>
</tr>
<tr>
<td class="pccm-row-label">Shock<br />Major trauma<br />Sepsis<br />Other critical illnesses</td>
<td>
<ul>
<li>Give Airvo 2 (nasal high flow system) or high flow O<sub>2</sub> with non-rebreathing mask at 15 L/minute until stable</li>
</ul>
</td>
<td>≥ 94%</td>
</tr>
<tr>
<td class="pccm-row-label">Acute Stroke</td>
<td>
<ul>
<li>Give if SpO<sub>2</sub> &lt; 92%</li>
</ul>
</td>
<td></td>
</tr>
<tr>
<td class="pccm-row-label">Hypotension</td>
<td>
<ul>
<li>Give until hypoxia can definitely be excluded</li>
</ul>
</td>
<td></td>
</tr>
<tr>
<td class="pccm-row-label">Carbon monoxide poisoning<br />eg house fire</td>
<td>
<ul>
<li>15 L/minute O<sub>2</sub> with non-rebreather mask for 6 hours</li>
<li>Keep at rest to minimise O<sub>2</sub> needs</li>
<li>Pulse oximetry is unreliable + may give false normal or high reading</li>
</ul>
</td>
<td></td>
</tr>
<tr>
<td class="pccm-row-label">Suspected decompression illness or pulmonary barotrauma after diving</td>
<td>
<ul>
<li>Give near 100% O<sub>2</sub> as soon as possible + even if pulse oximetry indicates ↑ SpO<sub>2</sub></li>
<li>Continue until advised by MO</li>
</ul>
</td>
<td></td>
</tr>
<tr>
<td class="pccm-row-label">Paraquat poisoning<br />Previous treatment with Bleomycin</td>
<td>
<ul>
<li>Do not routinely administer O<sub>2</sub> - can be hazardous</li>
<li>Only give if arterial O<sub>2</sub> saturation falls below 90%</li>
</ul>
</td>
<td>88–92%</td>
</tr>
<tr>
<td class="pccm-row-label">Patients at risk of hypercapnic respiratory failure (HRF) eg
<ul>
<li>Previous HRF
<ul>
<li>COPD, bronchiectasis, cystic fibrosis</li>
</ul>
</li>
<li>History of heavy smoking</li>
<li>Obstructive sleep apnoea
<ul>
<li>BMI &gt; 40</li>
</ul>
</li>
<li>Severe kyphoscoliosis or ankylosing spondylitis</li>
<li>Neuromuscular disorders with respiratory muscle weakness</li>
<li>Use of opioids, benzodiazepines</li>
<li>Patients with acute asthma who are tiring</li>
</ul>
</td>
<td>
<ul>
<li>Do not routinely administer O<sub>2</sub> - can be hazardous</li>
<li>Normal SpO<sub>2</sub> is sometimes 90–92%</li>
<li>Excess O<sub>2</sub> can lead to elevated SpO<sub>2</sub> &gt; 95% which ↓ respiratory drive leading to underventilation</li>
<li>Use capnometry if available</li>
</ul>
</td>
<td>88–92%</td>
</tr>
</table>
</div>

</td>
<td>As per manufacturer’s instructions</td>
</tr>

<td class="pccm-row-label">Nasal prongs</td>
<td>
<ul>

<li>Child &lt; 2 years max. 2 L/minute</li>
<li>Child &gt; 2 years–adult max. 4 L/minute</li>
<li>If requires &gt; 4 L/minute use a face mask</li>
<li>High flow nasal cannula, see <a href="/docs/Paediatrics/Bronchiolitis">Bronchiolitis</a></li>
</ul>
</td>
<td>0.125–4</td>
</tr>
