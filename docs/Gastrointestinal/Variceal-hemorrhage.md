Variceal hemorrhage ΓÇô Adult
Recommend
Variceal hemorrhage is a life-threatening complication of portal hypertension,
commonly seen in patients with liver cirrhosis from any cause (e.g. chronic hepatitis
B infection, alcohol use). Screening for hepatitis B (HBsAg) is important if the
patientΓÇÖs status is unknown. Prompt resuscitation, bleeding control, and infection
prevention are essential.
Background
Oesophageal or gastric varices are dilated veins that develop due to portal
hypertension. When these rupture, they can cause massive upper gastrointestinal
bleeding. Early identification and urgent management are critical to reduce
mortality and prevent complications.
