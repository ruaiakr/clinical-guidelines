(up to 24months)
•	 Also see Bronchiolitis - emergency management in children

3.	 Clinical assessment1
•	 Ask about:

–
recent URTI symptoms

–
feeding/hydration:

–
 inadequate feeds eg &lt; 50% of usual feeds over 12 hours

–
duration of feeds

–
number of wet nappies in last 24 hours
•  Do:

–
vital signs

–
listen to chest for crackles or wheeze

–
check ears

–
bare weight
•  Assess for Risk factors for severe disease1
Risk factors for severe disease1
•	 Born &lt; 37 weeks
•	 &lt; 10 weeks of age
•	 Underlying conditions eg congenital heart disease, Down syndrome,
immunocompromised
•	 Failure to thrive
•	 Exposure to cigarette smoke
•	 Breastfed for &lt; 2 months
4.	 Management
•	 Contact MO in all cases, urgently if moderate-severe
Moderate-severe cases1,2
•	 Require urgent evacuation
•	 Monitor closely until evacuation:

–
vital signs + WOB

–
SpO2

–
discontinue O2 if SpO2 persistently > 92%

–
note: brief↓ SpO2 &lt; 92% does not require O2

saline1-3
•	 Only give salbutamol on MO advice
