<div class="pccm-alert-panel">
<p><strong>These are ocular emergencies</strong> — Can be difficult to diagnose / differentiate (Corneal ulcer, Acute iritis, Acute glaucoma)</p>
</div>

<div class="pccm-guideline-panel">
<ul class="pccm-content-list">
<li><strong>Consult MO urgently + urgent evacuation for ophthalmic review</strong></li>
<li>Offer analgesia ± antiemetic. See <a href="/docs/Pain,%20nausea%20and%20vomiting/Acute-pain">Acute pain</a>, <a href="/docs/Pain,%20nausea%20and%20vomiting/Nausea-and-vomiting">Nausea and vomiting</a></li>
<li>If acute glaucoma likely, MO may order:<sup>13</sup>
<ul>
<li>eye drops to ↓ IOP eg 0.5% timolol, 2% pilocarpine + IV/oral acetazolamide</li>
</ul>
</li>
<li>If acute iritis likely, MO may order:<sup>14</sup>
<ul>
<li>corticosteroid eye drops eg dexamethasone AND mydriatic/cycloplegic eye drops eg atropine</li>
</ul>
</li>
<li>Ongoing management as per MO/ophthalmologist</li>
</ul>
</div>
