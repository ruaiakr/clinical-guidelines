<h1 class="pccm-page-title">Shock - Adult/Child</h1>
<div class="pccm-guideline-panel">
<h2 class="pccm-section-heading">Background</h2>
<ul class="pccm-content-list">
<li>Shock is a loss of effective circulation resulting in impaired tissue oxygen + nutrient delivery and causes life-threatening organ failure. Any seriously ill or seriously injured patient is at risk of developing shock<sup>1</sup></li>
<li>Causes include - <a href="/docs/Emergency/Sepsisseptic-shock">sepsis</a>, <a href="/docs/Emergency/Anaphylaxis">anaphylaxis</a>, major haemorrhage, large fluid loss + rarely obstruction of cardiac outflow + cardiogenic failure</li>
<li>Assume a serious cause until proven otherwise<sup>1</sup></li>
</ul>
</div>

<h2 class="pccm-section-heading-blue">1. May present with<sup>1,2</sup></h2>
<ul class="pccm-content-list">
<li>Signs of shock:<ul><li>↓ LOC</li><li>↓ perfusion - mottled or pale skin, cool extremities, capillary refill &gt; 2 seconds ± clammy</li><li>↑ HR, ↑ RR</li></ul></li>
</ul>
<h2 class="pccm-section-heading-blue">2.	 Immediate management</h2>
<ul class="pccm-content-list">
<li>DRSABCD — see <a href="/docs/Emergency/Basic-life-support">BLS</a></li>
<li>Call for help + urgently contact MO when able</li>
<li>If signs of shock + suspected anaphylaxis:<ul><li>give IM adrenaline (epinephrine) 1:1,000 (see <a href="/docs/Emergency/Anaphylaxis">Anaphylaxis</a> for doses)</li><li>repeat every 5 minutes if needed</li></ul></li>
<li>Lie the person down, if unconscious place on side<sup>1</sup></li>
<li>Start resuscitation</li>
<li>Give O<sup>2</sup> 15 L/minute via non-rebreather mask 1,2</li>
<li>Insert IVC x 2 eg 18 G or Intraosseous, if 2 failed attempts</li>
<li>Rapidly give bolus IV sodium chloride 0.9%:2-4<ul><li>adult 250–500 mL, child 20 mL/kg</li><li>reassess + repeat if no improvement in HR, LOC or perfusion</li></ul></li>
<li>If persisting signs of shock after fluid bolus x 2 - urgently contact MO +<ul><li>consider <a href="/docs/Emergency/Sepsisseptic-shock">Sepsis</a>, if suspected or no other cause of shock identified, initiate sepsis pathway + give broad spectrum antibiotics<sup>2</sup></li></ul></li>
<li>Monitor:<ul><li>vital signs, GCS, capillary refill time</li></ul></li>
<li>Continuous cardiac monitoring<sup>2</sup></li>
<li>Look for cause of shock:<ul><li>haemorrhage eg:<ul><li>Ectopic pregnancy - do pregnancy test</li><li>traumatic injuries</li><li>note: if haemorrhagic shock MO may order Tranexamic acid - give within 3hrs</li><li>Upper GI bleeding, Button battery, Rectal bleeding</li></ul></li><li>large fluid loss eg Gastroenteritis - adult, or Gastroenteritis - child</li><li><a href="/docs/Emergency/Sepsisseptic-shock">sepsis/septic shock</a>, <a href="/docs/Emergency/Anaphylaxis">anaphylaxis</a>, brain/spinal injuries</li><li>cardiac dysfunction<sup>3</sup> eg ACS</li><li>blockage of blood flow in/around the heart eg PE</li></ul></li>
</ul>
<h2 class="pccm-section-heading-blue">3.	 Clinical assessment</h2>
<ul class="pccm-content-list">
<li>Get rapid history, ask about:<ul><li>recent illness, nausea/vomiting, diarrhoea</li><li>decreased urine output</li><li>immunocompromised</li><li>underlying conditions eg cancer, heart disease, diabetes</li><li>any medications</li><li>recent drugs or alcohol</li></ul></li>
<li>Do physical assessment, including:<ul><li>listen to chest - any wheeze, crackles</li><li>urinalysis</li><li>check skin - any rash, wounds, bruising</li></ul></li>
<li>abdomen:<ul><li>listen to bowel sounds - present/absent - if absent, consider Bowel obstruction</li><li>palpate for any tenderness, guarding, rigidity, rebound tenderness, masses</li></ul></li>
</ul>
<h2 class="pccm-section-heading-blue">4.	 Management</h2>
<ul class="pccm-content-list">
<li>Ongoing management as per MO + likely cause of shock</li>
</ul>
<h2 class="pccm-section-heading-blue">5.	 Follow up</h2>
<ul class="pccm-content-list">
<li>As per MO</li>
</ul>
<h2 class="pccm-section-heading-blue">6.	 Referral/consultation</h2>
<ul class="pccm-content-list">
<li>Patient might need referral to the main hospital after discussion with MO</li>
</ul>
