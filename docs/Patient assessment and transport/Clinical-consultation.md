<h1 class="pccm-page-title">Clinical consultation</h1>
<div class="pccm-consult-intro">
<div class="pccm-consult-intro-banner">CONSULTING WITH MO/RETRIEVAL COORDINATOR<sup>1</sup></div>
<div class="pccm-consult-intro-body">
<ul>
<li>Be clear and methodical</li>
<li>Write your findings down first, time permitting</li>
<li>Advise early if you think the patient may need retrieval</li>
<li>Say what you think is wrong. Your assessment is important</li>
</ul>
</div>
</div>
<div class="pccm-isbar-wrap">
<table class="pccm-isbar-table">
<tr>
<td class="pccm-isbar-letter">I</td>
<td class="pccm-isbar-content">
<div class="pccm-isbar-title">Identify yourself <strong>AND</strong> identify name and spelling of receiving MO</div>
<ul>
<li>I am ... (your name and role)</li>
<li>I am calling from ... (location)</li>
<li>The best contact number for me is ...</li>
</ul>
</td>
</tr><tr>
<td class="pccm-isbar-letter">S</td>
<td class="pccm-isbar-content">
<div class="pccm-isbar-title">Situation and status - why are you calling</div>
<ul>
<li>I have a patient ... (name, age and gender)</li>
<li>Who is ... (stable/unstable/deteriorating/improving)</li>
<li>Any immediate clinical needs eg attend the hospital, urgent retrieval</li>
<li>I think the patient is/has ... (clinical impression/suspected diagnosis/unsure but worried) eg I have a critically unwell man with sepsis; I have a well female with diabetic foot ulcer</li>
<li>Most recent vital signs and Appropriate Early Warning Tool' score, or local EWARS</li>
</ul>
</td>
</tr><tr>
<td class="pccm-isbar-letter">B</td>
<td class="pccm-isbar-content">
<div class="pccm-isbar-title">Background</div>
<ul>
<li>History of presenting problem, relevant past history</li>
<li>Evaluation - physical examination, findings, investigation findings</li>
<li>Allergies</li>
<li>Current medicines</li>
<li>I have …(taken the following actions eg given O2, inserted IVC, started IV sodium chloride 0.9%)</li>
</ul>
</td>
</tr><tr>
<td class="pccm-isbar-letter">A</td>
<td class="pccm-isbar-content">
<div class="pccm-isbar-title">Assessment and actions</div>
<ul>
<li>General appearance</li>
<li>Weight + additional observations</li>
<li>I am wanting … (advice, orders, retrieval)</li>
<li>Level of urgency is ...</li>
<li>Agree on plan of action with MO/retrieval coordinator</li>
</ul>
</td>
</tr><tr>
<td class="pccm-isbar-letter">R</td>
<td class="pccm-isbar-content">
<div class="pccm-isbar-title">Recommendations and read back</div>
<ul>
<li>Confirm:
<ul>
<li>shared understanding of what needs to happen - who is doing what and when</li>
<li>contact details - name, number to be called back on</li>
<li>follow up requirements and referrals</li>
<li>medication orders</li>
</ul>
</li>
<li>Read back critical information</li>
<li>Identify parameters for review or escalation (any concerns - follow normal/local escalation pathway)</li>
<li>Identify any risks</li>
</ul>
</td>
</tr>
