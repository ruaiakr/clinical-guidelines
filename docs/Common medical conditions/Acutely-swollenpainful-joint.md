<h1 class="pccm-page-title">Acutely swollen/painful joint - Adult</h1>

<div class="pccm-guideline-panel">
<h2 class="pccm-section-heading">Recommend<sup>1,2</sup></h2>
<ul class="pccm-content-list">
<li>The causes can be difficult to diagnose. Be suspicious of septic arthritis (an orthopaedic emergency), acute osteomyelitis + ARF</li>
</ul>
</div>

<h2 class="pccm-section-heading-blue">1. May present with<sup>1-3</sup></h2>
<ul class="pccm-content-list">
<li>Painful, red + swollen joint(s) ± fever, malaise</li>
</ul>

<h2 class="pccm-section-heading-blue">2. Immediate management<sup>1</sup></h2>
<ul class="pccm-content-list">
<li>Vital signs</li>
<li>Screen for <a href="/docs/Emergency/Sepsisseptic-shock">Sepsis</a></li>
</ul>

<h2 class="pccm-section-heading-blue">3. Clinical assessment<sup>1,2</sup></h2>

<div class="pccm-red-flags">
<div class="pccm-red-flags-banner">Red flags<sup>1,2</sup></div>
<div class="pccm-red-flags-body">
<ul>
<li>Acute swelling, redness and marked ↓ in range of motion of joint</li>
<li>Systemic symptoms eg ↑ HR, fever, malaise, night sweats, weight loss<sup>3</sup></li>
</ul>
<ul>
<li>Recent joint surgery</li>
<li>Recent injury/wound (can be minor)</li>
</ul>
</div>
</div>

<ul class="pccm-content-list">
<li>Get history, including:
<ul>
<li>pain - sudden or gradual onset</li>
<li>past episodes ± diagnosis</li>
<li>ARF/RHD diagnosis or family history of<sup>1</sup></li>
<li>other symptoms eg fatigue, skin infection, sore throat<sup>3</sup></li>
<li>IV drug use<sup>2</sup></li>
</ul>
</li>
<li>Examine joints:
<ul>
<li>swelling, tenderness, warmth + mobility</li>
<li>if the pain seems out of proportion to the joint signs - consider ARF</li>
</ul>
</li>
<li>Check for swollen lymph nodes</li>
</ul>

<h2 class="pccm-section-heading-blue">4. Management<sup>1,2</sup></h2>
<ul class="pccm-content-list">
<li>Be suspicious of septic arthritis, acute osteomyelitis and ARF</li>
<li>If highly suggestive of gout, see <a href="/docs/Common medical conditions/Gout">Gout</a></li>
<li>If suspected sprain/soft tissue injury, see <a href="/docs/Emergency/Sprainssoft-tissue-injury">Sprains/soft tissue injury</a></li>
<li>Offer analgesia. See <a href="/docs/Pain,%20nausea%20and%20vomiting/Acute-pain">Acute pain</a></li>
<li>Contact MO if any Red flags</li>
</ul>
