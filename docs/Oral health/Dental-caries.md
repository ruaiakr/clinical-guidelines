<h1 class="pccm-page-title">Dental caries - Adult/Child</h1>
<p class="pccm-page-subtitle">Tooth decay</p>

<div class="pccm-guideline-panel">
<h2 class="pccm-section-heading">Background</h2>
<ul class="pccm-content-list">
<li>People from rural and remote areas are at high risk of dental caries<sup>1</sup></li>
<li>Application of fluoride varnish 2–4 times a year to primary (baby) + permanent (adult) teeth is associated with a substantial reduction in the extent of caries experienced<sup>2</sup></li>
</ul>
</div>

<h2 class="pccm-section-heading-blue" id="may-present-with">1. May present with<sup>2</sup></h2>
<ul class="pccm-content-list">
<li>Tooth/teeth sensitive to hot/cold, biting or pressure</li>
<li>Dental caries (tooth decay) – hole in tooth, broken down tooth, darkened tooth</li>
<li>Risk factors for ↑ risk of dental caries<sup>1</sup> – see the Chronic conditions manual<br /><a href="https://www.health.qld.gov.au/rrcsu/clinical-manuals/chronic-conditions-manual-ccm">www.health.qld.gov.au/rrcsu/clinical-manuals/chronic-conditions-manual-ccm</a></li>
</ul>

<h2 class="pccm-section-heading-blue" id="immediate-management">2. Immediate management</h2>
<ul class="pccm-content-list">
<li>Not applicable</li>
</ul>

<h2 class="pccm-section-heading-blue" id="clinical-assessment">3. Clinical assessment<sup>3</sup></h2>
<ul class="pccm-content-list">
<li>Ask when last dental visit was</li>
<li>Assess for risk factors for dental caries</li>
<li>Examine teeth for dental caries:
<ul>
<li>holes/cavities or structural damage which can be brown or black in appearance</li>
<li>tooth/teeth surfaces with a white or frosty appearance may indicate early stages of decay</li>
<li>pain or sensitivity</li>
<li>bad breath or a bad taste in the mouth</li>
</ul>
</li>
</ul>

<h2 class="pccm-section-heading-blue" id="management">4. Management<sup>3</sup></h2>
<ul class="pccm-content-list">
<li>If toothache, see <a href="/docs/Oral health/Toothache">Toothache</a>.</li>
<li>If symptomatic and caries – Dental Extraction by MA</li>
<li>Ask about any adverse experience associated with previous fluoride varnish application</li>
<li>Offer to apply fluoride varnish to teeth if:
<ul>
<li>there is evidence of dental caries or person is at high risk of dental caries +</li>
<li>regular brushing with fluoride toothpaste is likely to be ineffective +</li>
<li>person is &gt; 18 months old +</li>
<li>there are no contraindications</li>
</ul>
</li>
<li>Refer for dentist review</li>
<li>If patient has been recalled for re-application of fluoride varnish:
<ul>
<li>assess for any changes in risk status</li>
<li>check if patient has had fluoride varnish applied anywhere else during the recall period eg by a dentist</li>
<li>reapply as indicated</li>
</ul>
</li>
<li>Reinforce good oral hygiene. See the Chronic conditions manual<br /><a href="https://www.health.qld.gov.au/rrcsu/clinical-manuals/chronic-conditions-manual-ccm">www.health.qld.gov.au/rrcsu/clinical-manuals/chronic-conditions-manual-ccm</a></li>
</ul>

<h2 class="pccm-section-heading-orange" id="fluoride-varnish">FLUORIDE VARNISH</h2>
<div class="pccm-drug-box">
<div class="pccm-drug-header">
<span class="pccm-drug-name">FLUORIDE VARNISH</span>
<span class="pccm-drug-level">L2B</span>
</div>
<div class="pccm-assessment-table-wrap" style="margin:0; overflow-x:auto;">
<table class="pccm-drug-table">
<tr><th>FORM</th><th>STRENGTH</th><th>ROUTE</th><th>DOSE</th><th>DURATION</th></tr>
<tr>
<td>Liquid</td>
<td>0.4 g/0.4 mL 5% (single dose)<br />50 mg/1 mL (10 mL tube 5%)</td>
<td>Topical to teeth</td>
<td><strong>Child 18 months–6 years</strong><br />up to 0.25 mL<br /><strong>Child 6–12 years</strong><br />up to 0.4 mL<br /><strong>&gt; 12 years–adult</strong><br />up to 0.75 mL<br />Use Duraphat® dosing card if not using single dose preparation</td>
<td>stat<br />Then administer 6 monthly or 3 monthly if indicated<br />Do not supply for self or parent administration</td>
</tr>
</table>
</div>
<div class="pccm-drug-notes">
<p><strong>Offer CMI:</strong> Teeth may appear discoloured temporarily following application. Do not brush teeth on day of application – resume brushing the next morning. Eat soft foods for the rest of the day to minimise disruption of the varnish</p>
<p><strong>Note:</strong> Do not apply if ulcerative gingivitis or stomatitis to avoid discomfort for patient (contains alcohol). Do not leave fluoride varnish unattended when in use. Fluoride varnish is an S4 when used by clinicians other than dental practitioners</p>
<p><strong>Contraindication:</strong> Allergy to colophony (natural rosin) or sticking plaster; any episode of severe allergy or bronchial asthma that has required hospitalisation</p>
<p><strong>Management of associated emergency:</strong> Adverse reactions extremely rare. If occurs contact MO or dentist.</p>
</div>
</div>

<h2 class="pccm-section-heading-blue" id="follow-up">5. Follow up</h2>
<ul class="pccm-content-list">
<li>Arrange re-call for review of oral health status and reapplication of fluoride varnish:
<ul>
<li>if low risk – every 6 months, if high risk – every 3 months</li>
</ul>
</li>
</ul>

<h2 class="pccm-section-heading-blue" id="referral-consultation">6. Referral/consultation</h2>
<ul class="pccm-content-list">
<li>Refer high risk and patients with obvious dental caries to dentist</li>
</ul>
</div>
