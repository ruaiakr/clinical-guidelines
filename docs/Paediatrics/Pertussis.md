<h1 class="pccm-page-title">Pertussis - Adult/Child</h1>
<p class="pccm-page-subtitle">Whooping cough</p>

<div class="pccm-guideline-panel">
<h2 class="pccm-section-heading">Background</h2>
<ul class="pccm-content-list">
<li>Still prevalent - outbreaks occur every 3–4 years. Infants &lt; 6 months at ↑ risk of complications<sup>1</sup></li>
</ul>
</div>

<h2 class="pccm-section-heading-blue" id="may-present-with">1. May present with</h2>
<ul class="pccm-content-list">
<li>Apnoea or cyanosis ± cough - may be only symptom in infants<sup>1</sup></li>
<li>Intermittent violent uncontrollable coughing ± ‘whoop’ when breathing in<sup>1</sup></li>
<li>Persistent cough up to 3 months, otherwise well<sup>1,2</sup></li>
<li>Vomiting following coughing spasms<sup>2</sup></li>
</ul>

<h2 class="pccm-section-heading-blue" id="immediate-management">2. Immediate management</h2>
<ul class="pccm-content-list">
<li>Do vital signs</li>
<li>If SpO<sub>2</sub> ≤ 93%, cyanosis or apnoea:
<ul>
<li>give O<sub>2</sub></li>
<li>contact MO urgently</li>
</ul>
</li>
<li>Use droplet PPE. If able, see patient away from main treatment room<sup>3</sup></li>
</ul>

<h2 class="pccm-section-heading-blue" id="clinical-assessment">3. Clinical assessment<sup>1-3</sup></h2>
<ul class="pccm-content-list">
<li>Ask about:
<ul>
<li>cough - onset, persistent or getting worse, vomiting after coughing</li>
<li>similar illness in household contacts</li>
<li>vaccination status - patient + household contacts<sup>3</sup></li>
<li>other symptoms</li>
</ul>
</li>
<li>Check:
<ul>
<li>WOB. Chest - listen for air entry, crackles, wheeze</li>
<li>weight - bare weight if &lt; 2 years</li>
</ul>
</li>
</ul>

<h2 class="pccm-section-heading-blue" id="management">4. Management</h2>
<ul class="pccm-content-list">
<li>Contact MO promptly if:
<ul>
<li>young child or baby - even if mild symptoms</li>
<li>acutely unwell</li>
</ul>
</li>
<li>Advise Public Health Unit you suspect pertussis</li>
</ul>

<h3 class="pccm-section-heading-orange" id="if-pertussis-diagnosed">If pertussis diagnosed</h3>
<ul class="pccm-content-list">
<li>Consult MO + Public Health Unit, who may advise:
<ul>
<li>if ≤ 3 weeks of cough or other symptom onset give:<sup>2</sup>
<ul>
<li>azithromycin to patient and contacts (i.e. if been within 1 meter of patient for &gt; 1 hour)<sup>2</sup></li>
</ul>
</li>
</ul>
</li>
<li>Advise patient/carer:<sup>3</sup>
<ul>
<li>highly infectious - spreads by coughing/sneezing + direct contact eg wiping nose, mouth</li>
<li>stay away from others until antibiotics have been taken for 5 days, especially:
<ul>
<li>children and babies, pregnant women, work, school, preschool or child care</li>
</ul>
</li>
<li>cough can continue for a few weeks</li>
</ul>
</li>
</ul>

<div class="pccm-drug-box" id="azithromycin">
<div class="pccm-drug-header">
<span class="pccm-drug-name">AZITHROMYCIN</span>
<span class="pccm-drug-level">L2B</span>
</div>

</div>
</div>

<h2 class="pccm-section-heading-blue" id="follow-up">5. Follow up</h2>
<ul class="pccm-content-list">
<li>As per MO:
<ul>
<li>if discharged home advise to return if symptoms worsen or they are concerned</li>
<li>follow up swab result</li>
</ul>
</li>
</ul>

<h2 class="pccm-section-heading-blue" id="referral-consultation">6. Referral/consultation</h2>
<ul class="pccm-content-list">
<li>Notify Public Health Unit of confirmed or suspected pertussis cases<sup>1</sup></li>
</ul>
</div>
