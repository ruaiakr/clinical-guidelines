# Critical emergencies Unconscious/altered level of consciousness - Adult/Child

•	 Advise against diving for people with:

–
Lung disease or asthma

–
History of pneumothorax

–
Epilepsy or seizures

–
Migraine

–
Stroke

–
Heart disease or failure

–
Chronic ear problems or surgery

–
Vertigo

–
Pregnancy

–
Diabetes

–
Bleeding disorders
Additional diving precautions
•	 Do not dive:

–
If flown within last 24 hours

–
After non-decompression dive or dive longer than 2 hours in past 48 hours
