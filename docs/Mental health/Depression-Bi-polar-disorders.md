<h1 class="pccm-page-title">Depression, Bi polar disorders</h1>

<div class="pccm-guideline-panel">
<h2 class="pccm-section-heading">Background</h2>
<ul class="pccm-content-list">
<li>For ongoing management of depression, see the Chronic conditions manual: <a href="https://www.health.qld.gov.au/rrcsu/clinical-manuals/chronic-conditions-manual-ccm" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">www.health.qld.gov.au/rrcsu/clinical-manuals/chronic-conditions-manual-ccm</a></li>
</ul>
</div>

<h2 class="pccm-section-heading-blue" id="may-present-with">1. May present with</h2>
<ul class="pccm-content-list">
<li>Existing history of depression, mood disorders or mania</li>
</ul>

<h3 class="pccm-section-heading-orange" id="depression">Depression<sup>1,2</sup></h3>
<ul class="pccm-content-list">
<li>Suicidal ideation/attempts. See <a href="/docs/Mental%20health/Suicidal-behaviour">Suicidal behaviour</a></li>
<li>Insomnia or sleep pattern changes</li>
<li>Appetite changes</li>
<li>Irritability, low mood, tiredness</li>
<li>Difficulty concentrating</li>
<li>Concerns about social problems such as finances or relationships</li>
<li>Feelings of helplessness or hopelessness</li>
<li>Use of alcohol or other substances</li>
<li>Women in the perinatal period</li>
</ul>

<h3 class="pccm-section-heading-orange" id="mania-bipolar">Mania (bipolar)<sup>1,2</sup></h3>
<ul class="pccm-content-list">
<li>Irritable mood or anger</li>
<li>Elevated mood</li>
<li>Inflated self-esteem</li>
<li>Decreased or unable to sleep, active all night</li>
<li>Pressured speech and racing thoughts</li>
<li>Excessive goals, plans and activities</li>
<li>Poor judgement, impulsive and taking risks e.g. excessive spending, promiscuous behaviour</li>
<li>Symptoms of psychosis. See <a href="/docs/Mental%20health/Psychosis">Psychosis</a>,</li>
</ul>

<h2 class="pccm-section-heading-blue" id="immediate-management">2. Immediate management</h2>
<ul class="pccm-content-list">
<li><a href="/docs/Emergency/Basic-life-support">DRSABCD</a></li>
<li>Do not leave patient alone</li>
<li>If person has survived an attempted suicide or a self-harm event, manage accordingly</li>
<li>Consider injuries consistent with self-harm attempt e.g. for hanging, see <a href="/docs/Emergency/Traumatic-injuries">Traumatic injuries</a>, <a href="/docs/Emergency/Spinal-injuries">Spinal injuries</a></li>
</ul>
<p>If a person is a risk of harm to themselves, others or their behaviour is associated with perceptual or thought disturbance, delirium, dementia or impaired impulse control. See <a href="/docs/Mental%20health/Acute-severe-behavioral-disturbance">Mental health emergency</a></p>

<h2 class="pccm-section-heading-blue" id="clinical-assessment">3. Clinical assessment</h2>
<ul class="pccm-content-list">
<li>Listen. Allow patient to speak without interruption</li>
<li>Develop and maintain rapport with the patient</li>
<li>Be mindful of your own values and beliefs in relation to mood disorders</li>
<li>Convey a sense of warmth, non-judgmental acceptance, and an interest in understanding the patient and the cause of their pain or distress</li>
<li>Be respectful and empathic</li>
<li>Avoid minimising the seriousness of their presentation</li>
<li>Get history, including:
<ul>
<li>current mental health management plan and related presentations<sup>2</sup></li>
<li>establishing patient’s behaviour and personality prior to the current presentation, by family and friends if needed<sup>2</sup></li>
<li>treatment history and engagement with psychotherapy<sup>3</sup></li>
<li>adherence to medication at the dosages prescribed<sup>3</sup></li>
<li>recent loss or stressors</li>
<li>chronic illnesses</li>
<li>physical disorders</li>
<li>sleep history, patterns, insomnia</li>
<li>if a woman in the perinatal period, assess for:
<ul>
<li>capacity to safely care for her infant (if delivered)</li>
<li>quality of mother-infant relationship e.g. cuddling, eye contact, responding to cues</li>
<li>mood using a mood scale e.g. EPDS, DASS <a href="https://www.psychologytools.com/download-scales-and-measures/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">www.psychologytools.com/download-scales-and-measures/</a></li>
</ul>
</li>
</ul>
</li>
<li>Do physical examination +
<ul>
<li>vital signs</li>
<li>BGL + SpO<sub>2</sub> to exclude glycaemic or hypoxic causes of behaviour change</li>
<li>bloods – TFT, CHEM20, lithium and sodium valproate levels</li>
<li>urine for MCS and drug screen</li>
<li>pregnancy test if female of reproductive age</li>
</ul>
</li>
<li>Do Mental State Examination +</li>
<li>Risk assessment:<sup>3</sup>
<ul>
<li>suicidal intent. See <a href="/docs/Mental%20health/Suicidal-behaviour">Suicidal behaviour</a></li>
<li>neglect or victimisation by others</li>
</ul>
</li>
</ul>

<h2 class="pccm-section-heading-blue" id="management">4. Management</h2>
<ul class="pccm-content-list">
<li>Consult MO and Mental Health Team or psychiatrist who will:
<ul>
<li>plan immediate management and coordination</li>
<li>consider evacuation for comprehensive management</li>
</ul>
</li>
<li>For mania related behaviours consider sedation. See <a href="/docs/Mental%20health/Acute-severe-behavioral-disturbance">Mental health emergency</a></li>
<li>For women in the perinatal period consult the MO or midwife for further management options</li>
</ul>

<h2 class="pccm-section-heading-blue" id="follow-up">5. Follow up</h2>
<ul class="pccm-content-list">
<li>According to MO and psychiatrist or midwife</li>
</ul>

<h2 class="pccm-section-heading-blue" id="referral-consultation">6. Referral/consultation</h2>
<ul class="pccm-content-list">
<li>MO and psychiatrist</li>
<li>Mental Health, Alcohol and Other Drugs (MHAODS) if problem alcohol or drug use</li>
<li>Social worker if the patient requires counselling or support navigating services</li>
<li>Refer women in the perinatal period to a midwife</li>
<li>Consider child health services or Child and Youth Mental Health Services if concerned about welfare of children in care of patient. See <a href="/docs/Paediatrics/Child-protection">Child protection</a></li>
</ul>
</div>
