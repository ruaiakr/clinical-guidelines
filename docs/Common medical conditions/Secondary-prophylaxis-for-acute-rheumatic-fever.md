<h1 class="pccm-page-title">Secondary prophylaxis for acute rheumatic fever (ARF) - Adult/Child</h1>

<div class="pccm-guideline-panel">
<h2 class="pccm-section-heading">Recommend<sup>1</sup></h2>
<ul class="pccm-content-list">
<li>Strict regular long-term benzathine benzylpenicillin (Bicillin LA®) prophylaxis (<strong>every 21–28 days</strong>) is critical to prevent recurrent Group A Strep infections causing ARF</li>
<li><strong>Every day of non-treatment over 28 days puts the person at high risk of recurrence of ARF</strong></li>
</ul>
<h2 class="pccm-section-heading">Background<sup>1</sup></h2>
<ul class="pccm-content-list">
<li>Recurrent ARF can lead to a chronic condition called rheumatic heart disease (RHD) which involves deformity and dysfunction of the heart valves</li>
<li>The RHD Register and Control Program TCH + is available for clinical support and education ☎ 74028100 Ext 302</li>
<li>Recommended resources. See <a href="https://www.rhdaustralia.org.au/resources">https://www.rhdaustralia.org.au/resources</a>
<ul>
<li>The 2020 Australian guideline for prevention, diagnosis and management of acute rheumatic fever and rheumatic heart disease (3rd edition)</li>
</ul>
</li>
</ul>
</div>

<h2 class="pccm-section-heading-blue">1. May present with</h2>
<ul class="pccm-content-list">
<li>Common age group 5–25 years old</li>
<li>History of recurrent sore throat</li>
<li>Recurrent joint pains, may or may not be migratory</li>
<li>Fever especially with acute attacks of joint pains</li>
<li>Involuntary movements (Sydenham chorea)</li>
<li>Non-blanching rash which is painless and not itchy. Commonly located on the trunk and extremities</li>
<li>Easily fatigued</li>
<li>SOB if advanced to RHD</li>
<li>+/− Murmur on auscultation</li>
</ul>

<h2 class="pccm-section-heading-blue">2. Immediate management</h2>
<ul class="pccm-content-list">
<li>Pain relief: ibuprofen 400 mg tds no longer than a week</li>
<li>Paracetamol for fever</li>
<li>Bedrest</li>
</ul>

<h2 class="pccm-section-heading-blue">3. Clinical assessment</h2>
<ul class="pccm-content-list">
<li>Check and complete care items on the patient's ARF/RHD injection booklet
<ul>
<li>Fill in registration form if newly diagnosed</li>
<li>Check injection compliance</li>
<li>Last echo at least every two years, annually for RHD</li>
</ul>
</li>
<li>Ask:
<ul>
<li>if any problems after previous injection(s)</li>
<li>where patient wants injection:<sup>1</sup>
<ul>
<li>ventrogluteal preferred site; lateral thigh is acceptable</li>
<li>note: upper outer quadrant of the buttock is associated with sciatic nerve damage and must be used with caution</li>
</ul>
</li>
</ul>
</li>
</ul>

<h2 class="pccm-section-heading-blue">4. Management<sup>1</sup></h2>
<ul class="pccm-content-list">
<li>The duration of secondary prophylaxis is a specialist decision based on a number of individual and environmental factors. Prophylaxis can only be ceased by a specialist MO</li>
<li>Injections can be given anytime between 21–28 days:
<ul>
<li>a specialist may recommend a 21 day regimen (rather than 21–28 days) for patients who have breakthrough ARF despite complete adherence to a 28 day regimen <strong>OR</strong> have Moderate RHD</li>
</ul>
</li>
<li>Give benzathine benzylpenicillin (Bicillin LA®):
<ul>
<li>consider strategies for Managing injection pain</li>
<li>if allergic to penicillin, specialist will order oral erythromycin</li>
<li>Mix: 1 mL lidocaine + 3 mL water for injection</li>
</ul>
</li>
<li>If patient has bleeding problems after injections or consistently declines Bicillin LA® despite attempts to identify and address any barriers to injections:
<ul>
<li>specialist may order oral phenoxymethylpenicillin</li>
</ul>
</li>
<li>If oral antibiotics given:
<ul>
<li>only after discussion with MO</li>
<li>emphasise consequence of missed doses + encourage to consider returning to injections</li>
<li>↑ monitoring for Group A Strep infections eg Impetigo, Sore throat and recurrence of ARF</li>
</ul>
</li>
</ul>

<div class="pccm-drug-box">
<div class="pccm-drug-header">
<span class="pccm-drug-name">BENZATHINE BENZYLPENICILLIN (BICILLIN L-A®)</span>
<span class="pccm-drug-level">L2</span>
</div>
<div class="pccm-assessment-table-wrap" style="margin:0; overflow-x:auto;">
<table class="pccm-drug-table">
<tr><th>FORM</th><th>STRENGTH</th><th>ROUTE</th><th>DOSE</th><th>DURATION</th></tr>
<tr>
<td>Prefilled syringe</td>
<td>1.2 million units/2.3 mL</td>
<td>IM</td>
<td><strong>Child &lt; 20 kg</strong><br />600,000 units (1.2 mL)<br /><strong>Adult and child ≥ 20 kg</strong><br />1.2 million units (2.3 mL)</td>
<td>Once every 21–28 days<br />Inject slowly over at least 2–3 minutes</td>
</tr>
</table>
</div>
<div class="pccm-drug-notes">
<p><strong>Offer CMI:</strong> May cause diarrhoea, nausea and pain at injection site</p>
<p><strong>Note:</strong> Ventrogluteal or vastus lateralis sites preferred. Do not give in deltoid</p>
<p><strong>Contraindication:</strong> Severe or immediate allergic reaction to a penicillin. Be aware of cross-reactivity between penicillins, cephalosporins and carbapenems</p>
<p><strong>Management of associated emergency:</strong> Ensure access to adrenaline (epinephrine) 1:1,000 to treat anaphylaxis eg if giving in patients home. Consult MO. See <a href="/docs/Emergency/Anaphylaxis">Anaphylaxis</a><sup>1,2</sup></p>
</div>
</div>

<p><strong>Note:</strong> If renal impairment seek MO advice. Interacts with many medicines, including over-the-counter and herbal products. Use with caution in patients with myasthenia gravis</p>
<p><strong>Contraindication:</strong> Use with some statins. Severe or immediate allergic reaction to macrolides. Severe hepatic impairment</p>
<p><strong>Management of associated emergency:</strong> Consult MO. See <a href="/docs/Emergency/Anaphylaxis">Anaphylaxis</a><sup>1,4</sup></p>

<h2 class="pccm-section-heading-blue">5. Follow up<sup>1</sup></h2>
<ul class="pccm-content-list">
<li>Use strategies to support patient to return for next injection eg:
<ul>
<li>reschedule next appointment before they leave the clinic</li>
<li>update recall for 21 days time</li>
<li>fill out injection reminder card</li>
</ul>
</li>
</ul>

<h2 class="pccm-section-heading-blue">6. Referral/consultation</h2>
<ul class="pccm-content-list">
<li>Discussion as per discussion with MO/MA</li>
</ul>
