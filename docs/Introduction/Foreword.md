# <span style="color: #EF6A4C;">Foreword</span>
On behalf of the Ministry of Health and Medical Services, it is my great 
pleasure to introduce the Kiribati Primary Clinical Care Manual. This manual 
is a milestone in our commitment to improving the quality and accessibility 
of healthcare across Kiribati, ensuring that all I-Kiribati have access to safe, 
effective, and culturally appropriate care.

The development of this manual has been a collaborative effort, drawing 
on local expertise and the valuable knowledge and resources shared 
through partnerships with international organizations. It will serve as an 
essential tool for healthcare workers across our nation, empowering them 
to deliver care that meets the highest standards of clinical practice.

The Kiribati Primary Clinical Care Manual provides comprehensive 
guidance on managing medical conditions, responding to emergencies, 
promoting health and well-being, and enhancing the patient experience.  
It also emphasizes the importance of community-based care, with a focus 
on health promotion and prevention.

As we continue to address the health challenges faced by our 
communities, this manual will play a key role in strengthening our 
healthcare system, improving health outcomes, and enhancing the lives 
of all those we serve. The Ministry remains committed to supporting the 
health workforce and ensuring that our services meet the diverse needs of 
the I-Kiribati population.

I extend my gratitude to all those who have contributed to the creation 
of this valuable resource and to all healthcare workers who will use it to 
provide exceptional care. Together, we are building a healthier future for 
Kiribati. <br /> <br /> <br />



**Mr. Michael Bootii Nauan** <br />
Minister for Health <br />
Republic of Kiribati
