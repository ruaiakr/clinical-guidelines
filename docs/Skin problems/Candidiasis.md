<h1 class="pccm-page-title">Candidiasis (skin) – Adult/Child</h1>

<div class="pccm-guideline-panel">
<h2 class="pccm-section-heading">Recommend</h2>
<ul class="pccm-content-list">
<li>Treat localised skin candidiasis with topical antifungal cream. Refer if infection is widespread, the patient is immunocompromised, or not improving with treatment.</li>
</ul>
<h2 class="pccm-section-heading">Background</h2>
<ul class="pccm-content-list">
<li>Candidiasis of the skin is a fungal infection caused by Candida species. It most often affects warm, moist areas such as the axillae, groin, under the breasts, abdominal folds, and the nappy area. Risk factors include antibiotic or corticosteroid use, diabetes, obesity, immobility, and immunosuppression.</li>
</ul>
</div>

<h2 class="pccm-section-heading-blue" id="may-present-with">1. May present with</h2>
<ul class="pccm-content-list">
<li>Red, itchy rash or patches</li>
<li>May include small blisters or pustules</li>
<li>Most common in warm, moist areas: axillae, groin, under breasts, abdominal folds, nappy area</li>
</ul>

<h2 class="pccm-section-heading-blue" id="immediate-management">2. Immediate management</h2>
<ul class="pccm-content-list">
<li>Not applicable</li>
</ul>

<h2 class="pccm-section-heading-blue" id="clinical-assessment">3. Clinical assessment</h2>
<ul class="pccm-content-list">
<li>Take a history, including:
<ul>
<li>Onset and duration</li>
<li>Previous infections or treatments</li>
<li>Other symptoms</li>
<li>Predisposing factors, such as:
<ul>
<li>Diabetes</li>
<li>Immunocompromised state</li>
<li>Antibiotic or corticosteroid use</li>
<li>Obesity or immobility</li>
</ul>
</li>
</ul>
</li>
<li>Do vital signs
<ul>
<li>In adults, check blood glucose level (BGL)</li>
</ul>
</li>
<li>Examine the skin – see Skin assessment – adult, or Skin assessment – child</li>
</ul>

<h2 class="pccm-section-heading-blue" id="management">4. Management</h2>
<ul class="pccm-content-list">
<li>Refer to MO clinic if:
<ul>
<li>Infection is widespread</li>
<li>Patient is immunocompromised</li>
<li>MO may prescribe oral fluconazole and consider fungal MCS</li>
</ul>
</li>
<li>For localised infection, treat with topical antifungal cream:</li>
</ul>

<div class="pccm-drug-box" id="miconazole">
<div class="pccm-drug-header">
<span class="pccm-drug-name">MICONAZOLE</span>
<span class="pccm-drug-level">L2</span>
</div>
<div class="pccm-assessment-table-wrap" style="margin:0; overflow-x:auto;">
<table class="pccm-drug-table">
<tr><th>FORM</th><th>STRENGTH</th><th>ROUTE</th><th>DOSE</th><th>DURATION</th></tr>
<tr>
<td>Cream</td>
<td>2%</td>
<td>Topical</td>
<td>Apply a thin layer twice daily (bd)</td>
<td>Continue for 2 weeks after symptoms resolve</td>
</tr>
</table>
</div>
<div class="pccm-drug-notes">
<p><strong>Safety advice:</strong> Apply to the affected and surrounding skin. Pay particular attention to skin folds. Use regularly for effective treatment.</p>
</div>
</div>

<ul class="pccm-content-list">
<li>Additional advice:
<ul>
<li>Keep skin clean and dry</li>
<li>Dry skin folds well after washing</li>
<li>Improve diabetes or weight control if relevant</li>
</ul>
</li>
</ul>

<h2 class="pccm-section-heading-orange" id="management-of-associated-emergency">Management of associated emergency</h2>
<ul class="pccm-content-list">
<li>Consult MO</li>
</ul>

<h2 class="pccm-section-heading-blue" id="follow-up">5. Follow up</h2>
<ul class="pccm-content-list">
<li>Review at next MO clinic if no improvement after 2 weeks</li>
</ul>

<h2 class="pccm-section-heading-blue" id="referral-consultation">6. Referral / consultation</h2>
<ul class="pccm-content-list">
<li>Discuss the case by phone or messenger</li>
<li>Contact Skin Clinical Nurses first – they will liaise with the Doctor</li>
<li>Alternatively, contact the Doctor directly using phone or Facebook Messenger group chat</li>
</ul>
</div>
