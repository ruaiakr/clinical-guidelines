<h1 class="pccm-page-title">Patient retrieval</h1>
<div class="pccm-section-heading-blue">1. Who to contact<sup>1,2</sup></div>
<div class="pccm-guideline-panel">
<ul>
<li>Usually the MO, or specialist if possible, will arrange retrieval if required</li>
<li>If you think a patient may need retrieval, contact the relevant retrieval service EARLY</li>
<li>Notify change of clinical condition of patient if worsening or improving:
<ul>
<li>flight priority can always be reassessed</li>
</ul>
</li>
</ul>
</div>
<div class="pccm-section-heading-blue">2. Retrieval preparation<sup>1,2</sup></div>
<div class="pccm-assessment-table-wrap">
<table class="pccm-assessment-table">
<tr class="pccm-table-banner"><td colspan="2">RETRIEVAL PREPARATION</td></tr>
<tr><td class="pccm-row-label">Documentation</td><td class="pccm-row-content"><ul>
<li>Complete retrieval</li>
<li>Ensure documentation goes with the patient including:
<ul>
<li>pre-hospital documentation</li>
<li>discharge summary - medical/surgical letter from referring facility</li>
<li>observations forms, including all recent observations + current vital signs, neurological status, BGL, neurovascular</li>
<li>current medication chart, with prn analgesia + cardiac medications (as required)</li>
<li>IV fluid orders and fluid balance chart</li>
<li>Test result if available</li>
</ul>
</li>
</ul></td></tr>
<tr><td class="pccm-row-label">Handover location</td><td class="pccm-row-content"><ul>
<li>Handover location will be determined during the retrieval coordination process</li>
<li>If patient stabilised and prepared, handover at airport/airstrip may occur</li>
<li>Critical and unstable patients will be reviewed at the referring facility by the retrieval team prior to transport</li>
</ul></td></tr>
<tr><td class="pccm-row-label">Patient escort and baggage Space and weight restrictions apply</td><td class="pccm-row-content"><ul>
<li>An escort may be needed at the discretion of the pilot:
<ul>
<li>name + weight of escort required</li>
</ul>
</li>
<li>Medical aids/additional baggage at the pilot's discretion</li>
<li>Confirm no cigarette lighters, vapes, spare batteries and aerosol cans in luggage</li>
<li>Inform family/carers of plans/expectations</li>
</ul></td></tr>
</table>
</div>
<div class="pccm-assessment-table-wrap">
<table class="pccm-assessment-table pccm-prep-table">
<tr class="pccm-prep-header"><td colspan="3">GENERAL PREPARATION</td></tr>
<tr class="pccm-prep-header"><td>CONSIDERATION</td><td>REQUIREMENTS</td><td>RATIONALE</td></tr>
<tr><td class="pccm-prep-label">Allergies/ identification</td><td class="pccm-prep-req"><ul>
<li>Apply ID bands if available</li>
</ul></td><td class="pccm-prep-rationale"><ul>
<li>Rapid correct identification</li>
</ul></td></tr><tr><td class="pccm-prep-label">Weight, height + widest point</td><td class="pccm-prep-req"><ul>
<li>If &gt; 120 kg, measure width at widest point eg shoulder or hips</li>
</ul></td><td class="pccm-prep-rationale"><ul>
<li>Assists transport planning</li>
</ul></td></tr><tr><td class="pccm-prep-label">Analgesia</td><td class="pccm-prep-req"><ul>
<li>Give analgesia as clinically indicated prior to transfer</li>
<li>See Acute pain</li>
</ul></td><td class="pccm-prep-rationale"><ul>
<li>Movement of the patient may exacerbate pain</li>
</ul></td></tr><tr><td class="pccm-prep-label">Antiemetic</td><td class="pccm-prep-req"><ul>
<li>Antiemetic essential if:</li>
<li>head, spinal, or penetrating eye injury</li>
<li>Consider for history of motion sickness or general nausea</li>
<li>Give 30 minutes prior to transfer</li>
<li>See Nausea and vomiting</li>
</ul></td><td class="pccm-prep-rationale"><ul>
<li>Vomiting may exacerbate certain clinical conditions by raising ICP and intraocular pressure (IOP)</li>
<li>Puts airway at risk</li>
<li>Motion sickness common in aeromedical environment</li>
</ul></td></tr><tr><td class="pccm-prep-label">IVC</td><td class="pccm-prep-req"><ul>
<li>Ensure most patients have at least 1 IVC</li>
<li>IVC x 2 in critically ill/disturbed patients</li>
</ul></td><td class="pccm-prep-rationale"><ul>
<li>IV access may be difficult during flight due to space restrictions and turbulence</li>
</ul></td></tr><tr><td class="pccm-prep-label">IDC</td><td class="pccm-prep-req"><ul>
<li>Get patients to empty their bladder prior to transfer</li>
<li>Insert IDC if is/may be incontinent</li>
</ul></td><td class="pccm-prep-rationale"><ul>
<li>No toilet facilities on aircraft</li>
<li>Use of bedpans is avoided due to limitations of space and waste disposal</li>
</ul></td></tr>
</table>
</div>
<div class="pccm-assessment-table-wrap">
<table class="pccm-assessment-table pccm-prep-table">
<tr class="pccm-prep-header"><td colspan="3">GENERAL PREPARATION</td></tr>
<tr class="pccm-prep-header"><td>CONSIDERATION</td><td>REQUIREMENTS</td><td>RATIONALE</td></tr>
<tr><td class="pccm-prep-label">Infusions</td><td class="pccm-prep-req"><ul>
<li>Prior to transfer prepare infusions using compatible equipment, if possible, when using retrieval services</li>
</ul></td><td class="pccm-prep-rationale"><ul>
<li>Time is saved if infusions are prepared prior to of retrieval team arrival</li>
</ul></td></tr><tr><td class="pccm-prep-label">NGT/OGT</td><td class="pccm-prep-req"><ul>
<li>Ensure NGT/OGT inserted in:
<ul>
<li>all ventilated patients</li>
<li>patients with bowel obstruction</li>
</ul>
</li>
</ul></td><td class="pccm-prep-rationale"><ul>
<li>Allow for drainage of stomach contents and reduce risk of vomiting and aspiration</li>
</ul></td></tr>
</table>
</div>
<div class="pccm-assessment-table-wrap">
<table class="pccm-assessment-table pccm-prep-table">
<tr class="pccm-prep-header"><td colspan="3">SPECIFIC MEDICAL CONDITIONS</td></tr>
<tr class="pccm-prep-header"><td>CONSIDERATION</td><td>REQUIREMENTS</td><td>RATIONALE</td></tr>
<tr><td class="pccm-prep-label">Mental illness/ disturbed behaviour</td><td class="pccm-prep-req"><ul>
<li>Reliable IV access. If possible IVC x 2</li>
<li>A risk assessment will be completed by the retrieval service</li>
<li>Sedation and physical restraint may be required. Seek medical advice</li>
</ul></td><td class="pccm-prep-rationale"><ul>
<li>For aviation safety, special requirements apply to transportation of patients showing signs of disturbed behaviour, or regarded as being a danger to themselves or others</li>
</ul></td></tr><tr><td class="pccm-prep-label">Infectious conditions</td><td class="pccm-prep-req"><ul>
<li>Always notify retrieval coordinator of:
<ul>
<li>infectious conditions ± precautions</li>
<li>if immunocompromised</li>
</ul>
</li>
</ul></td><td class="pccm-prep-rationale"><ul>
<li>Facilitate decision-making regarding the aeromedical transport of infectious patients in order to minimise risk to other patients and staff</li>
<li>Limited ability to isolate patients in aircraft</li>
</ul></td></tr><tr><td class="pccm-prep-label">Spinal injury</td><td class="pccm-prep-req"><ul>
<li>Transport on vacuum mattress</li>
<li>Insert IDC</li>
<li>Insert NGT if have altered LOC</li>
</ul></td><td class="pccm-prep-rationale"><ul>
<li>To maintain stabilisation</li>
</ul></td></tr><tr><td class="pccm-prep-label">Bowel obstruction</td><td class="pccm-prep-req"><ul>
<li>Insert NGT - leave on free drainage or attach anti-reflux valve (do not spigot)</li>
<li>Give parenteral antiemetic and adequate analgesia prior to transfer</li>
</ul></td><td class="pccm-prep-rationale"><ul>
<li>Trapped gas will expand in volume at altitude and cause pain. NGT will allow gas to escape and reduce vomiting</li>
</ul></td></tr>
</table>
</div>
<div class="pccm-assessment-table-wrap">
<table class="pccm-assessment-table pccm-prep-table">
<tr class="pccm-prep-header"><td colspan="3">SPECIFIC MEDICAL CONDITIONS</td></tr>
<tr class="pccm-prep-header"><td>CONSIDERATION</td><td>REQUIREMENTS</td><td>RATIONALE</td></tr>
<tr><td class="pccm-prep-label">Pneumothorax</td><td class="pccm-prep-req"><ul>
<li>Chest drain insertion (by MO) will done prior airlift</li>
<li>Ensure chest drain is in place</li>
<li>Connect to ambulatory chest drain system</li>
<li>Suspected pneumothorax should be excluded clinical</li>
</ul></td><td class="pccm-prep-rationale"><ul>
<li>Trapped gas in the pleural cavity will expand at altitude and may result in respiratory compromise</li>
<li>Underwater seal drains are avoided due to the risk of retrograde flow during transfer</li>
</ul></td></tr><tr><td class="pccm-prep-label">Penetrating eye injury</td><td class="pccm-prep-req"><ul>
<li>Give antiemetic to all patients with proven or suspected eye injury</li>
<li>Patients may be transported at reduced cabin altitude</li>
</ul></td><td class="pccm-prep-rationale"><ul>
<li>Trapped gas in the globe will expand at altitude and potentially worsen the injury</li>
<li>Vomiting may also exacerbate injury by raising intraocular pressure</li>
</ul></td></tr>
</table>
