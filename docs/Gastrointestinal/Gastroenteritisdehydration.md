<h1 class="pccm-page-title">Gastroenteritis/dehydration - Adult</h1>
<p class="pccm-page-subtitle">Diarrhoea ± vomiting</p>

<div class="pccm-guideline-panel">
<h2 class="pccm-section-heading">Background<sup>1</sup></h2>
<ul class="pccm-content-list">
<li>Most acute diarrhoea is viral and resolves within 24–48 hours without specific treatment</li>
<li>Antibiotics are of no benefit in most cases and may exacerbate diarrhoea</li>
</ul>
</div>

<h2 class="pccm-section-heading-blue" id="may-present-with">1. May present with<sup>1</sup></h2>
<ul class="pccm-content-list">
<li>Sudden onset of diarrhoea ± vomiting, fever, abdominal pain, nausea, chills</li>
<li>Lethargy, dehydration</li>
</ul>

<h2 class="pccm-section-heading-blue" id="immediate-management">2. Immediate management<sup>1</sup></h2>
<ul class="pccm-content-list">
<li>Do vital signs + Hydration assessment</li>
<li>If severe dehydration:
<ul>
<li>contact MO urgently</li>
<li>continue to manage as per <a href="/docs/Emergency/Shock">Shock</a></li>
</ul>
</li>
<li>Screen for <a href="/docs/Emergency/Sepsisseptic-shock">Sepsis</a></li>
</ul>

<h2 class="pccm-section-heading-blue" id="clinical-assessment">3. Clinical assessment</h2>
<ul class="pccm-content-list">
<li>Get history, including:<sup>1</sup>
<ul>
<li>gastrointestinal symptoms:
<ul>
<li>date/time of onset, frequency + amounts, is there blood ± mucous in stools, bile stained or green vomit, location and severity of abdominal pain</li>
<li>note: if &gt; 24 hours of vomiting without diarrhoea, consider other causes. See <a href="/docs/Pain,%20nausea%20and%20vomiting/Nausea-and-vomiting">Nausea and vomiting</a></li>
</ul>
</li>
<li>fever, rash, headache, light headedness/dizziness, ↓mobility</li>
<li>known illness in contacts</li>
<li>recent overseas travel, antibiotic use or hospital admission</li>
<li>immunocompromised</li>
<li>medications eg aperients</li>
</ul>
</li>
<li>Do physical examination, including:
<ul>
<li>BGL + ketones - if ↑ consider <a href="/docs/Emergency/Diabetic-ketoacidosis">DKA</a></li>
<li>i-STAT - UE, lactate</li>
<li>urinalysis - ketones</li>
<li>Abdominal examination</li>
</ul>
</li>
<li>Do stool MCS + multiplex PCR + C.difficile if:<sup>1</sup>
<ul>
<li>diarrhoea &gt; 7 days</li>
<li>severe symptoms eg blood in stools, high fever, ↑HR, abdominal tenderness or severe pain</li>
<li>immunocompromised</li>
<li>recent overseas travel (C.difficile not required)</li>
</ul>
</li>
</ul>

<h2 class="pccm-section-heading-blue" id="management">4. Management<sup>1</sup></h2>
<ul class="pccm-content-list">
<li>Rehydration is the most important aspect of management</li>
<li>Consult MO if:
<ul>
<li>vomiting and unable to tolerate oral fluids - IV fluids may be required<sup>2</sup></li>
<li>severe symptoms</li>
<li>pregnant, immunocompromised or elderly</li>
</ul>
</li>
<li>If minimal or no dehydration:
<ul>
<li>advise to continue eating + drinking as tolerated</li>
</ul>
</li>
<li>If mild to moderate dehydration:
<ul>
<li>give ORS, even if intermittent vomiting</li>
<li>note: if ORS is not available, use 1/2 level teaspoon of salt + 6 level teaspoons of sugar in 1 L of clean drinking water</li>
<li>aim for 2–3 L over 24 hours. Frequent small volumes may be better tolerated eg 50 mL every 15–30 minutes</li>
<li>avoid fatty or sugary foods and excessive intake of soft drinks, sports/energy drinks, cordials and fruit juice. If used they should be diluted</li>
<li>once tolerating/responding to ORS, advise patient to return to normal diet<sup>1</sup></li>
</ul>
</li>
<li>Offer antiemetic if needed. See <a href="/docs/Pain,%20nausea%20and%20vomiting/Nausea-and-vomiting">Nausea and vomiting</a></li>
<li>Control the spread advice:
<ul>
<li>if prepares/serves food - avoid handling food until no diarrhoea or vomiting for 48 hours<sup>2</sup></li>
<li>if healthcare worker - do not return to work until no symptoms for 48 hours<sup>3</sup></li>
<li>otherwise, if single case - avoid others until no symptoms, feeling well + no loose bowel motions for at least 24 hours<sup>2</sup></li>
</ul>
</li>
</ul>

<h2 class="pccm-section-heading-blue" id="follow-up">5. Follow up<sup>1</sup></h2>
<ul class="pccm-content-list">
<li>Advise patient to return + contact MO if:
<ul>
<li>symptoms persist &gt; 48 hours, or worsen</li>
<li>develops severe vomiting, abdominal cramps, or blood in stools</li>
</ul>
</li>
<li>Review stool pathology results</li>
</ul>

<h2 class="pccm-section-heading-blue" id="referral-consultation">6. Referral/consultation<sup>2</sup></h2>
<ul class="pccm-content-list">
<li>Notify Public Health Unit if ≥ 2 cases of diarrhoea ± vomiting in the same location or single case in a food handler</li>
</ul>
