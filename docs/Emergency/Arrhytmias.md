<h1 class="pccm-page-title">Arrhytmias – Adult</h1>
<div class="pccm-guideline-panel">
<h2 class="pccm-section-heading">Recommend</h2>
<ul class="pccm-content-list">
<li>Good quality CPR and reducing time to defibrillation are the highest priorities in resuscitation from sudden cardiac arrest</li>
<li>Airway takes precedent over any injury, including possibility of spinal injury - handle gently with no twisting or bending of spinal column or neck<sup>1</sup></li>
<li>If pregnant, once CPR in progress put padding such as a towel under the woman’s right hip to tilt the hips about 15–30⁰, but leave her shoulders flat</li>
<li>Child/infant - central pulse palpation may be used to asess circulation. If a pulse cannot be definitely identified within 10 seconds (or HR &lt; 60 with signs of poor circulation) start CPR</li>
</ul>
<h2 class="pccm-section-heading">Background</h2>
<ul class="pccm-content-list">
<li>Agonal breaths (occasional irregular gasps) are common in early stages of cardiac arrest</li>
</ul>
</div>

<h2 class="pccm-section-heading-blue">1. May present with<sup>2</sup></h2>
<ul class="pccm-content-list">
<li>Fast, slow or irregular pulse</li>
<li>± symptoms eg:<ul><li>palpitations, sensation of ‘missed beats’</li><li>SOB, fatigue, dizziness, light-headedness, syncope/fainting, non-accidental falls</li><li>chest pain/discomfort, hypotension/shock</li></ul></li>
</ul>
<h2 class="pccm-section-heading-blue">2.	 Immediate management<sup>2</sup></h2>
<ul class="pccm-content-list">
<li>DRSABCD</li>
<li>If needed see BLS, and ALS.</li>
<li>If chest pain go to Chest pain assessment, and ACS</li>
<li>Vital signs</li>
<li>ECG<sup>3</sup> - to be reviewed by MO promptly - within 10 minutes if symptomatic<ul><li>if difficult to interpret, MO may send to cardiologist using ECG Flash if available. See https://qheps.health.qld.gov.au/caru/networks/cardiac/ecg-flash</li></ul></li>
<li>Attach cardiac monitor</li>
<li>Give O<sup>2</sup> if needed to maintain SpO<sup>2</sup> ≥ 94%</li>
<li>Insert IVC x 2</li>
<li>Take bloods:</li>
<li>FBC, UE, troponin ± VBG<sup>3</sup> If unstable/deteriorating urgently consult MO<sup>2</sup> for urgent evacuation +<ul><li>Look for reversible causes eg ACS, AF, hypothermia, hypovolaemic shock, electrolyte</li><li>abnormalities, hypoxaemia, infection, acidosis, hypothyroidism</li><li>If tachyarrhythmia - MO will advise management</li></ul></li>
<li>If bradyarrhythmia - MO will advise. May include:3<ul><li>check if taking beta blockers, calcium channel blocker (diltiazem or verapamil) or digoxin (drug toxicity)<sup>3</sup></li><li>medication (depending on cause) eg:3<ul><li>atropine (for vasovagal bradycardia), or</li><li>other medication(s) if drug toxicity</li><li>external cardiac pacing if deteriorates/haemodynamically unstable - as per local policy</li></ul></li></ul></li>
</ul>
<h2 class="pccm-section-heading-blue">3.	 Clinical assessment 2,3</h2>
<ul class="pccm-content-list">
<li>Get rapid history, including:3<ul><li>onset, associated symptoms</li><li>frequency, timing, duration, severity, longevity, circumstances, triggers, alleviating factors</li><li>relationship of symptoms to - medications, meals, emotional distress, exertion, positional changes, triggers (eg urination, defecation, cough, prolonged standing, shaving, tight collar, head turning)</li><li>previous episode(s)</li><li>medications</li><li>recent drug/alcohol use</li><li>cardiovascular disease, renal disease</li><li>risk factors for cardiovascular disease eg hypertension, diabetes, hyperlipidaemia, smoking,</li><li>family history</li></ul></li>
<li>Listen to the chest for air entry + added sounds - crackles or wheeze</li>
<li>Any oedema - check legs/ankles + if able sacrum (with patient sitting up and forward)</li>
</ul>
<h2 class="pccm-section-heading-blue">4.	 Management</h2>
<ul class="pccm-content-list">
<li>Consult MO/MA for all arrhythmias, who may advise:<ul><li>further investigation and management of any underlying cause</li><li>referral to cardiologist</li><li>evacuation/hospitalisation if required</li></ul></li>
</ul>
<h2 class="pccm-section-heading-blue">5.	 Follow up</h2>
<ul class="pccm-content-list">
<li>If patient not evacuated/hospitalised advise to be reviewed at next MO clinic, or sooner if indicated</li>
</ul>
<h2 class="pccm-section-heading-blue">6.	 Referral/consultation</h2>
<ul class="pccm-content-list">
<li>Consult MO as above</li>
</ul>
