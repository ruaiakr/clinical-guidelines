<h1 class="pccm-page-title">Mastoiditis - Adult/Child</h1>

<div class="pccm-guideline-panel">
<h2 class="pccm-section-heading">Recommend</h2>
<ul class="pccm-content-list">
<li>Refer all suspected mastoiditis cases urgently.</li>
<li>Treat with IV antibiotics if available. If IV access is not possible, give oral antibiotics while arranging urgent referral.</li>
<li>Consult MO for further assessment and referral.</li>
</ul>
<h2 class="pccm-section-heading">Background</h2>
<ul class="pccm-content-list">
<li>Mastoiditis occurs when an ear infection spreads to the mastoid bone (behind the ear), causing swelling, redness, and a possible collection of pus under the skin behind the pinna (outer ear).</li>
<li>This condition can become serious and requires urgent treatment to prevent complications.</li>
</ul>
</div>

<h2 class="pccm-section-heading-blue">1. May present with</h2>
<ul class="pccm-content-list">
<li>Pain behind the ear</li>
<li>Fever</li>
<li>Possible discharge from the ear</li>
</ul>

<h2 class="pccm-section-heading-blue">2. Immediate management</h2>
<ul class="pccm-content-list">
<li>Refer urgently to a health centre for prompt care</li>
<li>Administer IV antibiotics if available</li>
<li>If IV access is not possible, give oral antibiotics</li>
<li>Consult MO urgently</li>
</ul>

<h2 class="pccm-section-heading-blue">3. Clinical assessment</h2>
<ul class="pccm-content-list">
<li>History of ear discharge</li>
<li>Swelling and redness behind the pinna</li>
<li>Pain on pressing the bone behind the pinna (post-auricular pain)</li>
<li>Fluctuant swelling (pus under the skin may feel like a fluid-filled sac)</li>
<li>Fever</li>
<li>Otoscopy may show pus in the ear canal</li>
</ul>

<h2 class="pccm-section-heading-blue">4. Management</h2>
<ul class="pccm-content-list">
<li>Admit and start IV antibiotics:
<ul>
<li>Cloxacillin 25–50 mg/kg IV every 6 hours</li>
<li>Metronidazole (Flagyl) 500 mg IV every 8 hours</li>
<li>Gentamicin 5 mg/kg IV once daily for 5 days</li>
</ul>
</li>
<li>Ear drops: ciprofloxacin or chloramphenicol 4–5 drops twice daily for 7 days</li>
<li>Give pain relief. See <a href="/docs/Pain,%20nausea%20and%20vomiting/Acute-pain">Acute pain</a></li>
<li>Educate on ear hygiene</li>
</ul>

<h2 class="pccm-section-heading-blue">5. Referral / consultation</h2>
<ul class="pccm-content-list">
<li>Refer for surgical review (mastoidectomy) where indicated</li>
</ul>
</div>
