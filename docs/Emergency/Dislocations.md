# Dislocations - Adult/Child

Dislocations - Adult/Child
GENERAL CONSIDERATION:
•	 Pulling or pushing of a bone out of its normal position in the joint1
1.
May present with
•	 Injury + severe pain that is worse on movement + very apprehensive to move
joint2
•	 ± deformity, associated fractures
•	 Reports of popping or joint rolling out of place - mainly shoulder dislocations1
2.	 Immediate management
•	 Offer analgesia. See Acute pain
•	 Support limb using pillows as needed + apply ice packs as tolerated
•	 If simple dislocation eg patella, digit + skilled - quickly reduce to alleviate pain
•	 Contact MO if signs of neurovascular compromise distal to the site eg:

–
weakness or numbness

–
 or absent pulses, delayed capillary refill

–
changes in colour

•	 Ask about mechanism of injury +

–

•	 Get past history:

–
previous dislocations, when, was it reduced, if so how/which manoeuvre1

–
any orthopaedic surgery or underlying conditions eg osteoporosis2
•	 Do physical examination, including: 1,2

–
vital signs

–
check range of motion - painful or limited + palpate joints above and below
injury – any tenderness, swelling
