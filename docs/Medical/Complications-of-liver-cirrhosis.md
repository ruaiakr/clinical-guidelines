# Complications of liver cirrhosis – Adult

4.	 Management3,4
•	 Consult MO urgently, who may advise:

–
IV fluids

–
IV antibiotics ± octreotide if oesophageal varices

–
IV pantoprazole ± thiamine if alcohol misuse

–
retrieval/hospitalisation for urgent endoscopy/surgery
•	 Monitor vital signs closely until retrieval, keep nil by mouth
5.	 Follow up
•	 As advised by MO
6.	 Referral/consultation
•	 As above
