# Safe use of Drugs, Including Antibiotic Use

Ventrogluteal injection
Technique:
•	 Place patient in a side-lying position.
•	 Use your right hand on the patient’s left hip, or left hand on the patient’s right
hip:

–
Place palm of your hand on the greater trochanter, thumb pointing towards
the pelvis/abdomen.

–
Place your index finger towards the front (anterior superior iliac spine).

–
Fan your middle finger as far along the iliac crest as you can reach.
•	 The injection site is in the middle of the triangle formed between the middle and
index fingers.
•	 See video: Ventrogluteal injection technique
