<h1 class="pccm-page-title">Acute coronary syndrome (ACS) - Adult</h1>
<p class="pccm-page-subtitle">myocardial infarction</p>
<div class="pccm-guideline-panel">
<h2 class="pccm-section-heading">Recommend<sup>1,2</sup></h2>
<ul class="pccm-content-list">
<li>Evacuation/treatment of ACS is time critical Use cardiac clinical pathways. Ensure they are readily available 2</li>
<li>https://clinicalexcellence.qld.gov.au/resources/clinical-pathways/cardiac- clinicalpathways Suspected acute coronary syndrome clinical pathway</li>
<li>Acute coronary syndrome clinical pathway</li>
<li>Thrombolysis for STEMI clinical pathway</li>
<li>Elevated troponin alone (without cardiac symptoms) should not trigger the urgent treatment of</li>
<li>ACS. Troponin may be elevated in many conditions, including sepsis<sup>3</sup></li>
<li>and PE.1 Urgently consult</li>
<li>MO in all cases</li>
</ul>
<h2 class="pccm-section-heading">Background<sup>1</sup></h2>
<ul class="pccm-content-list">
<li>ACS includes myocardial infarction (MI) or unstable angina:</li>
<li>MI can be ST elevation (STEMI) or non-ST elevation (NSTEMI)</li>
<li>patients without ST elevation are initially described as having NSTEACS (non-ST elevation</li>
<li>acute coronary syndrome) until investigated further</li>
<li>Reperfusion is restoring the flow of blood to the heart Acute coronary syndrome STEMI NSTEACS NSTEMI Unstable angina</li>
</ul>
</div>

<h2 class="pccm-section-heading-blue">1. May present with<sup>1,4</sup></h2>
<ul class="pccm-content-list">
<li>Chest discomfort or pain:<ul><li>heaviness/discomfort/pressure</li><li>aching, numbness</li><li>tightness/fullness</li><li>squeezing/burning</li><li>can radiate to shoulder, arm, jaw, back, or upper abdominal area</li><li>duration ≥ 10 minutes</li><li>typically triggered eg by exertion, emotional stress, temperature extremes OR can occur at rest</li></ul></li>
<li>Associated symptoms:<ul><li>SOB or difficulty breathing ± chest discomfort</li><li>dizziness, light-headedness, feeling faint or anxious</li><li>nausea, vomiting, indigestion</li><li>sweating/cold sweat</li><li>lethargy/fatigue</li></ul></li>
<li>Consider atypical presentation:<ul><li>associated symptoms WITHOUT chest pain</li><li>especially if diabetes, renal failure, female, elderly<sup>5</sup></li></ul></li>
</ul>
<h2 class="pccm-section-heading-blue">2.	 Immediate management<sup>1,5</sup></h2>
<p><strong>Use Suspected acute coronary syndrome clinical pathway https://</strong></p>
<p>clinicalexcellence.qld.gov.au/resources/clinical-pathways/cardiac-clinical- pathways or local pathway(s)</p>
<ul class="pccm-content-list">
<li>Get rapid history of chest pain as per Chest pain assessment<ul><li>site, onset, characteristics, radiation, associated symptoms, timing, exacerbating or relieving factors, severity</li><li>other Life-threatening causes of chest pain, eg aortic dissection, PE, must be considered<sup>1</sup></li></ul></li>
<li>Do ECG + vital signs:<ul><li>send for urgent review by MO within 10 minutes of first patient contact</li><li>if difficult to interpret, MO may send to medical specialist using ECG Flash if available. See https://qheps.health.qld.gov.au/caru/networks/cardiac/ecg-flash</li></ul></li>
<li>Give aspirin 300mg as soon as possible (if not contraindicated/already given)</li>
<li>Give subling glyceryl trinitrate (GTN) if no contraindications:<ul><li>repeat every 5 minutes for ongoing chest pain or discomfort (aim for no pain)</li><li>up to 3 doses</li></ul></li>
<li>Insert IVC x 2</li>
<li>Take blood for troponin and send to the lab</li>
<li>If symptoms not relieved with GTN or for ongoing chest discomfort at any time during initial management:<ul><li>give IV fentanyl or morphine - titrate to pain. See Acute pain, for doses</li><li>note: opioids may delay absorption of clopidogrel and ticagrelor<sup>6</sup></li></ul></li>
<li>Give antiemetic if needed. See Nausea and vomiting</li>
<li>Continuous cardiac monitoring</li>
<li>If SpO<sup>2</sup> &lt; 93% or evidence of shock give O<sup>2</sup><ul><li>use with caution if COPD. Aim for SpO<sup>2</sup> 88–92% if known CO<sup>2</sup> retainer</li><li>titrate O<sup>2</sup> to SpO<sup>2</sup></li></ul></li>
<li>Repeat ECG every 10–15 minutes until pain free</li>
<li>MO may order chest x-ray</li>
<li>Give aspirin 300 mg (chewed or dissolved) if not contraindicated/already given</li>
</ul>

<h2 class="pccm-section-heading-blue">3.	 Clinical assessment<sup>5</sup></h2>
<ul class="pccm-content-list">
<li>MO will assess if reperfusion indicated - with cardiologist advice if needed</li>
<li>Use Suspected acute coronary syndrome clinical pathway/local pathway (summarised below) If YES to ALL If NO to ANY Possible NON-ST-ELEVATION ACS (NSTEACS) Reperfusion NOT indicated Reperfusion IS indicated Does ECG show ST-ELEVATION or (presumed new) LBBB Confirm indications for reperfusion Chest pain &gt; 30 minutes and &lt; 12 hours Persistent ST-elevation ≥ 1 mm in 2 contiguous limb leads OR persistent ST-elevation ≥ 2 mm in 2 contiguous chest leads OR new or presumed new LBBB (Note: if any LBBB get urgent cardiologist advice prior to thrombolysis) MI likely from history Yes No</li>
</ul>
<h2 class="pccm-section-heading-blue">4.	 Management</h2>
<ul class="pccm-content-list">
<li>Reperfusion IS indicated - arrange urgent evacuation:</li>
<li>OPTION 1<ul><li>if access to a cardiac lab for stent (primary PCI) IS possible within ≤ 90 minutes of first diagnostic ECG - MO to immediately contact on call interventional cardiologist</li></ul></li>
<li>OPTION 2<ul><li>if primary PCI is NOT possible within ≤ 90 minutes of first diagnostic ECG - assess for thrombolysis at rural or remote facility</li></ul></li>
<li>If reperfusion NOT indicated - see OPTION 3 OPTION 1: Urgent evacuation for stent (primary PCI)<sup>5</sup></li>
<li>STEMI + reperfusion indicated + ABLE to access cardiac lab within 90 minutes Use Suspected acute coronary syndrome clinical pathway https:// clinicalexcellence.qld.gov.au/resources/clinical-pathways/cardiac-clinical- pathways or local pathway(s)</li>
<li>MO will urgently:<ul><li>contact on call interventional cardiologist</li><li>arrange urgent evacuation</li><li>order antithrombotic therapy:</li><li>aspirin 300 mg - if not already given</li><li>ticagrelor 180 mg oral - or alternative if advised by interventional cardiologist</li><li>unfractionated heparin/heparin sodium - to confirm with interventional</li><li>cardiologist</li></ul></li>
<li>Continuous cardiac monitoring</li>
<li>Frequently monitor vital signs</li>
<li>Continue to liaise with MO for further management until evacuation OPTION 2: Thrombolysis at rural or remote facility<sup>9</sup></li>
<li>STEMI + reperfusion indicated + NOT ABLE to access cardiac lab within minutes Use Thrombolysis for STEMI clinical pathway https://clinicalexcellence.qld.gov.au/ resources/clinical-pathways/cardiac-clinical-pathways or local pathway(s) MO to assess if suitable for thrombolysis at rural or remote facility Check for Contraindications for thrombolysis ABSOLUTE CONTRAINDICATIONS RELATIVE CONTRAINDICATIONS Active bleeding or bleeding tendency (excluding menses) Suspected aortic dissection Significant closed head or facial trauma ≤ 3 months Any prior intracranial haemorrhage Ischaemic stroke ≤ 3 months Known cerebral vascular lesion Known malignant intracranial neoplasm Current anticoagulants, including novel anticoagulant agents Non-compressible vascular puncture Recent major surgery (&lt; 3 weeks) Traumatic or prolonged (&gt; 10 minutes) CPR Recent internal bleeding - within 4 weeks/active peptic ulcer Suspected pericarditis Advanced liver disease/advanced metastatic cancer History of chronic, severe, poorly controlled hypertension Severe uncontrolled hypertension on this presentation - systolic BP &gt; 180 or diastolic BP &gt; Ischaemic stroke &gt; 3 months ago, known intracranial abnormality (not covered in absolute contraindications)/dementia Pregnancy or ≤ 1 week postpartum ANY contraindications to thrombolysis or UNSURE MO will order thrombolysis Aim to give within 30 minutes of initial presentation MO to urgently consult cardiologist for advice Yes No Pre thrombolysis</li>
<li>Informed verbal consent required</li>
<li>Insert IVC x 2 if not in situ</li>
<li>Record baseline:<ul><li>vital signs + continuous cardiac monitoring</li><li>circulation observations ie for bleeding</li><li>neurological observations - GCS</li></ul></li>
<li>Weigh patient Thrombolysis medication</li>
<li>Note: patient should be monitored by staff trained in ALS with access to a defibrillator:<ul><li>significant arrhythmias including VF can occur after reperfusion - have pads on chest</li></ul></li>
<li>On MO order ONLY, give:<ul><li>aspirin 300 mg - if not already given</li><li>clopidogrel 300 mg - orally</li><li>Streptokinase only on Medical Order (await alternative from Pharmacist if needed)</li></ul></li>
<li>Prepare for urgent evacuation to PCI capable hospital:<ul><li>send copies of ECGs with other documentation</li><li>keep under direct observation of RN until evacuated</li></ul></li>
<li>Continuous cardiac monitoring:<ul><li>alert to arrhythmias including VF</li></ul></li>
<li>Monitor frequently:<ul><li>vital signs</li><li>circulation and neurological observations - to detect bleeding<sup>1</sup></li></ul></li>
<li>Repeat ECG at 30 minutes, 60 minutes and 90 minutes - to be reviewed by MO</li>
<li>Continuously liaise with MO</li>
<li>If failed reperfusion:<ul><li>ie unresolved pain and ST elevation has not reduced &gt; 50% at 60 minutes</li><li>MO will urgently consult on call interventional cardiologist for further advice OPTION 3: Reperfusion NOT indicated<sup>5</sup></li></ul></li>
<li>Possible NSTEACS or STEMI + does not meet criteria for reperfusion Use Suspected acute coronary syndrome clinical pathway AND Acute coronary syndrome pathway https://clinicalexcellence.qld.gov.au/resources/clinical- pathways/cardiac-clinical-pathways or local pathway(s)</li>
<li>MO will:<ul><li>risk stratify as per the Suspected acute coronary syndrome clinical pathway</li><li>if patient is high risk commence Acute coronary syndrome pathway</li></ul></li>
<li>Be guided by MO for further management, which may include:<sup>5</sup><ul><li>urgent evacuation to cardiac interventional facility</li><li>continuous cardiac monitoring</li><li>repeat ECGs</li><li>repeat troponin in 3 hours (laboratory) or 6–8 hours (i-STAT) after presentation +<ul><li>CHEM<sup>20</sup>, FBC, coagulation studies, HbA1C, lipid profile</li></ul></li></ul></li>
<li>BGL</li>
<li>frequent monitoring of:<ul><li>vital signs</li><li>circulation and neurological observations</li><li>SpO<sup>2</sup></li></ul></li>
<li>If assessed as low risk, MO may advise patient can be discharged home if:<ul><li>repeat ECG normal</li><li>repeat troponin negative at 3 hours (laboratory) or 6–8 hours (i-STAT)</li><li>no further chest pain</li></ul></li>
</ul>
