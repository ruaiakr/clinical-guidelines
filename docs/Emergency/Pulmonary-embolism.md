<h1 class="pccm-page-title">Pulmonary embolism (PE) - Adult</h1>
<div class="pccm-guideline-panel">
<h2 class="pccm-section-heading">Background</h2>
<ul class="pccm-content-list">
<li>A PE is a life threatening clot in the lung.</li>
</ul>
</div>

<h2 class="pccm-section-heading-blue">1. May present with<sup>2</sup></h2>
<ul class="pccm-content-list">
<li>Acute onset SOB</li>
<li>Hypotension, ↑ HR, ↑ RR, ↓ SpO<sup>2</sup></li>
<li>Chest pain - sharp/pleuritic, usually one sided ±<ul><li>signs of DVT</li><li>coughing up blood</li><li>shock, collapse (massive PE</li></ul></li>
</ul>
<h2 class="pccm-section-heading-blue">2.	 Immediate management</h2>
<ul class="pccm-content-list">
<li>May have massive PE if sBP &lt; 90, tachy/bradycardia, RR &gt; 20, SpO<sup>2</sup> &lt; 94% (&lt; if COPD)<sup>3</sup><ul><li>send for help</li><li>contact MO/MA urgently + urgent evacuation</li><li>insert IVC x 2</li><li>MO/MA may seek specialist advice ± order Streptokinase</li><li>for doses</li><li>CPR as needed. See BLS</li></ul></li>
</ul>
<h2 class="pccm-section-heading-blue">3.	 Clinical assessment<sup>1</sup></h2>
<ul class="pccm-content-list">
<li>Do Chest pain assessment</li>
<li>Ask about risk factors:<ul><li>cancer, recent surgery or hospitalisation, current/prior DVT, pregnant, 6 weeks postpartum,recent immobilisation, oestrogen use eg contraceptive pill</li></ul></li>
<li>Do vital signs:<ul><li>give O<sup>2</sup> if SpO<sup>2</sup> &lt; 94% or 88% if COPD</li></ul></li>
<li>Pregnancy test if female of reproductive age<sup>2</sup></li>
<li>ECG within 10 minutes + continuous cardiac monitoring</li>
<li>Contact MO/MA urgently</li>
<li>If NO signs of massive PE:<ul><li>in collaboration with MO/MA assess likelihood of PE using Wells score for PE below</li></ul></li>
</ul>
<h2 class="pccm-section-heading-blue">4.	 Management</h2>
<ul class="pccm-content-list">
<li>Continue management as per MO/MA</li>
<li>If anti coagulant ordered:</li>
<li>take blood first - FBC, coagulation studies, UECr</li>
<li>Offer analgesia. See Acute pain</li>
<li>Closely monitor vital signs until evacuated</li>
</ul>
<h2 class="pccm-section-heading-blue">5.	 Follow up</h2>
<ul class="pccm-content-list">
<li>As advised by MO/MA</li>
</ul>
<h2 class="pccm-section-heading-blue">6.	 Referral/consultation</h2>
<ul class="pccm-content-list">
<li>Requires medical admission WELLS SCORE FOR PE<sup>1</sup> Signs/symptoms of DVT 1 Coughing up blood 1 Immobility or surgery in prior 4 weeks 1 Alternative diagnosis less likely than PE 1 Previous DVT or PE 1 Cancer 1 HR &gt; 1 Score Score 0–1 PE unlikely D-dimer negative MO may consider other diagnoses Take blood for D-dimer<sup>1</sup></li>
<li>If result will take &gt; 4 hours or D-dimer not readily available:1<ul><li>MO may seek specialist advice ± order enoxaparin</li><li>see DVT, for dose Score 0≥2 PE likely D-dimer positive</li></ul></li>
<li>MO may order:1<ul><li>urgent retrieval for CTPA or VQ scan</li><li>enoxaparin. See DVT, for dose Urgent retrieval for CT pulmonary angiogram (CTPA) or ventilation perfusion scan (VQ)</li></ul></li>
<li>MO may order:<ul><li>enoxaparin</li><li>see DVT, for dose</li></ul></li>
</ul>
