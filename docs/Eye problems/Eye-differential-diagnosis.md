Ask patient to keep looking
downwards as you take hold of the
eyelashes and then gently pull the lid
slightly towards you (image 1)
Place cotton bud at the lid crease (or
5 mm from lid edge) and apply very
light pressure downward with the bud
(images 1 & 2)
Evert the eyelid by using the eyelashes
to gently pull the lid upwards over the
bud. Remove the bud (image 3)
