# <span style="color: #EF6A4C;">Kiribati Primary Clinical Care Manual 2026</span>

**Ministry of Health and Medical Services — Republic of Kiribati**

Welcome to the digital edition of the Kiribati Primary Clinical Care Manual. This resource supports nurses, medical assistants, and allied health professionals delivering primary health care across Kiribati.

**<span style="color: #EF6A4C;">About this manual</span>**</br>

This manual has been designed to serve as a comprehensive guide for
healthcare providers, including nurses, medical assistants, and allied
health professionals, working within Kiribati’s Primary Health Care (PHC)
system. It offers essential clinical information, guidelines, protocols, and
best practices for managing common health conditions, diseases, and
emergency situations, ensuring that healthcare providers can deliver
quality care at the community level. The manual will support healthcare
workers in conducting high-level patient assessments, managing
medications, and following evidence-based protocols that align with
international and local healthcare standards.


The primary purpose of this manual is to empower healthcare providers
with the necessary knowledge and resources to deliver safe, effective,
and patient-centered care. It is intended to be a practical tool that can
be referred to on a daily basis, supporting clinical decision-making and
facilitating consistent, high-quality healthcare delivery across all health
centers and outreach services in Kiribati.

**<span style="color: #EF6A4C;">Structure of the Manual</span>**</br>

The manual is organized into clear chapters based on the most common
health issues encountered in primary care settings in Kiribati. Each chapter
includes: 
<br />
* Overview of the health condition or issue – A concise explanation of the
disease or condition, its prevalence, and its impact on public health in
Kiribati.
<br />
* Diagnosis and clinical assessment – Key diagnostic criteria, assessment
techniques, and patient history gathering to make an accurate
diagnosis.
<br />
* Management and treatment protocols – Evidence-based
recommendations for managing the condition, including
pharmacological and non-pharmacological treatment options.
<br />
* Prevention and health promotion – Guidance on how to prevent the
condition, including community-based prevention strategies and education.
* Referral guidelines – When and how to refer patients for further
diagnostic testing, specialist care, or hospital-based treatment.
<br />


Each chapter also highlights important considerations such as patient
safety, cultural factors, and common challenges in the Kiribati context.
Throughout the manual, clinical flow charts, decision-making algorithms,
and checklists are included to guide healthcare providers through the
diagnostic and treatment process, ensuring that no steps are overlooked.


**<span style="color: #EF6A4C;">Integration of the Manual Within the Model of Care</span>**</br>

This manual is not an isolated resource; it is an integral part of the broader
healthcare model in Kiribati. The primary goal of the manual is to ensure
that all healthcare providers can confidently and competently deliver
essential services within the PHC framework. This manual complements
the Model of Care by guiding healthcare workers in providing services that
align with national health priorities and international standards.

The Model of Care in Kiribati is based on a comprehensive and patient-
centered approach to healthcare, which includes prevention, diagnosis,
treatment, and follow-up. The manual supports this approach by offering
evidence-based guidelines for managing health conditions at the
community level and ensuring that healthcare providers are well-equipped
to address the most common health issues. It ensures that patients receive
consistent and high-quality care, whether they are in urban or rural areas,
and helps maintain continuity of care by guiding healthcare providers on
follow-up visits and patient education.

Furthermore, the manual's integration with the regulatory and governance
frameworks, such as the Pharmaceuticals Legislation and Professional
Governance, ensures that healthcare providers adhere to the legal and
ethical standards governing medical practice in Kiribati. It also reinforces
the importance of maintaining scope of practice for nurses, medical
assistants, and doctors, ensuring that each provider works within their
professional boundaries, making appropriate referrals, and collaborating
effectively within the healthcare system.

Use of Antibiotics: All antimicrobial prescribing must align with the Kiribati
Antibiotic Guidelines (Second edition, 2022, Ministry of Health and Medical
Services, Government of Kiribati) or the most recent version. Clinicians
should use these Guidelines alongside this Manual to ensure compliance
with national policy and antimicrobial stewardship. Where there is
uncertainty or a deviation from the Guidelines is being considered, seek
advice from MHMS.

**<span style="color: #EF6A4C;">The Manual’s Role in Education and Training</span>**</br>

The manual is also a critical tool for education and training, especially for
nurses, medical assistants, and other healthcare providers who may be
less familiar with advanced medical procedures. It serves as a practical
resource during formal and informal training, allowing trainers to use it
as a reference for case studies, skills-building exercises, and continuing
education. Nurses and medical assistants can learn best practices, clinical
guidelines, and patient management techniques that will improve their
clinical competencies, helping them to undertake high-level patient
assessments and interventions with greater confidence.

For those involved in capacity building and training, the manual should
be used as a reference point in teaching clinical skills and protocols,
ensuring that PHC workers have a standard and consistent approach to
care. This also allows for continuous improvement as health conditions
and treatment guidelines evolve, ensuring that healthcare workers stay
updated with the latest evidence and standards of care.

**<span style="color: #EF6A4C;">Feedback and Continuous Improvement</span>**</br>

To ensure that the manual remains a living document that evolves with the
needs of the healthcare system, healthcare providers are encouraged to
provide feedback on the manual’s usability, content, and clinical guidelines.
This feedback will help identify areas for improvement and updates in
future editions, ensuring that the manual remains relevant, practical,
and effective in meeting the healthcare needs of the Kiribati population.

forefront of care delivery, improving patient outcomes, and contributing to
