# Cardiovascular and respiratory disorders

7.	 Follow up
•	 Monthly for BP or glucose control
•	 Annually for lipid profile, proteinuria, foot and eye checks
•	 Reinforce lifestyle advice at each visit
8.	 Referral/Consultation Urgent referral to hospital if:
•	 BP >200/120 mmHg after rest
•	 Blood glucose >30 mmol/L or >20 mmol/L with symptoms
•	 Diabetic foot ulcers, severe infection, or vision loss
•	 New chest pain, stroke/TIA symptoms
Refer to physician if:
•	 Persistent BP >140/90 in people &lt;40 yrs
•	 Target organ damage or known CVD
•	 Poor glucose control despite oral meds
•	 Newly diagnosed DM with urine ketones 2+
•	 Total cholesterol >8 mmol/L
