<h1 class="pccm-page-title">Drowning/submersion - Adult/Child</h1>

<div class="pccm-guideline-panel">
<h2 class="pccm-section-heading">Recommend</h2>
<ul>
<li>Ventilation + oxygenation to reverse hypoxia</li>
<li>Active rewarming is not routinely recommended. May lead to rapid overshoot of core T</li>
</ul>
</div>

<h2 class="pccm-section-heading-blue">1. May present with</h2>
<ul class="pccm-content-list">
<li>Immersion ± vomiting, respiratory compromise, apnoea or in cardiac arrest<sup>1,3</sup></li>
<li>Suspect spinal injury if history indicates eg dived into shallow water, found near dumping surf, rocks or after boating accident<sup>2</sup></li>
</ul>

<h2 class="pccm-section-heading-blue">2. Immediate management</h2>
<ul class="pccm-content-list">
<li>Rapidly assess + manage concurrently<sup>1</sup></li>
<li>DRSABCD + <a href="/docs/Emergency/Basic-life-support">BLS</a>, as needed. <strong>Note:</strong> if hypothermia do prolonged CPR</li>
<li>Assess patient on back with head + body at same level:
<ul>
<li>not head down - risk of regurgitation + vomiting<sup>2</sup></li>
<li>only roll on side if airway obstructed, reassess condition after rolling<sup>2</sup></li>
<li>c-spine immobilisation, if spinal injury suspected — see <a href="/docs/Emergency/Spinal-injuries">Spinal injuries</a></li>
</ul>
</li>
<li>If breathing, place on side + open airway eg head tilt<sup>2</sup></li>
<li>Do:
<ul>
<li>vital signs + GCS</li>
<li>BGL</li>
</ul>
</li>
<li>Consult MO urgently</li>
<li>If SpO<sub>2</sub> &lt; 95% + respiratory compromise eg ↑WOB:<sup>1</sup>
<ul>
<li>give O<sub>2</sub> to maintain SpO<sub>2</sub> ≥ 95% in adults + children</li>
<li>if unable to maintain SpO<sub>2</sub> with O<sub>2</sub> + adequate conscious state (GCS 13–15):
<ul>
<li>Refer to the main hospital. Remove wet clothing, dry + keep warm with blankets<sup>2</sup></li>
</ul>
</li>
</ul>
</li>
</ul>

<h2 class="pccm-section-heading-blue">3. Clinical assessment</h2>
<ul class="pccm-content-list">
<li>Ask about:<sup>1</sup>
<ul>
<li>circumstances leading to the drowning, duration of immersion</li>
<li>resuscitation, length of CPR + drugs given</li>
<li>witnesses</li>
<li>fresh or salt water, contaminated eg agricultural, sewage</li>
<li>If cause of drowning not clear, ask about:<sup>1</sup>
<ul>
<li>epilepsy</li>
<li>personal + family history of arrhythmias</li>
<li>diabetes</li>
<li>drug + alcohol use</li>
<li>dementia<sup>2</sup></li>
</ul>
</li>
</ul>
</li>
<li>Do physical examination, including:<sup>1</sup>
<ul>
<li>listen to chest - any crackles, wheeze</li>
<li>observe for few hours to monitor patient’s progress</li>
<li>if distended stomach do not try to empty by applying external pressure<sup>2</sup></li>
<li>check for other injuries. Avoid prolonged exposure to prevent heat loss</li>
</ul>
</li>
<li>Consider non-accidental injury or neglect if child with inconsistent histories, an obvious lapse in supervision, a delay in seeking care or other injuries eg bruises, old fractures.<sup>1</sup> See <a href="/docs/Paediatrics/Child-protection">Child protection</a></li>
</ul>

<h2 class="pccm-section-heading-blue">4. Management</h2>
<ul class="pccm-content-list">
<li>MO may advise:<sup>1</sup>
<ul>
<li>IV/Intraosseous, sodium chloride 0.9%, if child 20 mL/kg</li>
<li>IV antibiotics if grossly contaminated water</li>
<li>insert IDC, nasogastric tube</li>
<li>evacuation/hospitalisation - early contact with retrieval service is advised</li>
</ul>
</li>
<li>Monitor closely until evacuation:<sup>1,2</sup>
<ul>
<li>continue O<sub>2</sub> + encourage to cough + take deep breaths</li>
<li>keep warm</li>
<li>vital signs + strict fluid balance</li>
</ul>
</li>
<li>If patient initially appears well, they may still deteriorate due to pulmonary oedema:
<ul>
<li>observe for 4–8 hours before considering discharge in collaboration with MO<sup>1,3</sup></li>
</ul>
</li>
</ul>

<h2 class="pccm-section-heading-blue">5. Follow up</h2>
<ul class="pccm-content-list">
<li>As per MO</li>
</ul>

<h2 class="pccm-section-heading-blue">6. Referral/consultation</h2>
<ul class="pccm-content-list">
<li>A person in charge of a hospital is required to notify immersions of a child &lt; 5 years in a swimming pool<sup>1</sup></li>
</ul>
</div>
