<h1 class="pccm-page-title">Mumps</h1>

<div class="pccm-guideline-panel">
<h2 class="pccm-section-heading">Recommend</h2>
<ul class="pccm-content-list">
<li>Vaccination with the MMR vaccine is the best way to prevent mumps.</li>
<li>Isolate individuals with suspected mumps to prevent transmission.</li>
<li>Supportive care is essential to manage symptoms.</li>
</ul>
<h2 class="pccm-section-heading">Background</h2>
<ul class="pccm-content-list">
<li>Mumps is a viral infection caused by an RNA paramyxovirus spread via respiratory droplets that primarily affects the parotid glands, causing painful swelling. For unvaccinated children, it often occurs in school-aged children It can lead to complications such as orchitis, meningitis, and pancreatitis, particularly in post-pubertal males.</li>
</ul>
</div>

<h2 class="pccm-section-heading-blue" id="may-present-with">1. May present with</h2>
<ul class="pccm-content-list">
<li>Painful swelling of the parotid glands (near the jaw)</li>
<li>Fever and headache</li>
<li>Malaise and fatigue</li>
<li>Pain during chewing or swallowing</li>
</ul>

<h2 class="pccm-section-heading-blue" id="immediate-management">2. Immediate management</h2>
<ul class="pccm-content-list">
<li>Isolate the patient to reduce the risk of transmission.</li>
<li>Provide analgesics for pain relief (e.g., paracetamol).</li>
<li>Encourage fluids and soft foods to ease swallowing.</li>
</ul>

<h2 class="pccm-section-heading-blue" id="clinical-assessment">3. Clinical assessment</h2>
<ul class="pccm-content-list">
<li>Examine for parotid gland swelling and tenderness.</li>
<li>Assess for complications such as orchitis or <a href="/docs/Emergency/Meningitis">meningitis</a>. See also <a href="/docs/Genitourinary%20emergencies/Testicularscrotal-pain">Testicular/scrotal pain</a>.</li>
<li>Check vaccination history to determine if the patient has received the MMR vaccine.</li>
</ul>

<h2 class="pccm-section-heading-blue" id="management">4. Management</h2>
<ul class="pccm-content-list">
<li>Provide symptomatic relief with pain management and hydration.</li>
<li>Monitor for complications such as orchitis, meningitis, and pancreatitis.</li>
<li>Educate the patient on the importance of isolation to prevent spread.</li>
</ul>

<h2 class="pccm-section-heading-blue" id="follow-up">5. Follow-up</h2>
<ul class="pccm-content-list">
<li>Follow up within 2-3 weeks to monitor for complications.</li>
<li>Check for any residual symptoms, particularly related to orchitis or hearing loss.</li>
</ul>

<h2 class="pccm-section-heading-blue" id="referral-consultation">6. Referral/Consultation</h2>
<ul class="pccm-content-list">
<li>Refer to a specialist if complications like orchitis or meningitis are suspected.</li>
<li>Consider referral to a hearing specialist if there is suspected hearing loss.</li>
</ul>
