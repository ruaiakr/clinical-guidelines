Trachoma - Adult/Child
Background
•	 Repeated infection by the bacterium Chlamydia trachomatis causing
Conjunctivitis and possibly leading to trichiasis
•	 Different strains from the STI chlamydia.
•	 can lead to eyelid contraction and in-turned eyelashes that rub on the eyeball,
causing painful
1.
May present with1,2
•	 Child - repeated conjunctivitis
•	 Adult - in-turned eyelashes, cloudy cornea

–
Painful eyes

–
White or watery discharge

–
Photophobia

–
Irritated Red eye

–
Nearly always bilateral
2.	 Immediate management Not applicable
3.	 Clinical assessment
•	 Get history, including:1,2

–
previous episodes for patient and family

–
time spent in known area of outbreak

–
contact with person(s) with similar symptoms
•	 Do vital signs
•	 Examine eyes as per Eye assessment,  including:

–
evert the upper eyelids, look for:2
	›
≥ 5 pale round spots (follicles)
	›
intense redness, swelling - can you see normal blood vessels
	›
note: follicles ± intense redness are indications of active trachoma - take
eyelid swab for chlamydia PCR
	›
visible scarring, in-turned eyelashes, signs of pulling out eyelashes

–
check cornea - clear or cloudy

4.	 Management
•	 Surgery: For those who have entropion or Trichiasis

–
Epilation of in-turned lashes if surgery is not available
•	 Antibiotics:

–
Treats the Infected but also helps prevent the spread

–
Azithromycin (Zithromax)
	›
Now the main treatment for active Trachoma
	›
Single dose
	›
PO once per year
	›
Available in tablets and liquid (Paediatric Oral Suspension) for 6months old
- 7 years old plus children who are unable to swallow tablets.

–
note: aim to treat all members of relevant household(s) within 1 week of
starting treatment2

–
Tetracycline eye ointment
	›
Old Treatment
	›
Apply BID for 6 weeks  for &lt;6months old.
	›
Treats eye infection only
•	 Facial Cleanliness:

–
Facial washing is an important component
•	 Environmental Improvement:

–
Remove the environmental risk factors for trachoma

–
Includes bringing better water supply and toilets to a community

–
May have to convince people to change their habits:
	›
People must use the clean water made available to clean their hands and
faces
	›
People must not leave human and animal waste uncovered as it attracts
flies
	›
It is often very difficult for people to change their behaviours.
