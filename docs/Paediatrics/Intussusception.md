<h1 class="pccm-page-title">Intussusception - Child</h1>

<div class="pccm-guideline-panel">
<h2 class="pccm-section-heading">Background</h2>
<ul class="pccm-content-list">
<li>A condition where one part of the bowel telescopes into the next part and may cause a blockage<sup>1</sup></li>
<li>Commonly occurs 2 months–2 years, but can occur at any age<sup>2</sup></li>
</ul>
</div>

<h2 class="pccm-section-heading-blue" id="may-present-with">1. May present with<sup>2</sup></h2>
<ul class="pccm-content-list">
<li>Intermittent pain or distress</li>
<li>Lethargy</li>
<li>Diarrhoea and vomiting</li>
<li>Blood seen in stool</li>
</ul>

<h2 class="pccm-section-heading-blue" id="immediate-management">2. Immediate management<sup>2</sup></h2>
<ul class="pccm-content-list">
<li>If signs of shock ↑HR, ↑RR, ↓BP, ↓LOC. See <a href="/docs/Emergency/Shock">Shock</a></li>
</ul>

<h2 class="pccm-section-heading-blue" id="clinical-assessment">3. Clinical assessment<sup>2</sup></h2>
<ul class="pccm-content-list">
<li>Ask about:
<ul>
<li>pain - does it come and go</li>
<li>vomiting - any bile</li>
<li>stools - any bleeding, red currant jelly stools</li>
<li>pallor, lethargy - can be episodic and may look well between episodes</li>
<li>recent rotavirus vaccination</li>
</ul>
</li>
<li>Do:
<ul>
<li>vital signs</li>
<li>weight - bare weight if &lt; 2 years</li>
<li>check abdomen for:
<ul>
<li>sausage shaped mass on right side</li>
<li>distension, tenderness, guarding</li>
</ul>
</li>
<li>check stool/nappy for blood</li>
</ul>
</li>
</ul>

<h2 class="pccm-section-heading-blue" id="management">4. Management<sup>2</sup></h2>
<ul class="pccm-content-list">
<li>Consult MO who may advise:
<ul>
<li>analgesia</li>
<li>evacuation/hospitalisation</li>
<li>nil by mouth, consider NGT if transferring by air</li>
</ul>
</li>
<li>Monitor closely until evacuated</li>
</ul>

<h2 class="pccm-section-heading-blue" id="follow-up">5. Follow up</h2>
<ul class="pccm-content-list">
<li>As per MO instructions</li>
</ul>

<h2 class="pccm-section-heading-blue" id="referral-consultation">6. Referral/consultation</h2>
<ul class="pccm-content-list">
<li>Consult MO if suspected intussusception</li>
</ul>
