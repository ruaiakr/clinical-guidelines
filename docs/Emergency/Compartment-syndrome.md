<h1 class="pccm-page-title">Compartment syndrome - Adult/Child</h1>
<p class="pccm-page-subtitle">treatment causing</p>

<ul class="pccm-content-list">
<li>Limb threatening emergency - urgent evacuation for surgical management 1,2</li>
<li>Be alert in all limb injuries eg closed leg + forearm fractures, crush injuries or</li>
<li>compression/constriction eg casts/splints, circumferential bandaging<sup>2</sup></li>
<li>Hypoxia of muscle + nerves caused by bleeding + swelling in closed muscle cavity, leading to necrosis + amputation<sup>1</sup></li>
</ul>
<h2 class="pccm-section-heading-blue">1. May present with</h2>
<ul class="pccm-content-list">
<li>Limb injury + pain out of proportion to what would be expected ± not responding to analgesia<sup>1</sup></li>
<li>If child - anxiety, agitation, unable to rest comfortably<sup>3</sup></li>
</ul>
<h2 class="pccm-section-heading-blue">2.	 Immediate management</h2>
<ul class="pccm-content-list">
<li>Remove any bandaging and split the casts/splints to relieve pressure:3<ul><li>leave a portion of cast/splint to stabilise fracture as needed</li><li>Elevate the limb with the splint above the level of the heart</li><li>Give analgesic medication – panadol / NSAIDs</li></ul></li>
<li>Rapidly assess:2<ul><li>pain - severity, is it responding to analgesia</li><li>passively extend fingers/toes distal to injury, does this cause pain</li><li>any ↓ sensation, numbness, pins + needles + absent pulses - compare to opposite limb 1-4</li></ul></li>
<li>Contact MO urgently, even if low suspicion of compartment syndrome<sup>2</sup></li>
<li>Offer analgesia. See Acute pain</li>
<li>Rest, apply ice packs + elevate the limb to heart level with pillows 1,4</li>
<li>Insert IVC</li>
</ul>
<h2 class="pccm-section-heading-blue">3.	 Clinical assessment</h2>
<ul class="pccm-content-list">
<li>Ask about:<ul><li>mechanism of injury, when, treatment so far</li><li>underlying bleeding disorders, anticoagulants<sup>2</sup></li></ul></li>
<li>Do physical examination, including:<ul><li>vital signs</li><li>check entire limb for swelling<sup>3</sup></li><li>palpate muscles - firm, tight, tenderness<sup>3</sup></li></ul></li>
</ul>
<h2 class="pccm-section-heading-blue">4.	 Management 1,4</h2>
<ul class="pccm-content-list">
<li>Consult MO who will:<ul><li>order x-ray to exclude displaced fracture</li><li>arrange urgent evacuation</li><li>advise further management eg splinting of limb to prevent further ↑ pressure</li></ul></li>
<li>Nil by mouth</li>
<li>Closely monitor until evacuation:2<ul><li>vital signs + neurovascular observations at least hourly</li><li>pain</li></ul></li>
</ul>

<ul class="pccm-content-list">
<li>As per MA</li>
</ul>

</ul>
