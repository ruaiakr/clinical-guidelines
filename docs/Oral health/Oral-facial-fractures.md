Oral facial fractures (Kiribati Original Protocol)
Purpose:
To assist Medical Assistants and Nurses in the primary care setting to recognize,
assess, provide initial management, and appropriately refer patients presenting with
oral facial fractures.
Background
Facial fractures are increasingly common presentations in primary healthcare.
Prompt recognition and appropriate management are critical to prevent permanent
functional, cosmetic, or vision-threatening complications.
