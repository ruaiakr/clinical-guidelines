# Pneumonia - Adult

Pneumonia - Adult

Background
Pneumonia is an acute infection of the lung parenchyma, often presenting with
cough, fever, pleuritic chest pain, and shortness of breath. It is caused by a range of
pathogens, including bacteria, viruses, and fungi, and its severity can vary widely.
Risk factors include chronic illness, advanced age, recent travel, hazardous alcohol
use, and exposure to environmental sources (e.g. muddy soil/water).
