# Mastoiditis - Adult/Child

Mastoiditis - Adult/Child
Recommend
•	 Refer all suspected mastoiditis cases urgently.
•	 Treat with IV antibiotics if available. If IV access is not possible, give oral
antibiotics while arranging urgent referral.
•	 Consult MO for further assessment and referral.
Background
•	 Mastoiditis occurs when an ear infection spreads to the mastoid bone (behind
the ear), causing swelling, redness, and a possible collection of pus under the
skin behind the pinna (outer ear).
•	 This condition can become serious and requires urgent treatment to prevent
complications.
1.
May present with
•	 Pain behind the ear
•	 Fever
•	 Possible discharge from the ear
2.	 Immediate management
•	 Refer urgently to a health centre for prompt care
•	 Administer IV antibiotics if available
•	 If IV access is not possible, give oral antibiotics
•	 Consult MO urgently
3.	 Clinical assessment
•	 History of ear discharge
•	 Swelling and redness behind the pinna
•	 Pain on pressing the bone behind the pinna (post-auricular pain)
•	 Fluctuant swelling (pus under the skin may feel like a fluid-filled sac)
•	 Fever
•	 Otoscopy may show pus in the ear canal
