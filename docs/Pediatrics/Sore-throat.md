# Sore throat - Adult/Child

•	 Encourage fluids and stress the importance of ensuring that the child remains
well hydrated
•	 Caregivers can use saline nose drops to clear nasal secretions.
•	 Check the immunization status of the child. If he/she is incompletely immunized,
immunize the child during the visit.
•	 Remember that a cold is not a contraindication to vaccination
Possible complications of upper respiratory infections include the following:
•	 Otitis media
•	 Sinusitis (thick, purulent discharge for longer than 2 weeks)
•	 Pneumonia
•	 Asthma and wheezing
