<h2 class="pccm-section-heading-orange" id="calculating-liquid-medication-doses">Calculating Liquid Medication Doses</h2>
<ul class="pccm-content-list">
<li>To calculate the volume of liquid medication to administer, use the formula:</li>
</ul>
<p><strong>Dose required (mg) × Volume available (ml) ÷ Strength available (mg) = Volume to administer (ml)</strong></p>
<p><em>Example:</em></p>
<ul class="pccm-content-list">
<li>If you need 250 mg, and the medication comes as 500 mg in 2 ml:</li>
<li>(250 × 2) ÷ 500 = 1 ml</li>
</ul>
