<h1 class="pccm-page-title">Giardiasis - Adult/Child</h1>

<div class="pccm-guideline-panel">
<h2 class="pccm-section-heading">Background</h2>
<ul class="pccm-content-list">
<li>Ingestion of Giardia cysts from contaminated water or food is the most common route of transmission, but person-to-person transmission may occur<sup>1</sup></li>
</ul>
</div>

<h2 class="pccm-section-heading-blue" id="may-present-with">1. May present with<sup>1,2</sup></h2>
<ul class="pccm-content-list">
<li>Diarrhoea, flatulence</li>
<li>Abdominal cramps, bloating, burping</li>
<li>Nausea, vomiting</li>
<li>Fatigue, weight loss</li>
<li>Positive pathology for Giardia ± symptoms</li>
</ul>

<h2 class="pccm-section-heading-blue" id="immediate-management">2. Immediate management Not applicable</h2>

<h2 class="pccm-section-heading-blue" id="clinical-assessment">3. Clinical assessment<sup>1,2</sup></h2>
<ul class="pccm-content-list">
<li>Ask about:
<ul>
<li>recent travel</li>
<li>diarrhoea - watery, mucus or blood, greasy</li>
<li>vomiting</li>
<li>fever, pain</li>
<li>similar illness in contacts</li>
<li>immunocompromised</li>
<li>water source eg tank water</li>
</ul>
</li>
<li>Do physical examination, including:
<ul>
<li>vital signs</li>
<li>weight - bare weight if &lt; 2 years. Assess against recent weights</li>
<li>Hydration assessment - <a href="/docs/Gastrointestinal/Gastroenteritisdehydration">adult</a>, or <a href="/docs/Paediatrics/Gastroenteritisdehydration">child</a></li>
<li>palpate abdomen for tenderness, guarding</li>
</ul>
</li>
<li>Do a stool MCS + multiplex PCR if:
<ul>
<li>abdominal symptoms &gt; 2 weeks eg pain, diarrhoea<sup>3</sup></li>
</ul>
</li>
</ul>

<h2 class="pccm-section-heading-blue" id="management">4. Management<sup>3,4</sup></h2>
<ul class="pccm-content-list">
<li>Contact MO/MA
<ul>
<li>severe symptoms eg mucus or bloody diarrhoea, pain, fever</li>
<li>dehydrated. For rehydration see <a href="/docs/Paediatrics/Gastroenteritisdehydration">Gastroenteritis - child</a></li>
</ul>
</li>
<li>If no symptoms but positive pathology:
<ul>
<li>antibiotics are not required, unless patient handles food<sup>3</sup></li>
<li>advise patient infection usually self-curing<sup>1</sup></li>
</ul>
</li>
<li>If symptomatic give metronidazole 30mg/kg od for 3 days</li>
<li>Advise patient:
<ul>
<li>if they prepare or serve food, avoid handling food until they have not had any diarrhoea for 48 hours</li>
<li>stay away from others until 24 hours after the last loose bowel motion and they are well<sup>5</sup></li>
<li>wash hands frequently to prevent spreading</li>
</ul>
</li>
</ul>

<div class="pccm-drug-box" id="metronidazole">
<div class="pccm-drug-header">
<span class="pccm-drug-name">METRONIDAZOLE</span>
<span class="pccm-drug-level">L2</span>
</div>

<td>Oral</td>

<div class="pccm-drug-notes">
<p><strong>Offer CMI:</strong> Avoid alcohol while taking and for 24 hours after finishing the course. Take tablet with food to reduce stomach upset. Take oral liquid 1 hour before food for better absorption. May cause nausea, anorexia, abdominal pain, vomiting, diarrhoea, metallic taste, dizziness or headache</p>
<p><strong>Management of associated emergency:</strong> Consult MO. See <a href="/docs/Emergency/Anaphylaxis">Anaphylaxis</a><sup>6,7</sup></p>
</div>
</div>

<h2 class="pccm-section-heading-blue" id="follow-up">5. Follow up<sup>3</sup></h2>
<ul class="pccm-content-list">
<li>Advise to be reviewed if symptoms do not settle or concerned:
<ul>
<li>contact MO who may consider reinfection, drug resistance, alternative diagnosis</li>
</ul>
</li>
</ul>

</ul>
