<h1 class="pccm-page-title">Basic life support (BLS) - Adult/Child</h1>
<p class="pccm-page-subtitle">DRSABCD resuscitation, the collapsed patient</p>

<div class="pccm-guideline-panel">
<h2 class="pccm-section-heading">Recommend<sup>1-4</sup></h2>
<ul>
<li>Good quality CPR and reducing time to defibrillation are the highest priorities in resuscitation from sudden cardiac arrest</li>
<li>Airway takes precedent over any injury, including possibility of spinal injury - handle gently with no twisting or bending of spinal column or neck<sup>1</sup></li>
<li>If pregnant, once CPR in progress put padding such as a towel under the woman’s right hip to tilt the hips about 15–30°, but leave her shoulders flat</li>
<li>Child/infant - central pulse palpation may be used to assess circulation. If a pulse cannot be definitely identified within 10 seconds (or HR &lt; 60 with signs of poor circulation) start CPR</li>
</ul>
<h2 class="pccm-section-heading">Background</h2>
<ul>
<li>Agonal breaths (occasional irregular gasps) are common in early stages of cardiac arrest</li>
</ul>
</div>

<h2 class="pccm-section-heading-blue">1. May present with<sup>2</sup></h2>
<ul class="pccm-content-list">
<li>Sudden collapse</li>
<li>Unresponsive and absent or abnormal breathing ie gasping</li>
</ul>

<h2 class="pccm-section-heading-blue">2. Immediate management<sup>1-6</sup></h2>
<ul class="pccm-content-list">
<li>DRSABCD as per Basic life support flowchart</li>
<li><strong>Danger</strong> - check the environment/patient surroundings are safe before proceeding. PPE as needed</li>
<li><strong>Response</strong> - verbal and tactile stimuli (talk and touch)</li>
<li><strong>Send for help</strong> - shout for help. If help not arrived in 1 minute go get help 5+ contact MO/RSQ</li>
<li>If trauma related control catastrophic haemorrhage</li>
<li><strong>Airway</strong> (leave person in position found):<sup>1-5</sup>
<ul>
<li>open using head tilt–chin lift or jaw thrust (if neck injury, only use jaw thrust) +
<ul>
<li>child - head in ‘sniffing position’</li>
<li>infant &lt; 1 year - head in neutral position</li>
</ul>
</li>
<li>if fluid/matter obstructs airway at any time:
<ul>
<li>use suction if available OR if unavailable, roll on to side to clear airway<sup>1</sup></li>
<li>if visible material, remove if possible eg using rescuer’s fingers<sup>1</sup></li>
</ul>
</li>
</ul>
</li>
<li><strong>Breathing:</strong>
<ul>
<li>Look (movement of chest/abdomen), Listen (for breath sounds), Feel (air on rescuer’s cheek)</li>
<li>if unresponsive but breathing normally, see <a href="/docs/Emergency/Critical-emergencies-Unconsciousaltered-level-of-consciousness">Unconscious patient</a></li>
</ul>
</li>
<li>If unresponsive + breathing absent or NOT normal, place on firm surface and start CPR:
<ul>
<li>30 compressions: 2 ventilations (adult + child) OR</li>
<li>if paediatric BLS trained - child: 2 ventilations (first): 15 compressions<sup>5</sup></li>
<li>100–120 compressions per minute</li>
</ul>
</li>
<li><strong>Defibrillation</strong> - attach automated external defibrillator (AED) as soon as available and follow prompts:
<ul>
<li>adult and child &gt; 8 use adult pads. Child 1–8 use paediatric pads (or adult size if not available)</li>
<li>continue CPR + minimise interruptions to chest compressions</li>
<li>alternate person doing compressions when AED advises to stop (eg to analyse rhythm/shock) or if fatigued</li>
</ul>
</li>
<li>Get ALS trained clinician(s) if available</li>
<li>Continue CPR until responsive, normal breathing returns, or MO/SNO/MA instructs otherwise</li>
</ul>

<h2 class="pccm-section-heading-blue">3. Clinical assessment - See Immediate management</h2>

<h2 class="pccm-section-heading-blue">4. Management</h2>
<ul class="pccm-content-list">
<li>Control bleeding, protect from weather + other first aid measures depending on circumstances</li>
</ul>

<h2 class="pccm-section-heading-blue">5. Follow up</h2>
<ul class="pccm-content-list">
<li>According to patient’s condition/presentation</li>
<li>Sensitive, professional debriefing of people involved in resuscitation is valuable</li>
<li>Provide support for family members</li>
<li>If required, see Patient death in absence of MA</li>
</ul>

<h2 class="pccm-section-heading-blue">6. Referral/consultation</h2>
<ul class="pccm-content-list">
<li>Always contact Medical Officer</li>
</ul>
