<h2 class="pccm-section-heading-blue" id="child-with-fever">Child with fever</h2>

<figure class="pccm-flowchart-figure">
<img src="/img/paediatrics/child-with-fever.png" alt="Child with fever differential diagnosis flowchart from Green Book" />
<p class="pccm-flowchart-caption">Child with fever (source page)</p>
</figure>

<div class="pccm-note-panel">
<ul>
<li><strong>Babies &lt; 3 months of age with T ≥ 38 contact MO urgently<sup>1,2</sup></strong></li>
<li>Fever in most children &lt; 5 years old has a viral cause. A careful assessment will identify focus of infection in most patients</li>
<li><strong>Consult MO if fever with no obvious source of infection, or at any time you are unsure</strong></li>
</ul>
</div>

<div class="pccm-alert-panel">
<strong>Always consider <a href="/docs/Emergency/Sepsisseptic-shock">Sepsis</a></strong>
<ul class="pccm-content-list" style="margin-bottom:0;">
<li>Suspect febrile neutropenia if T ≥ 38.5 × 1 OR ≥ 38 × 2 an hour apart + chemotherapy in prior 2 weeks OR absolute neutrophil count (ANC) &lt; 1 × 10<sup>9</sup>/L</li>
</ul>
</div>

<div class="pccm-guideline-panel">
<h2 class="pccm-section-heading">Treating fever</h2>
<ul class="pccm-content-list">
<li>Remove excess layers of clothing, but ensure child is not under-dressed. Do not tepid sponge. Encourage oral fluids if tolerated to maintain hydration</li>
<li>Consider paracetamol OR ibuprofen if child appears distressed (do not use with sole aim of reducing T)<sup>1,2</sup></li>
<li>Parent/carer fact sheet Fever in children: <a href="https://www.childrens.health.qld.gov.au/chq/information-for-families/fact-sheets/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">www.childrens.health.qld.gov.au/chq/information-for-families/fact-sheets/</a></li>
</ul>
</div>

<div class="pccm-note-panel">
<ul>
<li><strong>Contact MO urgently</strong></li>
<li>Stridor is a harsh vibrating sound occurring on inspiration due to upper airway obstruction</li>
<li>Consider causes: croup (common), inhaled foreign body, epiglottitis (rare but important), trauma, angio-oedema, mass (tumor or abscess)</li>
<li>Get rapid history, including Hib immunization status. Limit examination. Do not examine mouth or throat</li>
<li>Significant features of assessment unclear or you are unsure of cause? <strong>Consult MO urgently</strong></li>
<li><strong>Always consider <a href="/docs/Emergency/Sepsisseptic-shock">Sepsis</a></strong></li>
</ul>

<h2 class="pccm-section-heading-blue" id="child-with-vomiting">Child with vomiting</h2>

<figure class="pccm-flowchart-figure">
<img src="/img/paediatrics/child-with-vomiting.png" alt="Child with vomiting differential diagnosis flowchart from Green Book" />
<p class="pccm-flowchart-caption">Child with vomiting (source page)</p>
</figure>

<div class="pccm-note-panel">
<ul>
<li><strong>Babies &lt; 3 months of age contact MO urgently</strong></li>
<li>Vomiting is a common and important symptom, which may indicate serious illness especially in a very young child. Be aware of vomiting without diarrhoea.</li>
<li>Causes may include: infection (pneumonia, UTI, meningitis, AOM), bowel obstruction (pyloric stenosis, intussusception, appendicitis, hernia), reflux esophagitis, ↑ ICP (trauma, abscess or tumour), metabolic (diabetic ketoacidosis, poisoning)</li>
<li>Also see <a href="/docs/Pain,%20nausea%20and%20vomiting/Nausea-and-vomiting">Nausea and vomiting</a>. Always consider <a href="/docs/Emergency/Sepsisseptic-shock">Sepsis</a>, and <a href="/docs/Emergency/Button-battery-ingestioninsertion">Button battery</a></li>
<li>Significant features of assessment unclear or you are unsure of cause, or if bile or blood stained — <strong>Consult MO urgently</strong></li>
</ul>
</div>

<h2 class="pccm-section-heading-blue" id="child-with-abdominal-pain">Child with abdominal pain</h2>

<figure class="pccm-flowchart-figure">
<img src="/img/paediatrics/child-with-abdominal-pain.png" alt="Child with abdominal pain differential diagnosis flowchart from Green Book" />
<p class="pccm-flowchart-caption">Child with abdominal pain (source page)</p>
</figure>

<div class="pccm-note-panel">
<ul>
<li><strong>For any child with significant pain + babies &lt; 3 months of age contact MO urgently</strong></li>
<li>Consult MO if any of:
<ul>
<li>Bile or blood stained vomit</li>
<li>Bloody stool</li>
<li>Distension or guarding</li>
<li>Localised or rebound tenderness</li>
<li>Palpable mass, vomit looks/smells like faeces - consider <a href="/docs/Emergency/Bowel-obstruction">Bowel obstruction</a></li>
<li>Inguinal-scrotal pain or swelling - assume Testicular torsion, until proven otherwise</li>
<li>Significant features of assessment unclear or you are unsure of cause</li>
</ul>
</li>
<li>Also see <a href="/docs/Emergency/Acute-abdominal-pain">Abdominal pain</a>. Always consider <a href="/docs/Emergency/Sepsisseptic-shock">Sepsis</a>, and Testicular torsion</li>
</ul>
