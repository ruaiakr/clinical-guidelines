<h1 class="pccm-page-title">Measles</h1>

<div class="pccm-guideline-panel">
<h2 class="pccm-section-heading">Recommend</h2>
<ul class="pccm-content-list">
<li>Vaccination with the measles, mumps, and rubella (MMR) vaccine is the best prevention strategy.</li>
<li>Early identification and isolation of measles cases are important to prevent outbreaks.</li>
<li>Supportive care is critical, especially for high-risk individuals (e.g., malnourished children, immunocompromised patients).</li>
</ul>
<h2 class="pccm-section-heading">Background</h2>
<ul class="pccm-content-list">
<li>Measles is a highly contagious viral disease characterized by a high fever, cough, conjunctivitis, and a characteristic red blotchy rash. It can lead to severe complications, especially in young children and those who are immunocompromised.</li>
</ul>
</div>

<h2 class="pccm-section-heading-blue" id="may-present-with">1. May present with</h2>
<ul class="pccm-content-list">
<li>High fever (often &gt; 38.3°C)</li>
<li>Cough, runny nose, and conjunctivitis</li>
<li>Koplik spots (small white spots inside the mouth)</li>
<li>Rash that begins on the face and spreads downward</li>
</ul>

<h2 class="pccm-section-heading-blue" id="immediate-management">2. Immediate management</h2>
<ul class="pccm-content-list">
<li>Isolate the patient to prevent transmission.</li>
<li>Provide antipyretics for fever and pain relief.</li>
<li>Hydration and nutritional support, especially if the patient is young or malnourished. See <a href="/docs/Paediatrics/Severe-Acute-Malnutrition">Severe Acute Malnutrition</a>.</li>
</ul>

<h2 class="pccm-section-heading-blue" id="clinical-assessment">3. Clinical assessment</h2>
<ul class="pccm-content-list">
<li>Assess the presence of Koplik spots and confirm the diagnosis.</li>
<li>Evaluate for complications such as <a href="/docs/Paediatrics/Pneumonia">pneumonia</a> or encephalitis.</li>
<li>Check immunization history and ensure the patient is isolated from non-immune individuals.</li>
</ul>

<h2 class="pccm-section-heading-blue" id="management">4. Management</h2>
<ul class="pccm-content-list">
<li>Administer supportive care, including hydration, fever management, and rest.</li>
<li>Monitor for secondary infections, especially pneumonia.</li>
<li>In high-risk groups, consider vitamin A supplementation to reduce the risk of complications.</li>
</ul>

<h2 class="pccm-section-heading-blue" id="follow-up">5. Follow-up</h2>
<ul class="pccm-content-list">
<li>Monitor for secondary infections (e.g., pneumonia, <a href="/docs/Ear,%20Nose%20and%20Throat/Acute-otitis-media">otitis media</a>) and other complications.</li>
<li>Follow-up visits within 2-3 weeks to ensure recovery and vaccination status.</li>
</ul>

<h2 class="pccm-section-heading-blue" id="referral-consultation">6. Referral/Consultation</h2>
<ul class="pccm-content-list">
<li>Refer to a pediatrician or infectious disease specialist if complications develop.</li>
<li>Consider referral for vitamin A supplementation if necessary.</li>
</ul>
