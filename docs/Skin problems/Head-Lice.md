<h1 class="pccm-page-title">Head Lice: (Pediculosis)</h1>

<div class="pccm-guideline-panel">
<ul class="pccm-content-list">
<li>The head louse is an obligate human parasite that lives exclusively on scalp hair. Transmission occurs mostly via direct head-to-head contact. The head louse can survive up to 4 days on fomites such as brushes, barrettes, and headgear; therefore, indirect transmission is possible.</li>
<li>Commonly seen in school age children- particularly girls.</li>
</ul>
</div>

<h2 class="pccm-section-heading-blue" id="immediate-management">1. Immediate management Not applicable</h2>

<h2 class="pccm-section-heading-blue" id="clinical-assessment">2. Clinical assessment</h2>
<p>Direct observation of adult lice and egg capsules (nits) is diagnostic:</p>
<ul class="pccm-content-list">
<li>Adult lice are small, wingless ectoparasites measuring 1-3 mm long with elongated bodies and 3 pairs of claw-like legs.</li>
<li>Nits (ovoid eggs about the size of a sesame seed) appear as 0.5- to 1-mm, gray-white specks that are firmly attached to individual hair shafts, typically located within a centimeter of the scalp.</li>
</ul>
<p>Excoriations, erythema, scaling, and/or papular urticaria may be present within the scalp, behind the ears, and along the neck as a result of a hypersensitivity reaction to live lice and resulting intense pruritus. Cervical and occipital lymph nodes may swell because of secondary bacterial infection.</p>
<p>Pyoderma in the scalp or on the posterior neck along with occipital and cervical lymphadenopathy should raise the suspicion for possible pediculosis infestation.</p>

<h2 class="pccm-section-heading-blue" id="management">3. Management</h2>
<ul class="pccm-content-list">
<li>Successful treatment requires elimination of all adult lice and eggs. This can be accomplished via manual removal of nits and lice, such as with a nit comb, or death of the active lice and nits.</li>
</ul>

<h2 class="pccm-section-heading-orange" id="pharmacological">Pharmacological</h2>
<ul class="pccm-content-list">
<li>Permethrin 1% or 5%, apply on head for 8-12hrs then rinse with warm water.</li>
<li>Infected- Give antibiotic- flucloxacillin (according to age and weight) or septrin if allergies to penicillin.</li>
</ul>
