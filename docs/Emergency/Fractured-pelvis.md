# Fractured pelvis - Adult/Child

Fractured pelvis - Adult/Child

•	 If suspected – need to be treated urgently because the patient can bleed and
loose about 4 liters of blood and develop hypovolumic shock
•	 Mechanism of injury is important to guide treatment plan
•	 Pelvic bind = pelvic fracture to help reduce internal bleeding

How to use a pelvic binder or a sheet if no binder :
A range of pelvic binders are available which aim to reduce and stabilise pelvic
fractures. Currently there is insufficient evidence to support one device over the
other. If no device is available, use a bed sheet to wrap around the pelvis. Pelvic
binders also help with:
•	 Prevention of re-injury from pelvic motion
•	 Decreasing pelvic volume
•	 Decreasing pain
•	 Controling bleeding
