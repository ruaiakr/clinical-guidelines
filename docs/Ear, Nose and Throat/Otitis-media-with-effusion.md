<h1 class="pccm-page-title">Otitis media with effusion (OME) - Child</h1>
<p class="pccm-page-subtitle">Fluid behind the intact eardrum(s), glue ear</p>

<div class="pccm-guideline-panel">
<h2 class="pccm-section-heading">Background<sup>1,2</sup></h2>
<ul class="pccm-content-list">
<li>If fluid persisting &gt; 3 months in both ears, treatment may be needed to help restore hearing loss</li>
</ul>
</div>

<h2 class="pccm-section-heading-blue">1. May present with<sup>1,2</sup></h2>
<ul class="pccm-content-list">
<li>Fluid behind intact eardrum(s) AND no symptoms:
<ul>
<li>detected by loss of movement of eardrum - shown by Type B tympanometry, pneumatic otoscopy or Valsalva eg on routine ear check</li>
</ul>
</li>
<li>Suspect if:
<ul>
<li>dull eardrum/light reflex missing ± eardrum retracted</li>
<li>grey-white fluid or bubbles behind eardrum - can be hard to see</li>
<li>± hearing loss - may be evident through speech delay/learning or behavioural issues</li>
</ul>
</li>
</ul>

<h2 class="pccm-section-heading-blue">2. Immediate management</h2>
<ul class="pccm-content-list">
<li>Not applicable</li>
</ul>

<h2 class="pccm-section-heading-blue">3. Clinical assessment<sup>1,2</sup></h2>
<ul class="pccm-content-list">
<li>Get history and examine ears as per <a href="/docs/Ear,%20Nose%20and%20Throat/Ear-assessment">Ear assessment</a>, including:
<ul>
<li>prior OME/ear infections, when, treatment, is child under ENT specialist</li>
<li>any concerns about hearing, speech and learning/behavioural problems, development</li>
<li>hearing test(s) - when, results</li>
<li>do vital signs</li>
<li>if possible, do valsalva, tympanometry or pneumatic otoscopy to confirm eardrum not moving.</li>
</ul>
</li>
<li>See <a href="/docs/Ear,%20Nose%20and%20Throat/Ear-assessment">Ear assessment</a>.</li>
</ul>

<ul class="pccm-content-list">
<li>Consider antibiotics for otitis media ONLY if symptoms of acute infection present<sup>3-5</sup></li>
</ul>

<h2 class="pccm-section-heading-blue" id="follow-up">5. Follow up<sup>1</sup></h2>
<ul class="pccm-content-list">
<li>Advise to be reviewed in 1 month</li>
<li>If bilateral OME resolved, or OME in 1 ear only, continue regular review of ears eg monthly</li>
</ul>

<h2 class="pccm-section-heading-blue">6. Referral/consultation</h2>
<ul class="pccm-content-list">
<li>If hearing impairment, speech delay or learning problem.</li>
</ul>
