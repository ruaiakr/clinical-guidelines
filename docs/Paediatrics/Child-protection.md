<h1 class="pccm-page-title">Child protection</h1>

<div class="pccm-guideline-panel">
<h2 class="pccm-section-heading">Recommend</h2>
<ul class="pccm-content-list">
<li>refer to local policy and procedures</li>
</ul>
</div>

<h2 class="pccm-section-heading-blue" id="may-present-with">1. May present with<sup>1</sup></h2>
<ul class="pccm-content-list">
<li>Direct or indirect discloses of abuse</li>
<li>Injuries eg facial, neck bruising, fractures especially &lt; 3 years of age</li>
<li>Signs of neglect eg untreated physical problems, developmental delay</li>
<li>Parental risk factors eg domestic and family violence, substance misuse, mental health concerns</li>
</ul>

<h2 class="pccm-section-heading-blue" id="immediate-management">2. Immediate management</h2>
<ul class="pccm-content-list">
<li>Assess and treat any injuries</li>
<li>Contact MO if:
<ul>
<li>traumatic injuries eg head, chest, abdominal</li>
<li>ongoing management as per MO</li>
</ul>
</li>
<li>If suspected sexual assault, also see Sexual assault</li>
</ul>

<h2 class="pccm-section-heading-blue" id="clinical-assessment">3. Clinical assessment<sup>1</sup></h2>
<ul class="pccm-content-list">
<li>Ask about:
<ul>
<li>who is the carer/guardian</li>
<li>if injured:
<ul>
<li>what happened, where, when, who was there</li>
<li>does injury(s) fit with the explanation</li>
</ul>
</li>
<li>previous injuries</li>
<li>medical problems eg non organic failure to thrive</li>
<li>is there a delay in seeking medical attention</li>
<li>social situation eg domestic and family violence</li>
<li>engagement of typical activities eg school attendance</li>
</ul>
</li>
<li>Do a comprehensive/detailed - assessment and documentation of any injuries, bruising, scarring however minor:
<ul>
<li>check the whole body and use diagrams if required +</li>
<li>vital signs</li>
</ul>
</li>
<li>Observe parent-child interactions, any:
<ul>
<li>cowering, hyper-vigilance, elevated startle responses</li>
</ul>
</li>
<li>Consider non-accidental injury if:
<ul>
<li>patterned skin injury, bruise, abrasion, burn you recognise eg hand, belt/buckle, cigarette</li>
<li>bruising to face, head, ears, bottom, arms</li>
<li>&lt; 12 months of age with any bruising or skin injury</li>
<li>&lt; 3 years of age with fracture(s)</li>
</ul>
</li>
</ul>

<h2 class="pccm-section-heading-blue" id="management">4. Management<sup>1</sup></h2>
<ul class="pccm-content-list">
<li>If non-accidental injury is suspected, contact MO:
<ul>
<li>head injuries + fractures may not be obvious clinically + require further investigation eg CT scan/skeletal survey</li>
</ul>
</li>
<li>If you have formed a reasonable suspicion that:
<ul>
<li>a child has suffered, is suffering or likely to suffer significant harm AND</li>
<li>may not have a parent able and willing to protect them
<ul>
<li>document the date, time + name of the person you spoke to in the patient’s medical record</li>
<li>forward a copy of the form to the Child Protection Liaison Officer (CPLO)
<ul>
<li>notify local clinic management as required</li>
</ul>
</li>
</ul>
</li>
</ul>
</li>
</ul>
