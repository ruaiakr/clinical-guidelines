<h1 class="pccm-page-title">General Guidlines</h1>

<ul class="pccm-content-list">
<li>Prescribe as few drugs as possible. Never use a drug unless there is a good indication. Every drug has potential side effects</li>
<li><strong>DO NOT</strong> give drugs just because patients want them. Educate patients about the body’s own ways of healing itself</li>
<li><strong>DO NOT</strong> prescribe antibiotics or antidiarrhoeal drugs for diarrhoea (exceptions are <a href="/docs/Infectious%20diseases/Typhoid">typhoid fever</a>, dysentery and <a href="/docs/Infectious%20diseases/Cholera">cholera</a>)</li>
<li><strong>DO NOT</strong> prescribe antibiotics for viral illnesses, such as influenza and URTI’s
<ul>
<li>If you cannot determine the cause of a fever, <strong>DO NOT</strong> treat it with antibiotics—observe the patient, encourage rest and plenty of fluids, and consult doctor</li>
<li>Ask if patient is allergic to any medication before prescribing drugs</li>
<li>AGE, HEPATIC (liver) AND RENAL disease all affect drug metabolism or excretion. Much lower doses may be needed and some drugs are to be avoided</li>
</ul>
</li>
<li><strong>DO NOT USE ASPIRIN IN INFANTS, CHILDREN, ADOLESCENTS</strong>
<ul>
<li>Give very clear instructions to all patients</li>
<li>Use drugs you are familiar with. Look up any drug you haven’t used before and/or consult doctor or pharmacist</li>
</ul>
</li>
</ul>

<h2 class="pccm-section-heading-orange" id="drug-use-in-children">Drug Use in Children</h2>
<ul class="pccm-content-list">
<li>Children differ from adults in their response to drugs</li>
<li>Special care is needed during the neonatal period—organs are immature and inefficient, therefore special dosing is required. See drug reference tables for neonates</li>
<li>Drug doses are based on body weight in kilograms (until 50 kg of weight). Use the Drug Reference Table and estimate weight by age <strong>ONLY IF YOU CANNOT WEIGH THE CHILD</strong></li>
<li>If the drug reference table lists mg dosage without volume in ml, use the following formula → see <a href="/docs/Safe%20use%20of%20Drugs,%20Including%20Antibiotic%20Use/Calculating-Liquid-Doses-When-Working-with-Medications">Calculating liquid doses when working with medications</a></li>
</ul>
