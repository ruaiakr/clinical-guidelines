Background
Congestive Heart Failure (CHF) is a condition caused by structural or functional
abnormalities of the heart leading to impaired cardiac output. It is not a disease
itself, but a syndrome resulting from other cardiac or systemic conditions.
CHF is commonly due to conditions such as ischaemic heart disease, valvular heart
disease, hypertension, hyperthyroidism, or cardiomyopathy. Less common causes
include congenital heart disease, infective endocarditis, myocarditis, cor pulmonale,
and pericardial disease. In many cases, CHF is in a compensated state until
decompensated by precipitating factors.
