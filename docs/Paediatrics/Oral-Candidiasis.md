<h1 class="pccm-page-title">Oral Candidiasis (Thrush) – Child</h1>

<div class="pccm-guideline-panel">
<h2 class="pccm-section-heading">Background</h2>
<ul class="pccm-content-list">
<li>Oral candidiasis (thrush) is a fungal infection caused by Candida albicans. It commonly occurs in young children, particularly infants, and may be associated with poor immune function, chronic illness, or recent antibiotic use. It can be passed on by contact with contaminated items, from the mother (especially if she has a fungal breast infection), or from the child’s own fungal nappy rash.</li>
</ul>
<h2 class="pccm-section-heading">Recommendations</h2>
<ul class="pccm-content-list">
<li>Treat with oral nystatin suspension and discontinue antibiotics as soon as clinically appropriate. Treat concurrent fungal nappy rash with nystatin cream. Maintain breastfeeding and promote good hygiene.</li>
</ul>
</div>

<h2 class="pccm-section-heading-blue" id="may-present-with">1. May present with</h2>
<ul class="pccm-content-list">
<li>No symptoms (mild cases)</li>
<li>Pain when feeding or refusal to feed (severe cases)</li>
<li>Diarrhoea</li>
<li>White patches on lips, gums, tongue, or palate</li>
<li>Red, inflamed base when patches are wiped off</li>
</ul>

<h2 class="pccm-section-heading-blue" id="immediate-management">2. Immediate management</h2>
<ul class="pccm-content-list">
<li>None required unless child is critically unwell or unable to feed</li>
<li>If very unwell or malnourished, consult doctor urgently</li>
</ul>

<h2 class="pccm-section-heading-blue" id="clinical-assessment">3. Clinical assessment</h2>
<ul class="pccm-content-list">
<li>Assess general appearance for signs of malnutrition or chronic illness</li>
<li>Examine lips, gums, tongue, palate, and pharynx</li>
</ul>

<h2 class="pccm-section-heading-blue" id="management">4. Management</h2>

<div class="pccm-drug-box" id="nystatin-suspension">
<div class="pccm-drug-header">
<span class="pccm-drug-name">NYSTATIN SUSPENSION</span>
<span class="pccm-drug-level">L2</span>
</div>
<div class="pccm-assessment-table-wrap" style="margin:0; overflow-x:auto;">
<table class="pccm-drug-table">
<tr><th>FORM</th><th>STRENGTH</th><th>ROUTE</th><th>DOSE</th><th>DURATION</th></tr>
<tr>
<td>Liquid</td>
<td>100,000 units/mL</td>
<td>Oral/NG</td>
<td>&lt;12 months: 100,000 units every 6 hours<br />&gt;12 months: 500,000 units every 6 hours</td>
<td>Continue for 2 days after lesions disappear</td>
</tr>
</table>
</div>
<div class="pccm-drug-notes">
<p><strong>Notes:</strong> Apply inside the mouth using dropper or syringe</p>
</div>
</div>

<div class="pccm-drug-box" id="nystatin-cream">
<div class="pccm-drug-header">
<span class="pccm-drug-name">NYSTATIN CREAM</span>

<tr>
<td>Topical</td>
<td>–</td>
<td>Skin</td>

<p><strong>Notes:</strong> For fungal nappy rash</p>
</div>
</div>

<ul class="pccm-content-list">
<li>Discontinue antibiotics as soon as possible if not essential</li>
<li>Advise caregiver to continue breastfeeding</li>
<li>Advise to wash hands and breasts before each feed</li>
</ul>

<h2 class="pccm-section-heading-blue" id="follow-up">5. Follow up</h2>
<ul class="pccm-content-list">
<li>Return if no improvement after treatment</li>
<li>Recurrent or non-resolving oral thrush may require HIV or TB screening (consult doctor)</li>
</ul>

<h2 class="pccm-section-heading-blue" id="referral-consultation">6. Referral / consultation</h2>
<ul class="pccm-content-list">
<li>Consult doctor if the child is severely malnourished, chronically ill, or has enlarged lymph nodes</li>
<li>Discuss with doctor if further investigation (e.g. HIV or TB screening) is needed in case of recurrent oral thrush</li>
</ul>
</div>
