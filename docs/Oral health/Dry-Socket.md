<h1 class="pccm-page-title">Dry Socket - Adult/Child</h1>

<div class="pccm-guideline-panel">
<h2 class="pccm-section-heading">Background<sup>1</sup></h2>
<ul class="pccm-content-list">
<li>Dry socket (alveolar osteitis) is when a blood clot disintegrates prematurely after a tooth/teeth extraction resulting in inflammation of the bone below</li>
</ul>
</div>

<h2 class="pccm-section-heading-blue" id="may-present-with">1. May present with<sup>1</sup></h2>
<ul class="pccm-content-list">
<li>Severe pain ± bad breath 1–4 days post tooth extraction</li>
</ul>

<h2 class="pccm-section-heading-blue" id="immediate-management">2. Immediate management</h2>
<ul class="pccm-content-list">
<li>Not applicable</li>
</ul>

<h2 class="pccm-section-heading-blue" id="clinical-assessment">3. Clinical assessment<sup>1</sup></h2>
<ul class="pccm-content-list">
<li>Ask about:
<ul>
<li>when tooth extraction occurred, pain</li>
</ul>
</li>
<li>Do vital signs</li>
<li>Inspect tooth extraction site:
<ul>
<li>typically the socket appears grey, non healing and is often filled with debris</li>
</ul>
</li>
</ul>

<h2 class="pccm-section-heading-blue" id="management">4. Management<sup>1</sup></h2>
<ul class="pccm-content-list">
<li>Offer analgesia – ibuprofen preferred.<sup>2</sup> See <a href="/docs/Pain,%20nausea%20and%20vomiting/Acute-pain">Acute pain</a>.
<ul>
<li>add paracetamol if needed</li>
<li>advise to take regularly, rather than as required, to achieve continuous pain relief</li>
</ul>
</li>
<li>Irrigate socket gently with warm sodium chloride 0.9% to remove debris</li>
<li>Place Alvogyl (antiseptic and analgesic) dressing loosely into socket:
<ul>
<li>does not require removal later. Note: do not use if allergic to iodine</li>
<li>if not available contact MO/dentist for further advice</li>
</ul>
</li>
<li>Should heal spontaneously within 2–3 weeks</li>
<li>If pain persists &gt; 3 weeks, or signs (eg pain, redness) outside of the tooth socket, consult MO/Dentist / dentist for review of diagnosis</li>
</ul>

<h2 class="pccm-section-heading-blue" id="follow-up">5. Follow up</h2>
<ul class="pccm-content-list">
<li>Advise patient to be reviewed daily initially. Redress the socket as needed</li>
<li>Consult MO/dentist if not improving</li>
<li>Refer for next dentist clinic visit</li>
</ul>

<h2 class="pccm-section-heading-blue" id="referral-consultation">6. Referral/consultation</h2>
<ul class="pccm-content-list">
<li>Consult MO/dentist as above. Consider telehealth consult with dentist</li>
</ul>
</div>
