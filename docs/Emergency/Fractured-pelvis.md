<h1 class="pccm-page-title">Fractured pelvis - Adult/Child</h1>
<p class="pccm-page-subtitle">loose about 4 liters of blood and develop hypovolumic shock</p>

<ul class="pccm-content-list">
<li>If suspected – need to be treated urgently because the patient can bleed and</li>
<li>Mechanism of injury is important to guide treatment plan</li>
<li>Pelvic bind = pelvic fracture to help reduce internal bleeding</li>
</ul>
<h2 class="pccm-section-heading-blue">1. May present with</h2>
<ul class="pccm-content-list">
<li>Suspected pelvic fracture post trauma eg:1<ul><li>tenderness of pelvic area</li><li>abnormal rotation of affected leg ± shock</li></ul></li>
<li>Major haemorrhage<sup>1</sup> + likely to have other injuries</li>
</ul>
<h2 class="pccm-section-heading-blue">2.	 Immediate management</h2>
<ul class="pccm-content-list">
<li>Do primary survey DRSCABCD as per Traumatic injuries + initiate any life saving measures</li>
<li>Consult MA / orthopedic surgeon urgently - suspected fractured pelvis requires.</li>
<li>Early notification of trauma for interfacility transfer (inside front cover)</li>
<li>Do not spring the pelvis to check for stability - may dislodge clots + cause catastrophic haemorrhage</li>
<li>Insert IVC x 2</li>
<li>Give IV morphine. See Acute pain</li>
<li>Only log roll to clear airway if suction is ineffective/not available - may cause haemodynamic Instability</li>
<li>Apply pelvic binder urgently to ↓risk of haemorrhage, even if low suspicion of fracture:<sup>3-5</sup><ul><li>slide under the legs + then buttocks<sup>6</sup></li><li>apply over the greater trochanters + symphysis pubis region, do not apply above iliac crests<sup>1</sup></li><li>secure firmly, should be able to insert 2 fingers between patient + binder<sup>7</sup></li><li>use sheet if binder not available</li></ul></li>
<li>When to use the pelvic binder: Mechanism of injury suggestive of pelvic fracture NO BINDER REQUIRED Pulse &gt; or SBP &lt; GCS 13 or less Distracting injury APPLY PELVIC BINDER Abnormal clinical assessment of pelvis No No No No No Yes Yes Yes Yes Yes How to use a pelvic binder or a sheet if no binder : A range of pelvic binders are available which aim to reduce and stabilise pelvic fractures. Currently there is insufficient evidence to support one device over the other. If no device is available, use a bed sheet to wrap around the pelvis. Pelvic binders also help with:</li>
<li>Prevention of re-injury from pelvic motion</li>
<li>Decreasing pelvic volume</li>
<li>Decreasing pain</li>
<li>Controling bleeding Application of the pelvic binder</li>
<li>Straighten both legs</li>
<li>Apply to skin directly</li>
<li>At the level of greater trochanter</li>
<li>Sam’s Sling is commonly found in most EDs – 3 sizes available (small, standard and large. If in doubt, use the standard size which is designed to fit 95% of the population) or a bed sheet</li>
<li>If appropriately applied the feet should be internally rotated</li>
</ul>
<h2 class="pccm-section-heading-blue">3.	 Clinical assessment</h2>
<ul class="pccm-content-list">
<li>Do secondary survey EFGHIJ as per Traumatic injuries</li>
<li>Also check:1<ul><li>lower leg - pulses, power, sensation - any ↓</li><li>for blood at urethral opening + scrotal bruising - in males</li><li>urinalysis - for blood if possible, or take from IDC if inserted</li></ul></li>
<li>May also have genitourinary + Abdominal injuries</li>
</ul>
<h2 class="pccm-section-heading-blue">4.	 Management</h2>
<ul class="pccm-content-list">
<li>Gently insert IDC if delay to evacuation<ul><li>Never attempt IDC in cases with bleeding urethra</li><li>Consult surgeon before inserting the IDC</li></ul></li>
<li>Important to stabilise patient before transferring to main hospital</li>
<li>Need IVF resuscitation 2 big IV canulas to insert: 2 litres crystalloid fluid to run fast and then reassess</li>
<li>Need regular opiod injection for pain</li>
<li>Transport on scoop where possible</li>
<li>Keep warm + closely monitor until evacuation:<ul><li>vital signs + signs of hypovolaemic shock</li><li>lower leg - pulses, power, sensation + pain</li></ul></li>
</ul>
<h2 class="pccm-section-heading-blue">5.	 Follow up</h2>
<ul class="pccm-content-list">
<li>As per MO advice</li>
</ul>
<h2 class="pccm-section-heading-blue">6.	 Referral/consultation</h2>
<ul class="pccm-content-list">
<li>MA to discuss with General Surgeon</li>
<li>Surgical Admission</li>
</ul>
