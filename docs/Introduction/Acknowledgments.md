**<span style="color: #EF6A4C;">Source attribution and adaptation notice</span>**

Except where specifically identified as Kiribati Original Protocols or from 
the Kiribati Health Workers Manual (Green Book)  the clinical protocols in 
this Manual are adapted from the Queensland Primary Clinical Care Manual 
(PCCM) published by the State of Queensland (Queensland Health) and 
the Royal Flying Doctor Service (Queensland Section). The adaptations 
were undertaken by the Ministry of Health and Medical Services (MHMS), 
Government of Kiribati, and reviewed by MHMS clinical experts, to ensure 
relevance and compliance for Kiribati. Changes include (but are not limited 
to) adjustments to:

* reflect the unique cultural context and health-system setting of Kiribati 
  and its people;
* align with MHMS policies and procedures and applicable national 
  regulations;
* reflect local medicine supply chains, the Kiribati pharmacy formulary/
  essential medicines list, and other operational constraints; and
* incorporate local clinical guidelines, including the Kiribati Antibiotic 
  Guidelines (Second edition, 2022) and any subsequent updates.
