<h1 class="pccm-page-title">Fractured jaw - Adult/Child</h1>
<div class="pccm-guideline-panel">
<h2 class="pccm-section-heading">Recommend</h2>
<ul class="pccm-content-list">
<li>Be alert to airway obstruction from bleeding or swelling</li>
</ul>
</div>

<h2 class="pccm-section-heading-blue">1. May present with</h2>
<ul class="pccm-content-list">
<li>Trauma to face + pain, swelling, bleeding, deformity, drooling</li>
</ul>
<h2 class="pccm-section-heading-blue">2.	 Immediate management</h2>
<ul class="pccm-content-list">
<li>Do primary survey DRSCABCD as per Traumatic injuries,</li>
<li>Assess against Criteria for early notification of trauma for interfacility transfer (inside front cover)</li>
<li>Consider need for c-spine immobilisation,</li>
</ul>
<h2 class="pccm-section-heading-blue">3.	 Clinical assessment</h2>
<ul class="pccm-content-list">
<li>Ask about mechanism of injury +<ul><li>timing + events surrounding the injury</li><li>any witnesses, any first aid</li></ul></li>
<li>Do vital signs + secondary survey EFGHIJ as per Traumatic injuries,</li>
<li>Assess as per Head injuries,</li>
<li>Do full facial examination, including:<ul><li>check for signs of fractured jaw:<ul><li>clenched teeth not ‘fitting together’ as usual/misaligned</li><li>limited or painful mouth opening<sup>1</sup></li><li>unable to maintain bite on tongue depressor while twisted<sup>2</sup></li><li>flattening, swelling of cheeks</li><li>difficulty swallowing<sup>2</sup></li></ul></li></ul></li>
<li>any other injuries eg:<ul><li>mouth + tongue - lacerations, swelling</li><li>broken teeth - keep knocked out teeth for possible replacement. See Trauma to teeth,</li><li>orbit - palpate for step defect, numbness - may indicate fracture</li></ul></li>
</ul>
<h2 class="pccm-section-heading-blue">4.	 Management</h2>
<ul class="pccm-content-list">
<li>Consult DO/MO who may consider:<ul><li>IV antibiotics, x-ray, nil by mouth</li></ul></li>
<li>evacuation/hospitalisation for surgical review</li>
<li>Offer analgesia. See Acute pain,<ul><li>consider ice packs + soft collar for comfort<sup>1</sup></li></ul></li>
<li>Check tetanus vaccination status and give booster if indicated.1 See Tetanus immunisation,</li>
<li>Consider Domestic and family violence,</li>
<li>Closely monitor until evacuation + frequent airway checks</li>
</ul>
<h2 class="pccm-section-heading-blue">5.	 Follow up</h2>
<ul class="pccm-content-list">
<li>As per MO/DO</li>
</ul>
<h2 class="pccm-section-heading-blue">6.	 Referral/consultation</h2>
<ul class="pccm-content-list">
<li>Refer to Oral Surgeon</li>
</ul>
