# Fitting - convulsions, seizures – Adult/Child

Fitting - convulsions, seizures – Adult/Child
Recommend 1,2
•	 Status epilepticus is continuous seizure activity or repeated seizures without full
recovery tonormal LOC between episodes - neurological emergency, aim to stop
seizure urgently1,2
Background
•	 Most fits are brief + end within 1–3 minutes without needing midazolam2
•	 Febrile convulsions occur with rapid  T in early acute illness in child aged
6 months–6 years1
