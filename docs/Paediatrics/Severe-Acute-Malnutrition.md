<h1 class="pccm-page-title">Severe Acute Malnutrition – Child</h1>
<p class="pccm-page-subtitle">(Kiribati Original Protocol)</p>

<div class="pccm-guideline-panel">
<h2 class="pccm-section-heading">Recommendations</h2>
<ul class="pccm-content-list">
<li>All children with severe acute malnutrition (SAM) should be assessed urgently.</li>
<li>If hypoglycaemia, hypothermia, infection or dehydration are suspected, initiate treatment immediately.</li>
<li>Children who fail the appetite test or present with danger signs require inpatient care and referral.</li>
<li>Begin F-75 therapeutic milk and administer antibiotics and rehydration as appropriate.</li>
<li>Monitor closely for hypoglycaemia, infection, overhydration and complications.</li>
</ul>
<h2 class="pccm-section-heading">Background</h2>
<ul class="pccm-content-list">
<li>Severe acute malnutrition is defined as:
<ul>
<li>Weight-for-height/length &lt; –3 SD (wasted)</li>
<li>Mid-upper arm circumference (MUAC) &lt; 115 mm</li>
<li>Bilateral pitting oedema (regardless of weight-for-height)</li>
</ul>
</li>
<li>Children may be stunted (weight-for-age &lt; –3 SD) but not wasted. These children do not require hospitalisation unless another serious illness is present. Treatment is similar for severe wasting and oedematous malnutrition (kwashiorkor).</li>
</ul>
</div>

<h2 class="pccm-section-heading-blue" id="may-present-with">1. May present with</h2>
<ul class="pccm-content-list">
<li>Noted weight loss or failure to gain weight</li>
<li>Poor appetite</li>
<li>Diarrhoea or vomiting</li>
<li>Sleepiness or lethargy</li>
<li>Head lag</li>
<li>Recurrent fevers or illness</li>
<li>Pallor</li>
<li>Poor feeding</li>
<li>Hypoglycaemia or hypothermia</li>
<li>Oedema (bilateral pitting feet/ankles)</li>
<li>Skin changes, oral thrush, ulcers, or eye signs (vitamin A deficiency)</li>
</ul>

<h2 class="pccm-section-heading-blue" id="immediate-management">2. Immediate Management</h2>

<h2 class="pccm-section-heading-orange" id="hypoglycaemia">Hypoglycaemia (RBS &lt; 3 mmol/L)</h2>
<ul class="pccm-content-list">
<li>If RBS unavailable, assume hypoglycaemia and treat.</li>
<li>Give 50 mL of 10% glucose/sugar solution orally or via NGT.</li>
<li>Begin F-75 as soon as possible and feed every 2 hours.</li>
<li>If unconscious, give IV 10% glucose (5 mL/kg) or NGT sugar solution. If not available, give 1 tsp sugar sublingually every 20 minutes.</li>
</ul>

<h2 class="pccm-section-heading-orange" id="hypothermia">Hypothermia (Temp &lt;35.5°C rectal / &lt;35°C axillary)</h2>
<ul class="pccm-content-list">
<li>Assume hypothermia if temp is low or not registering.</li>
<li>Feed immediately and 2-hourly.</li>
<li>Rewarm: wrap child, use heater/lamp (indirect), or skin-to-skin with mother.</li>
<li>Keep child covered, especially head.</li>
</ul>

<h2 class="pccm-section-heading-orange" id="dehydration-sam">Dehydration</h2>
<ul class="pccm-content-list">
<li>Suspect in children with diarrhoea or reduced urine.</li>
<li>Do not use IV fluids unless in shock.</li>
<li>Rehydrate slowly with ReSoMal orally or via NGT:
<ul>
<li>5 mL/kg every 30 minutes for 2 hours, then</li>
<li>5–10 mL/kg/hr for next 4–10 hours alternating with F-75.</li>
</ul>
</li>
<li>If rehydration needed &gt;10 hrs, replace ReSoMal with same volume of F-75.</li>
<li>In shock and unable to tolerate oral: use IV Ringer’s lactate + 5% dextrose or 0.45% saline + 5% dextrose.</li>
</ul>

<h2 class="pccm-section-heading-orange" id="infection-sam">Infection</h2>
<ul class="pccm-content-list">
<li>Assume infection in all SAM children.</li>
<li>Start broad-spectrum antibiotics:
<ul>
<li>Uncomplicated: Amoxicillin 30 mg/kg BID x 5 days</li>
<li>Complicated (lethargy, danger signs):
<ul>
<li>Benzylpenicillin 50,000 U/kg IV/IM q6h or</li>
<li>Ampicillin 50 mg/kg IV/IM q6h for 2 days</li>
<li>Gentamicin 5 mg/kg IV/IM daily x 7 days</li>
</ul>
</li>
<li>Optional: Metronidazole 7.5 mg/kg q8h x 7 days</li>
<li>Give measles vaccine if ≥6 months and unvaccinated (delay if in shock)</li>
</ul>
</li>
</ul>

<h2 class="pccm-section-heading-orange" id="electrolyte-imbalance">Electrolyte imbalance</h2>
<ul class="pccm-content-list">
<li>Give potassium 3–4 mmol/kg/day</li>
<li>Do not use diuretics</li>
<li>Avoid sodium-containing fluids</li>
</ul>

<h2 class="pccm-section-heading-orange" id="initial-feeding">Initial feeding</h2>
<ul class="pccm-content-list">
<li>F-75 therapeutic milk: 135 mL/kg/day ÷ 8 feeds (first 4 days)</li>
<li>If dehydrated or in shock, delay feeds until stabilised</li>
</ul>

<h2 class="pccm-section-heading-blue" id="clinical-assessment">3. Clinical Assessment</h2>
<ul class="pccm-content-list">
<li>Check general appearance and vital signs</li>
<li>Appetite test: if poor, manage as inpatient</li>
<li>Look for signs of dehydration, oedema, hypothermia, lethargy</li>
<li>Perform full physical exam, including:
<ul>
<li>Shock (cold hands, weak pulse, cap refill &gt;3s)</li>
<li>Severe pallor</li>
<li>Skin signs of kwashiorkor</li>
<li>Oral thrush, ulcers</li>
<li>Eye signs (dryness, corneal ulceration, Bitot’s spots)</li>
<li>Local infections (ear, skin, throat, chest)</li>
<li>Fever or hypothermia</li>
</ul>
</li>
</ul>

<h2 class="pccm-section-heading-blue" id="management">4. Management</h2>
<ul class="pccm-content-list">
<li>Treat underlying complications urgently (hypoglycaemia, dehydration, hypothermia, infection)</li>
<li>Monitor RBS if available; treat again if &lt;3 mmol/L</li>
<li>Continue 2–3 hourly feeding with F-75</li>
<li>Provide warmth and oxygen as needed</li>
<li>Monitor fluid intake, signs of overhydration (e.g. rapid weight gain, oedema)</li>
<li>Avoid high sodium fluids and diuretics</li>
<li>Support mother to feed, observe, and keep child warm</li>
<li>Identify and treat other infections (e.g. pneumonia, skin infection)</li>
</ul>

<h2 class="pccm-section-heading-blue" id="follow-up">5. Follow Up</h2>
<ul class="pccm-content-list">
<li>Feed every 2–3 hours for first days</li>
<li>Monitor weight, oedema, consciousness, temp</li>
<li>Monitor for overhydration</li>
<li>Reassess appetite daily</li>
<li>Provide micronutrients as per SAM guideline (not covered in this section)</li>
</ul>

<h2 class="pccm-section-heading-blue" id="referral-consultation">6. Referral / Consultation</h2>
<ul class="pccm-content-list">
<li>Discuss all SAM children with Medical Assistant or Medical Officer</li>
<li>Children with:
<ul>
<li>Failed appetite test</li>
<li>Severe oedema (+++)</li>
<li>Danger signs (shock, unconscious, persistent vomiting)</li>
<li>Suspected underlying disease (e.g. TB, HIV)</li>
<li>Require inpatient referral</li>
</ul>
</li>
<li>If unable to refer immediately, continue with feeding and supportive care</li>
</ul>
</div>
