<h1 class="pccm-page-title">Gum disease - Adult/Child</h1>
<p class="pccm-page-subtitle">Gingivitis, periodontitis, necrotising gingivitis</p>

<div class="pccm-guideline-panel">
<h2 class="pccm-section-heading">Background<sup>1,2</sup></h2>
<ul class="pccm-content-list">
<li>Gingivitis is inflammation of the gums caused by the presence of undisturbed plaque. If not managed appropriately, can lead to periodontitis (loss of bone/tissue that supports the teeth).</li>
</ul>
</div>

<h2 class="pccm-section-heading-blue" id="may-present-with">1. May present with<sup>1</sup></h2>

<h2 class="pccm-section-heading-orange" id="gingivitis-presentation">Gingivitis<sup>1</sup></h2>
<ul class="pccm-content-list">
<li>Red, swollen gums that bleed easily</li>
<li>Rarely painful</li>
</ul>

<h2 class="pccm-section-heading-orange" id="periodontitis-presentation">Periodontitis<sup>2</sup></h2>
<ul class="pccm-content-list">
<li>Bad breath, bad taste</li>
<li>Gum recession</li>
<li>Bleeding ± swollen gums</li>
<li>If severe, may have:
<ul>
<li>loose teeth, spaces between teeth</li>
<li>pain</li>
<li>abscess. See <a href="/docs/Oral health/Tooth-abcess">Tooth abscess</a> for management</li>
</ul>
</li>
<li>Rarely seen in children</li>
</ul>

<h2 class="pccm-section-heading-orange" id="necrotising-gingivitis-presentation">Necrotising gingivitis<sup>3</sup></h2>
<ul class="pccm-content-list">
<li>Bleeding gums, ulcers – may have grey membrane</li>
<li>Significant pain</li>
<li>Bad breath</li>
<li>± swollen lymph nodes, fever</li>
<li>Most common in young adult smokers. Rarely seen in children</li>
</ul>

<h2 class="pccm-section-heading-blue" id="immediate-management">2. Immediate management</h2>
<ul class="pccm-content-list">
<li>Not applicable</li>
</ul>

<h2 class="pccm-section-heading-blue" id="clinical-assessment">3. Clinical assessment</h2>
<ul class="pccm-content-list">
<li>Get history, including:
<ul>
<li>smoking</li>
<li>diabetes</li>
<li>teeth brushing/oral hygiene/dental history</li>
</ul>
</li>
<li>Do vital signs + BGL</li>
<li>Inspect lips, gums, teeth, tongue, lymph glands in neck</li>
</ul>

<h2 class="pccm-section-heading-blue" id="management">4. Management<sup>1-3</sup></h2>
<ul class="pccm-content-list">
<li>Offer analgesia as needed. See <a href="/docs/Oral health/Toothache">Toothache</a> for dental pain recommendations</li>
<li>Consult MO/dentist if:
<ul>
<li>severe ± systemic signs and symptoms eg fever, malaise</li>
<li>patient has an underlying medical condition eg poorly controlled diabetes, immunocompromised</li>
</ul>
</li>
</ul>

<h2 class="pccm-section-heading-orange" id="in-all-cases">In all cases<sup>1,2</sup></h2>
<ul class="pccm-content-list">
<li>Offer education on good oral hygiene. See the Chronic conditions manual<br /><a href="https://www.health.qld.gov.au/rrcsu/clinical-manuals/chronic-conditions-manual-ccm">https://www.health.qld.gov.au/rrcsu/clinical-manuals/chronic-conditions-manual-ccm</a></li>
</ul>

<h2 class="pccm-section-heading-orange" id="gingivitis-management">Gingivitis<sup>1</sup></h2>
<ul class="pccm-content-list">
<li>Refer to dentist for check-up and clean – removal of plaque and calculus</li>
<li>If pain limits ability to brush teeth/floss well:
<ul>
<li>consider short-term use of chlorhexidine gluconate 0.2% mouthwash</li>
</ul>
</li>
<li>Should resolve within 1 month</li>
</ul>

<h2 class="pccm-section-heading-orange" id="periodontitis-management">Periodontitis<sup>2</sup></h2>
<ul class="pccm-content-list">
<li>If child consult MO/Dentist – needs urgent paediatrician review to investigate underlying cause<sup>2</sup></li>
<li>Refer to next dental clinic for removal of plaque and calculus + ongoing care</li>
<li>Support patient to modify risk factors eg smoking, diabetes management<sup>2</sup></li>
<li>Antibiotics are rarely needed</li>
</ul>

<h2 class="pccm-section-heading-orange" id="necrotising-gingivitis-management">Necrotising gingivitis<sup>3</sup></h2>
<ul class="pccm-content-list">
<li>Refer to dentist as soon as possible – for debridement of plaque and calculus + ongoing care</li>
<li>Give metronidazole + advise to use chlorhexidine gluconate 0.2% mouthwash until pain reduces</li>
<li>Advise to stop smoking</li>
<li>Note: antibiotics alone, without debridement by dentist + improvement of oral hygiene will usually lead to recurrence</li>
</ul>

<h2 class="pccm-section-heading-orange" id="chlorhexidine-gluconate">CHLORHEXIDINE GLUCONATE MOUTHWASH</h2>
<div class="pccm-drug-box">
<div class="pccm-drug-header">
<span class="pccm-drug-name">CHLORHEXIDINE GLUCONATE MOUTHWASH</span>
<span class="pccm-drug-level">L3</span>
</div>
<div class="pccm-assessment-table-wrap" style="margin:0; overflow-x:auto;">
<table class="pccm-drug-table">
<tr><th>FORM</th><th>STRENGTH</th><th>ROUTE</th><th>DOSE</th><th>DURATION</th></tr>
<tr>
<td>Liquid</td>
<td>0.2%</td>
<td>Topical to mouth</td>
<td><strong>Adult</strong><br />10 mL 8–12 hourly<br /><strong>Child 6–12 years</strong><br />5 mL under adult supervision 8–12 hourly</td>
<td>5–10 days<br />Rinse or gargle for 1 minute, then spit out</td>
</tr>
</table>
</div>
<div class="pccm-drug-notes">
<p><strong>Offer CMI:</strong> Can cause altered taste, burning sensation, brown discolouration of teeth, tartar build up. Rarely severe allergy. Limit use to up to 2 weeks to minimise side effects</p>
<p><strong>Contraindication:</strong> Allergy to chlorhexidine – any route</p>
<p><strong>Management of associated emergency:</strong> Consult MO/Dentist. See <a href="/docs/Emergency/Anaphylaxis">Anaphylaxis</a>.</p>
</div>
</div>

<h2 class="pccm-section-heading-orange" id="metronidazole">METRONIDAZOLE</h2>
<div class="pccm-drug-box">
<div class="pccm-drug-header">
<span class="pccm-drug-name">METRONIDAZOLE</span>
<span class="pccm-drug-level">L3</span>
</div>
<div class="pccm-assessment-table-wrap" style="margin:0; overflow-x:auto;">
<table class="pccm-drug-table">
<tr><th>FORM</th><th>STRENGTH</th><th>ROUTE</th><th>DOSE</th><th>DURATION</th></tr>
<tr>
<td>Tablet</td>
<td>200 mg<br />400 mg</td>
<td>Oral</td>
<td><strong>Adult</strong><br />400 mg bd</td>
<td><strong>3–5 Days</strong></td>
</tr>
</table>
</div>
<div class="pccm-drug-notes">
<p><strong>Offer CMI:</strong> Avoid alcohol while taking and for 24 hours after finishing the course. Take with food to reduce stomach upset. May cause nausea, anorexia, abdominal pain, vomiting, diarrhoea, metallic taste, dizziness or headache</p>
<p><strong>Management of associated emergency:</strong> Consult MO/Dentist. See <a href="/docs/Emergency/Anaphylaxis">Anaphylaxis</a>.</p>
</div>
</div>

<h2 class="pccm-section-heading-blue" id="follow-up">5. Follow up</h2>
<ul class="pccm-content-list">
<li>If necrotising gingivitis advise to be reviewed daily until improving</li>
</ul>

<h2 class="pccm-section-heading-blue" id="referral-consultation">6. Referral/consultation</h2>
<ul class="pccm-content-list">
<li>Consult MO/dentist as above. Consider telehealth consult with dentist</li>
<li>If diabetic, consider referral to diabetic educator</li>
</ul>
</div>
