<h1 class="pccm-page-title">Problems of Thyroid Gland - Adult</h1>

<div class="pccm-guideline-panel">
<h2 class="pccm-section-heading">Background</h2>
<p>Thyroid gland problems encompass range of disorders that affect the thyroid's ability to produce hormones, leading to either an overactive (hyperthyroidism) or underactive (hypothyroidism) state. These conditions can manifest in various symptoms and may result from various causes, including autoimmune diseases, iodine deficiency, or thyroiditis.</p>

<h2 class="pccm-section-heading-blue">1. May Present with</h2>
<ul class="pccm-content-list">
<li>Can manifest in various ways, but common symptoms include changes in heart rate, weight, and body temperature, as well as fatigue, mood changes.</li>
</ul>

<h2 class="pccm-section-heading-blue">2. Immediate management</h2>
<ul class="pccm-content-list">
<li>Not applicable</li>
</ul>

<h2 class="pccm-section-heading-blue">3. Clinical assessment<sup>1</sup></h2>
<ul class="pccm-content-list">
<li>Thorough history-taking is vital, and the presence of any red flag symptoms or risk factors should be documented.</li>
<li>Red flag symptoms for head and neck cancer include:
<ul>
<li>a mass that has been present for &gt;2 weeks</li>
<li>a recent voice change</li>
<li>dysphagia or odynophagia</li>
<li>ipsilateral otalgia, nasal obstruction or epistaxis</li>
<li>unexplained weight loss or loss of appetite.</li>
</ul>
</li>
<li>Risk factors for head and neck cancer include:
<ul>
<li>smoking</li>
<li>alcohol use</li>
<li>age &gt;40 years</li>
<li>past history of previous head and neck malignancy</li>
<li>past history of head and neck cutaneous lesions.</li>
</ul>
</li>
</ul>

<h2 class="pccm-section-heading-blue">4. Management</h2>
<ul class="pccm-content-list">
<li>Refer suspicion cases for hyper and hypothyroidism for further investigation and treatment.</li>
<li>Consult for patient suspicious of neck malignancy (may need FNA, Biopsy to confirm a diagnosis.)</li>
</ul>

</ul>
