Child and adult
•	 Viral conjunctivitis:

–
usually in one eye, but often spreads to both

–
reassure patient usually self-limiting but may take weeks to resolve

–
do not give chloramphenicol2

–
lubricating eye drops may provide symptomatic relief
•	 Allergic conjunctivitis:

–
if patient has Allergic rhinitis, oral antihistamines may help2

–
antihistamine eye drops eg ketotifen, may help reduce symptoms and
lubricating eye drops may help to remove allergen1
•	 Bacterial conjunctivitis:

–
most cases will resolve in 7 days without treatment2

–
if marked symptoms eg purulent discharge, give chloramphenicol2
•	 Chlamydial conjunctivitis:

–

chronic
