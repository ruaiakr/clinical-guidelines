<h1 class="pccm-page-title">Acute lower leg ischaemia - Adult</h1>
<div class="pccm-guideline-panel">
<h2 class="pccm-section-heading">Background</h2>
<ul class="pccm-content-list">
<li>Caused by a sudden blockage of blood supply to limb needing urgent evaluation and management</li>
</ul>
</div>

<h2 class="pccm-section-heading-blue">1. May present with 1,2</h2>
<ul class="pccm-content-list">
<li>Sudden onset intense pain in 1 leg ±<ul><li>limb is pale, no pulse, pins and needles/paralysis, cold</li></ul></li>
</ul>
<h2 class="pccm-section-heading-blue">2.	 Immediate management<sup>1</sup></h2>
<ul class="pccm-content-list">
<li>Contact MO urgently + urgent evacuation - a longer transfer time = higher risk of limb loss</li>
<li>Get rapid history, including:<ul><li>leg + any other symptoms</li><li>past medical history - allergies, current medicines, including recent anticoagulants,</li><li>antiplatelets or thrombolytic agents eg warfarin, aspirin, clopidogrel<sup>3</sup></li><li>prior thromboembolic events, active bleeding, ↑ risk of bleeding/bleeding disorders eg</li><li>haemophilia<sup>3</sup></li></ul></li>
<li>Do vital signs + ECG<sup>2</sup></li>
<li>+ weight</li>
<li>Offer analgesia. See Acute pain</li>
<li>Assess limb - neurovascular observations<sup>4</sup> compare to other leg:<ul><li>pain, sensation, movement, pulses, capillary refill, warmth, colour, swelling</li></ul></li>
<li>Insert IVC + take bloods for baseline APTT (not on i-STAT), FBC</li>
</ul>
<h2 class="pccm-section-heading-blue">3.	 Clinical assessment</h2>
<ul class="pccm-content-list">
<li>As above</li>
</ul>
<h2 class="pccm-section-heading-blue">4.	 Management</h2>
<ul class="pccm-content-list">
<li>MO will consult General surgeon for advice, which may include IV heparin bolus ± infusion</li>
<li>Note: heparin is a high risk medicine and APTT has to be monitored (baseline + 4–6 hourly):</li>
<li>if APTT not able to be done on-site DO NOT give infusion or only start immediately prior to</li>
<li>evacuation<sup>5</sup></li>
<li>use local heparin protocol/heparin order form for doses</li>
<li>Monitor vital signs + neurovascular observations until evacuated</li>
<li>Protect limb. Use cage and heel pad if available - do not elevate<sup>1</sup></li>
</ul>
