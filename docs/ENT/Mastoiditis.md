# Mastoiditis - Adult/Child

Mastoiditis - Adult/Child
Recommend
ΓÇó	 Refer all suspected mastoiditis cases urgently.
ΓÇó	 Treat with IV antibiotics if available. If IV access is not possible, give oral
antibiotics while arranging urgent referral.
ΓÇó	 Consult MO for further assessment and referral.
Background
ΓÇó	 Mastoiditis occurs when an ear infection spreads to the mastoid bone (behind
the ear), causing swelling, redness, and a possible collection of pus under the
skin behind the pinna (outer ear).
ΓÇó	 This condition can become serious and requires urgent treatment to prevent
complications.
1.
May present with
ΓÇó	 Pain behind the ear
ΓÇó	 Fever
ΓÇó	 Possible discharge from the ear
2.	 Immediate management
ΓÇó	 Refer urgently to a health centre for prompt care
ΓÇó	 Administer IV antibiotics if available
ΓÇó	 If IV access is not possible, give oral antibiotics
ΓÇó	 Consult MO urgently
3.	 Clinical assessment
ΓÇó	 History of ear discharge
ΓÇó	 Swelling and redness behind the pinna
ΓÇó	 Pain on pressing the bone behind the pinna (post-auricular pain)
ΓÇó	 Fluctuant swelling (pus under the skin may feel like a fluid-filled sac)
ΓÇó	 Fever
ΓÇó	 Otoscopy may show pus in the ear canal
