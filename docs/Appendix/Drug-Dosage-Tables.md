# Drug Dosage Tables

DRUGS SAFE TO USE DURING PREGNANCY AND BREASTFEEDING
•	 Adrenaline
•	 Aminophylline (Theophylline)
•	 Amoxycillin, Ampicillin
•	 Cloxacillin
•	 Digoxin
•	 Ethambutol
•	 Erythromycin
•	 Ferrous Sulfate
•	 Folic Acid
•	 Hydralazine
•	 INH
•	 Insulin
•	 Methyldopa (Aldomet)
•	 Paracetamol
•	 Penicillin
•	 Pyrazinamide
•	 Salbutamol (Ventolin)
•	 Vaginal medications (Clotrimazole, Nystatin)
•	 Vitamins (except excess Vitamin A)
When in doubt consult with TCH doctor.
Calculating Liquid Doses When Working with
Medications
To calculate the volume of liquid medication required:
Dose you want (mg)×Volume on hand (ml)÷Dose on hand (mg)=Volume to
administer (ml)Dose you want (mg)×Volume on hand (ml)÷Dose on hand
(mg)=Volume to administer (ml)
Example:
If the order is for 250 mg, and the medication comes as 500 mg in 2 ml:
(250 mg)×(2 ml)÷(500 mg)=1 ml(250mg)×(2ml)÷(500mg)=1ml
