<h1 class="pccm-page-title">Open (compound) fractures - Adult/Child</h1>
<p class="pccm-page-subtitle">Defined as a fractures with a cut through the skin. The overlying skin is wounded</p>

<p>and the broken bone may be exposed. Compound fractures are known as open fractures. These fractures can become infected easily and need thorough cleansing and debriding as soon as possible after the injury.</p>
<p><strong>GENERAL CONSIDERATION:</strong></p>
<h2 class="pccm-section-heading-blue">1. Always treat as a trauma patients : Primary survey / secondary survey and</h2>
<p>admit</p>
<h2 class="pccm-section-heading-blue">2. Consult MA/Orthopedic surgeon – for urgent referral and treatment opinion</h2>

<h2 class="pccm-section-heading-blue">3. Open fracture treatment = exposed fracture bone to reduce + thorough wound</h2>
<p>wash + IV antibiotic (Reduce the risk of osteomyelitis) + TT injection  +  splint and elevate</p>
<h2 class="pccm-section-heading-blue">1. May present with</h2>
<ul class="pccm-content-list">
<li>Open wound on same limb segment as an associated fracture<sup>2</sup></li>
<li>Bone has broken the skin or mucous membrane ± visible bone<sup>1</sup></li>
<li>Wound may be small eg puncture<sup>3</sup></li>
</ul>
<h2 class="pccm-section-heading-blue">2.	 Immediate management</h2>
<ul class="pccm-content-list">
<li>Do primary + secondary survey as per Traumatic injuries.</li>
<li>Assess against Criteria for early notification of trauma for interfacility transfer (inside front cover)</li>
<li>Stop any bleeding by putting pressure on wound, not on exposed bone<sup>1</sup></li>
<li>If signs of neurovascular compromise contact MO urgently +<ul><li>thoroughly irrigate with sodium chloride 0.9%</li><li>give antibiotics + requires urgent reduction</li></ul></li>
<li>Immobilise/apply splint as per Simple fracture of limbs.<ul><li>irrigate with sodium chloride 0.9% if severely contaminated wound and remove all debris and foreign objects.2</li><li>apply a saline-soaked dressing covered with an occlusive layer 3,4</li><li>apply a splint to immobilise the limb</li><li>elevate the limb and advice to rest on the bed (not to mobilise)</li></ul></li>
<li>Insert IVC</li>
<li>Give analgesia. See Acute pain.</li>
</ul>
<h2 class="pccm-section-heading-blue">3.	 Clinical assessment 1,3</h2>
<ul class="pccm-content-list">
<li>Assess as per Simple fracture of limbs.<ul><li>ask if contaminated environment eg agricultural, sewage, fresh or salt water<sup>1</sup></li><li>assess neurovascular status distal to the fracture</li></ul></li>
</ul>
<h2 class="pccm-section-heading-blue">4.	 Management</h2>
<ul class="pccm-content-list">
<li>Urgently consult MO who may advise:</li>
<li>V Cloxacillin 1g IV Q6H</li>
<li>IV cefazolin ± IV metronidazole<sup>1</sup></li>
<li>x-ray, backslab, evacuation</li>
<li>neurovascular observations</li>
<li>nil by mouth when time of referral or medical evacuation is confirmed (6 – 8 hours NBM before surgical procedures carried out) METRONIDAZOLE L<sup>3</sup> FORM STRENGTH ROUTE DOSE DURATION Injection 500mg / 100mL IV Adult 500mg stat infuse over 20 minutes Child 12.5 mg/ kg (max. mg) Offer CMI: May cause nausea, anorexia, abdominal pain, vomiting, diarrhoea, metallic taste, dizziness, headache or thrombophlebitis Management of associated emergency: Consult MO. See Anaphylaxis.</li>
</ul>
<h2 class="pccm-section-heading-blue">5.	 Follow up</h2>
<ul class="pccm-content-list">
<li>As per MO advice</li>
</ul>
<h2 class="pccm-section-heading-blue">6.	 Referral/consultation</h2>
<ul class="pccm-content-list">
<li>All open fractures require consultation with local orthopaedic services</li>
<li>Surgical Admission</li>
<li>Need to refer to main hospital for imaging and proper wound wash</li>
</ul>
