<h1 class="pccm-page-title">Bronchiolitis - Child</h1>

<div class="pccm-guideline-panel">
<h2 class="pccm-section-heading">Background<sup>1</sup></h2>
<ul class="pccm-content-list">
<li>Bronchiolitis is a lower respiratory tract viral illness in infants &lt; 12 months of age (up to 24 months)</li>
<li>Also see Bronchiolitis - emergency management in children</li>
</ul>
</div>

<h2 class="pccm-section-heading-blue" id="may-present-with">1. May present with<sup>1</sup></h2>
<ul class="pccm-content-list">
<li>Consider bronchiolitis if history of URTI followed by onset of respiratory distress + fever and ≥ 1 of:
<ul>
<li>cough</li>
<li>tachypnoea</li>
<li>retractions</li>
<li>diffuse crackles or wheeze on auscultation</li>
<li>feeding difficulties</li>
</ul>
</li>
</ul>

<h2 class="pccm-section-heading-blue" id="immediate-management">2. Immediate management</h2>
<ul class="pccm-content-list">
<li>Always consider differential diagnosis eg:<sup>1</sup>
<ul>
<li><a href="/docs/Emergency/Sepsisseptic-shock">Sepsis</a>, Foreign body inhalation, <a href="/docs/Paediatrics/Pneumonia">Pneumonia</a></li>
</ul>
</li>
</ul>

<h2 class="pccm-section-heading-blue" id="clinical-assessment">3. Clinical assessment<sup>1</sup></h2>
<ul class="pccm-content-list">
<li>Ask about:
<ul>
<li>recent URTI symptoms</li>
<li>feeding/hydration:
<ul>
<li>inadequate feeds eg &lt; 50% of usual feeds over 12 hours</li>
<li>duration of feeds</li>
<li>number of wet nappies in last 24 hours</li>
</ul>
</li>
</ul>
</li>
<li>Do:
<ul>
<li>vital signs</li>
<li>listen to chest for crackles or wheeze</li>
<li>check ears</li>
<li>bare weight</li>
</ul>
</li>
<li>Assess for Risk factors for severe disease<sup>1</sup></li>
</ul>

<div class="pccm-alert-panel">
<strong>Risk factors for severe disease<sup>1</sup></strong>
<ul class="pccm-content-list" style="margin-bottom:0;">
<li>Born &lt; 37 weeks</li>
<li>&lt; 10 weeks of age</li>
<li>Underlying conditions eg congenital heart disease, Down syndrome, immunocompromised</li>
<li>Failure to thrive</li>
<li>Exposure to cigarette smoke</li>
<li>Breastfed for &lt; 2 months</li>
</ul>
</div>

<h2 class="pccm-section-heading-blue" id="management">4. Management</h2>
<ul class="pccm-content-list">
<li>Contact MO in all cases, urgently if moderate-severe</li>
</ul>

<h3 class="pccm-section-heading-orange" id="moderate-severe">Moderate-severe cases<sup>1,2</sup></h3>
<ul class="pccm-content-list">
<li>Require urgent evacuation</li>
<li>Monitor closely until evacuation:
<ul>
<li>vital signs + WOB</li>
<li>SpO<sub>2</sub></li>
<li>discontinue O<sub>2</sub> if SpO<sub>2</sub> persistently &gt; 92%</li>
<li>note: brief ↓ SpO<sub>2</sub> &lt; 92% does not require O<sub>2</sub></li>
</ul>
</li>
<li>Do NOT give corticosteroids, adrenaline (epinephrine) or nebulized hypertonic saline<sup>1-3</sup></li>
<li>Only give salbutamol on MO advice</li>
<li>Support parent/carer to offer small frequent feeds:<sup>1,2</sup>
<ul>
<li>if feeding inadequately MO may consider NG or IV hydration</li>
</ul>
</li>
</ul>

<h3 class="pccm-section-heading-orange" id="mild-symptoms">If mild symptoms</h3>
<ul class="pccm-content-list">
<li>If mild with risk factors, further observation/hospitalization may be required<sup>1</sup></li>
<li>Consider sending home in consultation with MO if:<sup>1,2</sup>
<ul>
<li>able to maintain SpO<sub>2</sub> in room air</li>
<li>feeding adequately</li>
<li>parent/carer can safely manage infant at home eg consider time of day, parent/carer understanding of condition, access to clinic</li>
</ul>
</li>
<li>Provide Advice to parents/carers before sending home<sup>1,2</sup></li>
</ul>

<div class="pccm-guideline-panel">
<h2 class="pccm-section-heading">Advice to parents/carers<sup>1,2</sup></h2>
<ul class="pccm-content-list">
<li>Most children are back to normal within 7–10 days. Cough may last up to 1 month</li>
<li>Bring your child back if you are worried or if any:
<ul>
<li>trouble with feeding and fewer wet nappies than usual</li>
<li>difficultly breathing</li>
<li>very sleepy, becomes pale or sweaty or begins to look blue in the skin</li>
<li>pauses between breaths</li>
</ul>
</li>
<li>Make sure your child is getting enough fluids. Smaller feeds given more often may help</li>
<li>Paracetamol may help if child looks uncomfortable</li>
<li>Keep child away from smoke</li>
<li>Prevent spread of infection by:
<ul>
<li>keeping your child away from other small children for the first few days of illness</li>
<li>washing your hands frequently</li>
</ul>
</li>
</ul>
</div>

<h2 class="pccm-section-heading-blue" id="follow-up">5. Follow up</h2>
<ul class="pccm-content-list">
<li>If not evacuated advise to be reviewed the following day or earlier if concerned:
<ul>
<li>consult MO if not improving</li>
</ul>
</li>
</ul>

<ul class="pccm-content-list">

</ul>
