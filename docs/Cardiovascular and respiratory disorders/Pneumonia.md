<h1 class="pccm-page-title">Pneumonia - Adult</h1>

<div class="pccm-guideline-panel">
<h2 class="pccm-section-heading">Recommend</h2>
<ul class="pccm-content-list">
<li>Pneumonia is a common and potentially serious lung infection requiring timely recognition and appropriate management.</li>
<li>Antibiotic treatment should be initiated early in suspected cases.</li>
<li>Patients with moderate or severe pneumonia may require referral or hospitalisation.</li>
</ul>
<h2 class="pccm-section-heading">Background</h2>
<p>Pneumonia is an acute infection of the lung parenchyma, often presenting with cough, fever, pleuritic chest pain, and shortness of breath. It is caused by a range of pathogens, including bacteria, viruses, and fungi, and its severity can vary widely. Risk factors include chronic illness, advanced age, recent travel, hazardous alcohol use, and exposure to environmental sources (e.g. muddy soil/water).</p>
</div>

<h2 class="pccm-section-heading-blue">1. May present with</h2>
<ul class="pccm-content-list">
<li>Pleuritic chest pain</li>
<li>Fever, rigors</li>
<li>Cough with sputum production, shortness of breath</li>
<li>Fatigue, muscle aches or generalised body pain</li>
</ul>

<h2 class="pccm-section-heading-blue">2. Immediate management Clinical assessment</h2>
<ul class="pccm-content-list">
<li>Take detailed history, including:
<ul>
<li>Chest pain, fever, rigors, night sweats</li>
<li>Cough and sputum characteristics (purulent or coloured)</li>
<li>Shortness of breath, increased respiratory rate</li>
<li>Existing chronic illnesses (e.g. diabetes, CKD, CLD), immunocompromised status</li>
<li>Hazardous alcohol intake</li>
<li>Recent travel or exposure to muddy soil or water</li>
</ul>
</li>
<li>Physical examination:
<ul>
<li>Observe work of breathing</li>
<li>Listen for decreased air entry, wheeze, crackles (do they clear with cough?)</li>
<li>Assess for dullness on percussion</li>
<li>Inspect skin for ulcers, abscesses, non-healing sores</li>
</ul>
</li>
</ul>

<h2 class="pccm-section-heading-blue">3. Clinical assessment</h2>
<ul class="pccm-content-list">
<li>Contact MO in all cases for further advice
<ul>
<li>May recommend antibiotics, sputum for MCS ± PCR, IV fluids, or evacuation</li>
</ul>
</li>
</ul>

<h2 class="pccm-section-heading-blue">4. Management</h2>
<ul class="pccm-content-list">
<li>Evacuation/hospitalisation is needed if any of the following:
<ul>
<li>RR ≥ 25/min, HR ≥ 100 bpm, sBP &lt; 90 mmHg</li>
<li>SpO<sub>2</sub> &lt; 92% on room air (or lower than normal in COPD)</li>
<li>Acute confusion</li>
<li>Comorbidities, social circumstances, or age ≥ 65 years</li>
</ul>
</li>
</ul>

<h2 class="pccm-section-heading-orange">CORB-65 Severity Score</h2>
<ul class="pccm-content-list">
<li><strong>C:</strong> acute Confusion</li>
<li><strong>O:</strong> Oxygen saturation ≤ 90%</li>
<li><strong>R:</strong> Respiratory rate ≥ 30/min</li>
<li><strong>B:</strong> low Blood pressure (systolic &lt; 90 mmHg or diastolic ≤ 60 mmHg)</li>
<li>Score ≥ 2 suggests severe CAP and need for inpatient management</li>
</ul>

<h2 class="pccm-section-heading-orange">Antibiotic Treatment</h2>
<ul class="pccm-content-list">
<li>Outpatient (if mild):
<ul>
<li>Oral amoxicillin or daily IM procaine penicillin</li>
<li>If penicillin allergy: doxycycline orally</li>
</ul>
</li>
<li>Inpatient (severe or moderate with CORB-65 ≥ 2):
<ul>
<li>Benzylpenicillin 2MU IV every 6 hours</li>
<li><strong>PLUS</strong> doxycycline 100 mg orally twice daily</li>
<li>Discontinue IV once clinically improving and switch to oral antibiotics to complete course</li>
</ul>
</li>
</ul>

<h2 class="pccm-section-heading-orange">Advise:</h2>
<ul class="pccm-content-list">
<li>Fever should reduce within a few days</li>
<li>Appetite and general wellbeing should improve progressively</li>
<li>Other symptoms may take weeks to fully resolve</li>
</ul>

<h2 class="pccm-section-heading-blue">5. Follow up</h2>
<ul class="pccm-content-list">
<li>Daily review until improvement, or earlier if symptoms worsen</li>
<li>Consult MO if no improvement or worsening</li>
<li>Continue follow-up at next scheduled clinic visit</li>
</ul>

<h2 class="pccm-section-heading-blue">6. Referral/Consultation</h2>
<ul class="pccm-content-list">
<li>Refer if:
<ul>
<li>No improvement after 3 days</li>
<li>Severe pneumonia suspected</li>
</ul>
</li>
</ul>
