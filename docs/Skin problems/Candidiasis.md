# Candidiasis (skin) – Adult/Child

Candidiasis (skin) – Adult/Child
Recommend
Treat localised skin candidiasis with topical antifungal cream. Refer if infection is
widespread, the patient is immunocompromised, or not improving with treatment.
Background
Candidiasis of the skin is a fungal infection caused by Candida species. It most often
affects warm, moist areas such as the axillae, groin, under the breasts, abdominal
folds, and the nappy area. Risk factors include antibiotic or corticosteroid use,
diabetes, obesity, immobility, and immunosuppression.
