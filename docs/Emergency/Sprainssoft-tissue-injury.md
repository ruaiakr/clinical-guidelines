# Sprains/soft tissue injury - Adult/Child

Sprains/soft tissue injury - Adult/Child
GENERAL CONSIDERATION:
•	 Be alert to associated injuries eg fractures + dislocations1
•	 In children consider fibula fracture, often misdiagnosed as a sprain
•	 Sprain is over stretching to incomplete or complete tear of the ligament2

May present with
•	 Injury + swelling, bruising + pain on movement2
•	 No fracture seen on x-ray
