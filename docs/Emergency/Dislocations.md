<h1 class="pccm-page-title">Dislocations - Adult/Child</h1>

<ul class="pccm-content-list">
<li>Pulling or pushing of a bone out of its normal position in the joint<sup>1</sup></li>
</ul>
<h2 class="pccm-section-heading-blue">1. May present with</h2>
<ul class="pccm-content-list">
<li>Injury + severe pain that is worse on movement + very apprehensive to move joint<sup>2</sup></li>
<li>± deformity, associated fractures</li>
<li>Reports of popping or joint rolling out of place - mainly shoulder dislocations<sup>1</sup></li>
</ul>
<h2 class="pccm-section-heading-blue">2.	 Immediate management</h2>
<ul class="pccm-content-list">
<li>Offer analgesia. See Acute pain</li>
<li>Support limb using pillows as needed + apply ice packs as tolerated</li>
<li>If simple dislocation eg patella, digit + skilled - quickly reduce to alleviate pain</li>
<li>Contact MO if signs of neurovascular compromise distal to the site eg:<ul><li>weakness or numbness</li><li>↓ or absent pulses, delayed capillary refill</li><li>changes in colour</li></ul></li>
</ul>
<h2 class="pccm-section-heading-blue">3.	 Clinical assessment</h2>
<ul class="pccm-content-list">
<li>Ask about mechanism of injury +<ul><li>Timing + events surrounding the injury. Any witnesses, any first aid</li></ul></li>
<li>Get past history:<ul><li>previous dislocations, when, was it reduced, if so how/which manoeuvre<sup>1</sup></li><li>any orthopaedic surgery or underlying conditions eg osteoporosis<sup>2</sup></li></ul></li>
<li>Do physical examination, including: 1,2<ul><li>vital signs</li><li>check range of motion - painful or limited + palpate joints above and below injury – any tenderness, swelling</li></ul></li>
</ul>
<h2 class="pccm-section-heading-blue">4.	 Management</h2>
<ul class="pccm-content-list">
<li>Consult MO who may advise:<ul><li>x-ray to check for fracture + if can be reduced locally, or</li><li>evacuation/hospitalisation</li></ul></li>
<li>Collaborate with MO on reduction manoeuvres - if skilled + appropriate. Consider:<ul><li>nil by mouth</li><li>IV analgesia/sedation</li><li>monitor neurovascular status before + after reduction:<ul><li>if shoulder - check sensation over deltoid muscle, nerve can be damaged during reduction</li></ul></li><li>after care eg x-ray + immobilise joint/limb<sup>2</sup></li></ul></li>
</ul>
