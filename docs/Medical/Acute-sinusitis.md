# Acute sinusitis - Adult/Child

Acute sinusitis - Adult/Child
Also commonly known as acute rhinosinusitis is a symptomatic inflammation of the
mucosal lining of the nasal cavity and paranasal sinuses.
1.
May present with1
•	 with purulent nasal drainage
•	 accompanied with nasal obstruction, facial pain/pressure/fullness.
2.	 Immediate management1
•	 Suspect Foreign body in child with:

–
any nasal occlusion, facial swelling ± pain, or smelly, purulent or blood-stained
nasal discharge
•	 Consult MA urgently if there are any signs of spreading infection:

–
acute onset confusion

–
Double or ↓vision

–
neck stiffness, severe headache, photophobia

–
swelling or cellulitis around eyes, forehead or face

–
bulging eyes, painful eye movements
3.	 Clinical assessment
•	 Get history, including:

–
onset, duration, severity of symptoms

–
facial pain/tenderness

–
fever, cough, nasal discharge - colour

–
immunocompromised
•	 Do physical examination, including:

–
any facial swelling + tenderness with gentle palpation

–
ENT
