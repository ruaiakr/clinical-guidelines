Otitis externa (acute) - Adult/Child
Ear canal infection, swimmer’s ear, tropical ear
1.
 May present with1
•	 Ear pain (can be severe), itch
•	 Tenderness on moving outer ear
•	 ± discharge
2.	 Immediate management Not applicable
3.	 Clinical assessment1
•	 Get history including:

–
onset, duration

–
swimming/water exposure or trauma to ear canal eg vigorous cleaning/
scratching, use of hearing aids, cotton bud.

–
prior otitis externa - when, treatment

–
immunocompromised, diabetes, recent radiotherapy
•	 Do vital signs
•	 Examine ears - often very painful, approach gently:

–
if discharge, take swab for MCS first

–
dry mop using Tissue spears, to clean any discharge/debris from canal. Do not
syringe with water2
•	 using otoscope, look for:

–
widespread redness/swelling of ear canal

–
discharge/debris/foreign body

–
eardrum - intact or perforated - may be too swollen to see. If no discharge,
consider

–
Tympanometry. Type A means the eardrum is intact1
•	 Is cause likely bacterial or fungal3

4.	 Management1
•	 Offer Painkiller:

–
Paracetamol: children: 10-15 mg/kg orally/ rectally every 4-6 hours when
required.
adults: 500-1000 mg orally every 4-6 hours when required.

–
Ibuprofen: children: 5-10 mg/kg orally every 6-8 hours when required,
adults: 200-400 mg orally every 4-6 hours when required.
•	 Start Oral antibiotic + ear drop if any of:

–
fever

–
cellulitis/redness extends outside the ear canal

–
immunocompromised, diabetes.
•	 Antibiotic: Amoxycillin

–
children ≤3 months of age: 30 mg/kg/day orally given in 2 divided doses.

–
children >3 months of age: 20-40 mg/kg/day orally given in 3 divided doses.

–
adults: 250-500 mg orally three times daily, or 500-875 mg orally twice daily.
Amoxicillin/clavulanate:

–
children ≤3 months of age: 30 mg/kg/day orally given in 2 divided doses

–
children >3 months of age: 20-40 mg/kg/day orally given in 3 divided doses

–
adults: 250-500 mg orally three times daily, or 500-875 mg orally twice daily
Dose refers to amoxicillin component.
Ciprofloxacin:

–
children: consult specialist for guidance on dose; adults: 500-750 mg orally
twice daily.
•	 Ear Drops:

–
Chloramphenicol 4-5 drops BD for 7 days

–
ciprofloxacin 4-5 drops BD for 7 days
•	 Advise:1,2

–
symptoms should improve after 2–3 days of treatment, with full resolution up
to 2 weeks4

–
keep ear dry + for 2 weeks after resolved. Use cotton wool balls with Vaseline®
over top while showering; avoid swimming
Ear Drops Advice – Otitis Externa
•	 If possible, ask someone else to help administer the drops
•	 Clean the ear first using Tissue Spears (As above) Ensure thorough cleaning,
especially in cases of fungal infection, as leftover spores may cause recurrence
•	 Warm the bottle of ear drops in your hand for 5–10 minutes before use
•	 Lie down with the affected ear facing up. Instill the drops, then gently press the
tragus (the small flap at the front of the ear) for 30 seconds. Remain lying with
the ear up for at least 3–5 minutes
•	 Stop using the drops if the person develops ringing in the ears, hearing loss, or
problems with balance
