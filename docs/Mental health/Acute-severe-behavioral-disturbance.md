<h1 class="pccm-page-title">Acute severe behavioral disturbance (ASBD)</h1>

<h2 class="pccm-section-heading-blue" id="may-present-with">1. May present with</h2>
<ul class="pccm-content-list">
<li>Violent behaviour or extreme agitation</li>
<li>Self-destructive, physically or verbally aggressive or threatening behaviour</li>
<li>Possession of a weapon with intent to use</li>
<li>Bizarre, disoriented behaviour</li>
<li>Hallucinations</li>
<li>Delusions</li>
<li>Withdrawn e.g refusing to talk</li>
<li>Current suicide attempt, or</li>
<li>Situational crisis</li>
<li>Family member seeking help for any of the above</li>
</ul>

<h2 class="pccm-section-heading-blue" id="immediate-management">2. Immediate management</h2>
<ul class="pccm-content-list">
<li><a href="/docs/Emergency/Basic-life-support">DRSABCD</a></li>
<li>If person has survived an attempted suicide or a self-harm event, manage accordingly:
<ul>
<li>E.g, hanging see <a href="/docs/Emergency/Traumatic-injuries">Traumatic injuries</a>, <a href="/docs/Emergency/Spinal-injuries">spinal injuries</a>.</li>
</ul>
</li>
<li>Consider injuries consistent with self-harm attempt</li>
</ul>

<h3 class="pccm-section-heading-orange" id="stay-safe">Stay safe:</h3>
<ul class="pccm-content-list">
<li>Never approach a patient who has a weapon.</li>
<li>If needed take protective measures e.g lock yourself in your clinic, leave your facility</li>
<li>Consider risks in the immediate environment e.g access to knives, scissors, IV poles</li>
<li>Note exits for escape</li>
<li>Remove other people and bystanders</li>
<li>Assess in an open space</li>
<li>Minimise distractions and give full attention to the patient</li>
<li>Use a calm, confident manner, avoid sudden or threatening gestures</li>
<li>Avoid prolonged eye contact, and do not confront, corner or stand over the patient</li>
<li>Be familiar with duress alarms</li>
</ul>

<h3 class="pccm-section-heading-orange" id="then">Then</h3>
<ul class="pccm-content-list">
<li>Call police if concerned about safety</li>
<li>Call Mental Health Doctor / DPNO</li>
<li>Ensure at least 2 staff are always on hand</li>
<li>Always remain with patient unless your safety is at risk</li>
</ul>

<h3 class="pccm-section-heading-orange" id="de-escalation">If required attempt de-escalation</h3>
<ul class="pccm-content-list">
<li>Listen. Allow patient to speak without interruption</li>
<li>Be empathic, non-judgemental and respectful</li>
<li>Monitor changes in mood or composure that may lead to aggression</li>
<li>Continually show respect and empathy for patient</li>
<li>Identify any patient needs that have not been met</li>
<li>Use a slow, clear and steady voice, and do not raise your voice</li>
<li>If the patient raises their voice, pause and listen to the patient vent their frustrations</li>
<li>For children, consider removing people who may be the source of violent or aggressive behaviour</li>
<li>If restraints is to be used, it should be undertaken in accordance with the KSPEC Training guidelines and the Mental Health Act 2022.</li>
</ul>

<div class="pccm-alert-panel">
<strong>Never undertake physical restraint</strong>
<ul class="pccm-content-list" style="margin-bottom:0;">
<li>In Outer Islands clinics and health centres lack the resources to undertake this procedure safely</li>
<li>As a matter of urgency staff are to:
<ul>
<li>Call the police</li>
<li>Protect themselves and others – retreat from the situation</li>
</ul>
</li>
</ul>
</div>

<div class="pccm-guideline-panel">
<h2 class="pccm-section-heading">For non-consenting or uncooperative patient</h2>
<ul class="pccm-content-list">
<li>Forms are required by law before examination or treatment</li>
<li>If brought in by police or hospital vehicle/ambulance (Mental Health – ACT, POLICY.SOPS)
<ul>
<li>In South Tarawa,
<ul>
<li>For a new case, police will bring patient to the Emergency Department for initial assessment and examination of the patient. If ED Dr finds that the patient has a mental disorder, patient will be referred to the Mental Health Doctor for involuntary admission at the Meeria ward.</li>
<li>For a relapse case, police will bring patient to the KTM clinic or Meeria ward for assessment and treatment.</li>
</ul>
</li>
</ul>
</li>
</ul>
<p>In Outer Islands, Police with family will bring patient (new case or relapse case) to the Medical Assistant for assessment and Examination. If MA finds that the patient has mental disorder and aggressive and can not be manage, MA will contact the Mental Health Dr for referral for further treatment.</p>
</div>

<div class="pccm-guideline-panel">
<h2 class="pccm-section-heading">If patient presents otherwise (e.g with family):</h2>
<ul class="pccm-content-list">
<li>If a new case, the family will take the patient to the Emergency Department for Assessment and Examination and if the ED Dr finds that patient has a mental disorder, patient will be referred to the Mental Health Doctor for admission and treatment at the Meeria ward.</li>
<li>If a relapse case, the family will take the patient to the KTM clinic or Meeria ward for assessment, treatment and admission.</li>
<li>In Outer-islands, family bring the patient (both new case and relapse case) to Health Center to the Medical Assistant for Assessment. Treatment will be provided and patient aggressive and violent, patient will be detain at the Police cell for safety and will be transferred to Meeria ward for further assessment and treatment.</li>
</ul>
</div>

<h2 class="pccm-section-heading-blue" id="clinical-assessment">3. Clinical assessment</h2>
<p><strong>Get history, including</strong></p>
<ul class="pccm-content-list">
<li>Current mental health management plan and related presentations</li>
<li>Establishing patient’s behaviour plus premorbid personality. Via family and friends if needed.</li>
<li>Do vital signs
<ul>
<li>BSL + SPO<sub>2</sub> to exclude glycaemic plus hypoxic causes of behaviour change.</li>
</ul>
</li>
<li>Do physical examination – consider injuries consistent with self-harm attempt</li>
<li>Do a Mental State Examination (MSE). See following box for observations plus example questions.</li>
<li>For children, assess for sudden or significant, unexplained changes of behaviour or emotional state such as
<ul>
<li>Unusual fearfulness or severe distress e.g. inconsolable crying</li>
<li>Self-harm or social withdrawal</li>
<li>Aggression or running away from home</li>
<li>Indiscriminate attention seeking with adults</li>
<li>Development of new behaviours e.g. soiling or wetting, thumb sucking</li>
<li>Consider alternate cause where child presentation is inconsistent with history. See <a href="/docs/Paediatrics/Child-protection">child protection</a></li>
</ul>
</li>
</ul>

<h2 class="pccm-section-heading-blue" id="management">4. Management</h2>
<ul class="pccm-content-list">
<li>Once imminent risk of harm to themselves or others has been addressed refer to relevant presentation:
<ul>
<li><a href="/docs/Mental%20health/Suicidal-behaviour">Suicidal behaviour</a></li>
<li><a href="/docs/Mental%20health/Psychosis">Psychosis</a></li>
<li><a href="/docs/Mental%20health/Depression-Bi-polar-disorders">Mood disorder</a></li>
<li><a href="/docs/Mental%20health/Anxiety-disorders-panic-attack">Panic attack</a></li>
<li><a href="/docs/Mental%20health/Dementia">Dementia</a></li>
<li><a href="/docs/Emergency/Delirium">Delirium</a></li>
</ul>
</li>
<li>Manage in consultation with Medical Assistant/Medical Officer + Psychiatrist or Mental Health Team</li>
<li>Only consider sedation (see flowchart on next page)
<ul>
<li>If attempts at de-escalation have been exhausted</li>
<li>To control severe behaviour disturbance for patient’s safety and safety of others</li>
<li>To allow diagnostic assessment + management</li>
<li>To relieve patient distress</li>
<li>If child, adolescent or medically frail patient – after consultation with MA/MO or Psychiatrist</li>
<li>For eligible adult only.</li>
</ul>
</li>
</ul>

<h3 class="pccm-section-heading-orange" id="family-support">For family members and relative of patients, including children:</h3>
<p>This may be a very frightening experience for them. Provide support</p>
<ul class="pccm-content-list">
<li>Consider immediate safety needs of vulnerable people for whom the patient has care responsibilities i.e. children</li>
<li>Provide a copy of any management plan</li>
</ul>
