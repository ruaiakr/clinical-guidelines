<h1 class="pccm-page-title">Anxiety disorders panic attack</h1>

<div class="pccm-guideline-panel">
<h2 class="pccm-section-heading">Background</h2>
<ul class="pccm-content-list">
<li>Unless complex or recurrent, most panic attacks will not require mental health intervention</li>
<li>For ongoing management of anxiety, see the Chronic conditions manual: <a href="https://www.health.qld.gov.au/rrcsu/clinical-manuals/chronic-conditions-manual-ccm" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">www.health.qld.gov.au/rrcsu/clinical-manuals/chronic-conditions-manual-ccm</a></li>
</ul>
</div>

<h2 class="pccm-section-heading-blue" id="may-present-with">1. May present with<sup>1,2</sup></h2>
<ul class="pccm-content-list">
<li>History of panic attacks</li>
<li>Fear they may collapse or die</li>
<li>Restlessness or feeling ‘keyed up’ or ‘on edge’</li>
<li>Trembling</li>
<li>Difficulty concentrating or mind ‘going blank’</li>
<li>Irritability and racing heart beat</li>
<li>Feeling like they can’t catch their breath, hyperventilating</li>
<li>Dizziness</li>
<li>Sleep disturbance</li>
<li>Preoccupation about attacks recurring</li>
</ul>

<h2 class="pccm-section-heading-blue" id="clinical-assessment">2. Clinical assessment</h2>
<p><strong>Get history, including:</strong><sup>2</sup></p>
<ul class="pccm-content-list">
<li>personal and family history</li>
<li>perform mood scale e.g. DASS (access online)</li>
<li>alcohol and drug use</li>
<li>symptoms, fears or phobias</li>
<li>what may be making them anxious. Can occur for no reason</li>
<li>what does it stop them doing</li>
<li>what do they fear will happen</li>
<li>how long have they had the symptoms</li>
<li>what led them to come today</li>
<li>medication use</li>
</ul>
<p><strong>Do physical examination +</strong></p>
<ul class="pccm-content-list">
<li>vital signs</li>
<li>BGL + SpO<sub>2</sub> to exclude glycaemic or hypoxic causes of behaviour change</li>
<li>ECG</li>
<li>bloods – TFT, UE, FBC</li>
</ul>

<h2 class="pccm-section-heading-blue" id="management">3. Management</h2>
<ul class="pccm-content-list">
<li>Simply sitting in the waiting room can help reduce anxiety</li>
<li>If overwhelming or unresolving panic attack, consult MO who may recommend stat dose of a benzodiazepine</li>
<li>Reassess medications and their effect on anxiety</li>
<li>Patient education:<sup>2</sup>
<ul>
<li>teach and practice breathing exercises</li>
<li>reassure that their disorder has been recognised and that help is available</li>
<li>anxiety is a normal arousal response to enable a person to focus and act quickly to a threat</li>
<li>anxiety in the face of no rational threat can escalate to a pronounced disabling anxiety state; a panic attack</li>
<li>treatments take time and may include medications i.e. antidepressants</li>
<li>lifestyle modification:
<ul>
<li>diet and nutrition</li>
<li>good sleep patterns</li>
<li>regular exercise</li>
<li>avoid caffeine, tobacco, alcohol, illicit drug use and overworking</li>
</ul>
</li>
<li>self-monitoring and recording of symptoms if they reoccur:
<ul>
<li>where and when do the symptoms happen</li>
<li>how do they respond e.g. escape, avoid, medications, substances</li>
<li>return if panic attacks persist</li>
</ul>
</li>
</ul>
</li>
<li>For recurrent panic attacks refer to mental health services for psychological interventions e.g. cognitive behaviour therapy</li>
</ul>

<h2 class="pccm-section-heading-blue" id="follow-up">4. Follow up</h2>
<ul class="pccm-content-list">
<li>Review the patient the next day</li>
<li>According to MO</li>
<li>If referral has been made to Mental Health Team see response time and action under <a href="/docs/Mental%20health/Acute-severe-behavioral-disturbance#follow-up">Mental health emergency</a></li>
</ul>

<h2 class="pccm-section-heading-blue" id="referral-consultation">5. Referral/consultation</h2>
<ul class="pccm-content-list">
<li>MO or psychologist</li>
<li>If complex or recurrent then psychiatrist and mental health services</li>
<li>Mental Health, Alcohol and Other Drugs (MHAODS) for problem alcohol or drug use</li>
<li>Social worker if the patient requires counselling or support navigating services</li>
<li>Consider child health services or Child and Youth Mental Health Services if concerned about child in care of patient. See <a href="/docs/Paediatrics/Child-protection">Child protection</a></li>
</ul>
</div>
