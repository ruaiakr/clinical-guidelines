<h1 class="pccm-page-title">Epiglottitis - Adult/Child</h1>

<div class="pccm-guideline-panel">
<h2 class="pccm-section-heading">Background<sup>1</sup></h2>
<ul class="pccm-content-list">
<li>A life-threatening condition caused by infection of the epiglottis + surrounding area</li>
<li>Since the Haemophilus influenzae type b (Hib) vaccine, epiglottitis is rare</li>
</ul>
</div>

<h2 class="pccm-section-heading-blue">1. May present with</h2>
<ul class="pccm-content-list">
<li>High fever, looks sick</li>
<li>Hyperextension of neck, may not move neck</li>
<li>Drooling saliva, difficulty swallowing</li>
<li>Stridor, muffled voice</li>
<li>Restless, unsettled</li>
</ul>

<h2 class="pccm-section-heading-blue">2. Immediate management</h2>
<ul class="pccm-content-list">
<li>Secure airway
<ul>
<li>Anticipate any airway obstruction but avoid distressing a child as may exacerbate symptoms:</li>
<li>Avoid examining the oral cavity and throat</li>
<li>allow the child to settle in the position most comfortable.</li>
</ul>
</li>
<li>The patient should be kept in an upright position as supine positioning can aggravate airway obstruction</li>
<li>Start urgent IV antibiotic chloramphenicol 25mg/kg up 1g IV 6hrly
<ul>
<li>Oral antibiotic if can’t access IV: amoxicillin clavulanate 500+125 mg (child 15 mg/kg amoxicillin component up to 500 mg) orally, 8-hourly.</li>
</ul>
</li>
<li>Corticosteroid:
<ul>
<li>Dexamethasone 10 mg IV, as a single dose; repeat at 24 hours if required (not recommended for children)</li>
</ul>
</li>
<li>IV Fluid to maintain hydration, especially if the patient is unable to swallow.</li>
</ul>

<h2 class="pccm-section-heading-blue">3. Clinical assessment<sup>1</sup></h2>
<ul class="pccm-content-list">
<li>Assess for any upper airway obstruction (stridor, muffled voice, difficulty breathing)</li>
<li>Also looking for drooling, and dysphagia.</li>
<li>Sore throat which is progressive rapidly.</li>
<li>High grade fever, sick looking.</li>
</ul>

<h2 class="pccm-section-heading-blue">4. Management</h2>
<ul class="pccm-content-list">
<li>Continue with Oxygen support, antibiotic and IVF Maintenance. See <a href="/docs/Emergency/Oxygen-delivery">Oxygen delivery</a></li>
<li>Urgent refer if shows sign of airway obstruction.</li>
</ul>

<h2 class="pccm-section-heading-blue">5. Referral/Consultation</h2>
<ul class="pccm-content-list">
<li>Consult every case diagnosed with epiglottitis</li>
<li>Refer if a patient shows airway compromise.</li>
</ul>
</div>
