Background

systemic complications. If left untreated, these infections can spread to adjacent
tissues, including the neck spaces, leading to severe conditions such as LudwigΓÇÖs
Angina. Early detection and prompt management are essential to prevent airway
compromise, sepsis, and other life-threatening consequences.

ΓÇó	 Follow standard infection control protocols to prevent cross-contamination and

ΓÇó	 Recognize red flags and symptoms that require urgent referral.
ΓÇó	 Provide early pain management and antibiotics as indicated.
ΓÇó	 Always ensure airway management is prioritized, especially if neck space
