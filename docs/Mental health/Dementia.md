<h1 class="pccm-page-title">Dementia – Adult</h1>
<p class="pccm-page-subtitle" style="color:#ef6a4c; font-weight:700; font-size:1.1rem; margin-top:-0.35rem;">Severe behavioral and psychological symptoms</p>

<h2 class="pccm-section-heading-blue" id="may-present-with">1. May present with</h2>
<ul class="pccm-content-list">
<li>A patient with known dementia plus</li>
<li>Psychosis, agitation or aggression</li>
</ul>

<h2 class="pccm-section-heading-blue" id="immediate-management">2. Immediate management</h2>
<ul class="pccm-content-list">
<li><a href="/docs/Emergency/Basic-life-support">DRSABCD</a></li>
<li>If a person is at risk of harm to themselves, others or their behaviour is associated with perceptual or thought disturbance, delirium or impaired impulse control, see <a href="/docs/Mental%20health/Acute-severe-behavioral-disturbance">Mental health emergency</a>,</li>
</ul>

<h2 class="pccm-section-heading-blue" id="clinical-assessment">3. Clinical assessment</h2>
<ul class="pccm-content-list">
<li>Assess + manage calmly in a quiet area, with a familiar person present</li>
<li>Get history, including:
<ul>
<li>current mental health management plan + related presentations<sup>2</sup></li>
<li>patient’s behaviour + personality prior to the current presentation</li>
<li>alcohol + drug use</li>
</ul>
</li>
<li>Identify the trigger (antecedent)<sup>1</sup>
<ul>
<li>side effects or toxicity from medicines</li>
<li>signs of infection or presence of wounds e.g. acute febrile illness + altered mental status</li>
<li>herpes simplex virus leading to encephalitis e.g. headache, seizures, focal neurological signs<sup>1,2</sup></li>
<li>acute thiamine deficiency e.g. ocular abnormalities, gait ataxia, inability to concentrate, apathy, impaired awareness of the immediate situation, spatial disorientation, confusion, psychosis + coma<sup>1,3</sup></li>
<li>environmental stimuli e.g. temperature extremes, noise
<ul>
<li>unmet needs e.g. hunger, thirst, warmth</li>
<li>patient-carer conflict</li>
<li>pain, constipation</li>
<li>vision or hearing difficulties</li>
<li>separation from family</li>
</ul>
</li>
</ul>
</li>
<li>What behaviour occurred:
<ul>
<li>does behaviour cause significant distress to themselves or others e.g. verbal aggression, use of a weapon<sup>4</sup></li>
</ul>
</li>
<li>What was consequences of the behaviour e.g. injury, fall</li>
<li>Consider elder abuse where presentation for non-accidental cause is inconsistent with history or is unexpected in older or vulnerable people</li>
<li>Do physical examination<sup>1</sup> +
<ul>
<li>vital signs</li>
<li>BGL + SpO<sub>2</sub> to exclude glycaemic or hypoxic causes of behaviour change</li>
<li><strong>bloods</strong> – FBC, TFT, PTH, UE, calcium, vitamin B12, folate, niacin, CHEM20, thiamine</li>
<li><strong>urine</strong> for MCS + drug screen</li>
</ul>
</li>
</ul>

<h2 class="pccm-section-heading-blue" id="management">4. Management</h2>
<ul class="pccm-content-list">
<li>Address any identified triggers e.g. polypharmacy, pain, <a href="/docs/Genitourinary%20emergencies/Urinary-Tract-Infection">UTI</a></li>
<li>Consult MO or general physician/geriatrician/psychiatrist who may order:
<ul>
<li>sedation for violence, agitation or aggression.<sup>4</sup> See <a href="/docs/Mental%20health/Acute-severe-behavioral-disturbance">Mental health emergency</a></li>
<li>thiamine for suspected thiamine deficiency<sup>1,3</sup></li>
</ul>
</li>
<li>Offer analgesia if indicated. See <a href="/docs/Pain,%20nausea%20and%20vomiting/Acute-pain">Acute pain</a></li>
<li>Develop a Behavioural management plan with patient, carer and family, specifically:
<ul>
<li>access to dangerous items</li>
<li>access to exits if patient is wandering</li>
<li>over-stimulation e.g. too many people in house, excessive noise, clutter</li>
<li>under-stimulation e.g. lack of activities or items of interest to patient</li>
<li>regular exercise</li>
<li>limiting access to alcohol or other drug use</li>
</ul>
</li>
<li>Consider transfer/evacuation for comprehensive management</li>
</ul>

<h2 class="pccm-section-heading-blue" id="follow-up">5. Follow up<sup>2</sup></h2>
<ul class="pccm-content-list">
<li>According to MO. Regular review if commenced on pharmacological management</li>
</ul>

<h2 class="pccm-section-heading-blue" id="referral-consultation">6. Referral/consultation<sup>2</sup></h2>
