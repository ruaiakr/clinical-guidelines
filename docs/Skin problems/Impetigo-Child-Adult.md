<h1 class="pccm-page-title">Impetigo Child/ Adult</h1>

<div class="pccm-guideline-panel">
<ul class="pccm-content-list">
<li>Impetigo is a common, contagious skin infection caused primarily by <strong>Staphylococcus aureus</strong> and <strong>Streptococcus pyogenes</strong>.</li>
<li>The infection can present in both bullous and non-bullous forms.</li>
<li>Untreated or recurrent impetigo may lead to complications such as <a href="/docs/Paediatrics/Acute-post-streptococcal-glomerulonephritis">post-streptococcal glomerulonephritis</a>; however, it can cause <a href="/docs/Paediatrics/Acute-rheumatic-fever">rheumatic heart disease</a> secondary to pyoderma or skin bacterial infection.</li>
</ul>
</div>

<h2 class="pccm-section-heading-blue" id="may-present-with">1. May present with</h2>
<ul class="pccm-content-list">
<li>Non-bullous- erythematous macules, papules or pustules with superficial erosions with honey-coloured yellow crust.</li>
<li>Bullous- vesicles or bullae (clear fluid filled) without crusting.</li>
</ul>

<h2 class="pccm-section-heading-blue" id="immediate-management">2. Immediate Management:</h2>
<ul class="pccm-content-list">
<li>Procaine Penicillin-Intramuscular (Not for Penicillin allergies)
<ul>
<li>Stat dose IM-
<ul>
<li>Child- 25-50mg/kg</li>
<li>Adult- 1.2 -2.4g</li>
</ul>
</li>
</ul>
</li>
</ul>
