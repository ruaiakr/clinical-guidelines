<h1 class="pccm-page-title">Child health visits (Kiribati Original Protocol)</h1>

<div class="pccm-guideline-panel">
<h2 class="pccm-section-heading">Recommend</h2>
<ul class="pccm-content-list">
<li>Monitor growth and development at regular intervals</li>
<li>Identify and follow up children at risk for poor health outcomes</li>
</ul>
<h2 class="pccm-section-heading">Background</h2>
<ul class="pccm-content-list">
<li>Child health visits are essential for the early detection of growth, developmental, or health concerns. These visits offer an opportunity to educate parents, prevent disease, and ensure the wellbeing of children. Special attention should be given to children with known risk factors, and all children should be assessed regularly, even if they appear well.</li>
</ul>
</div>

</ul>

<h2 class="pccm-section-heading-orange" id="children-needing-special-care">Children Needing Special and More Frequent Care</h2>
<ul class="pccm-content-list">
<li>Not breastfed</li>
<li>Birthweight &lt;2.5 kg</li>
<li>Complications during pregnancy</li>
<li>Multiple pregnancy</li>
<li>Congenital anomalies</li>
<li>Neonatal problems</li>
<li>Fourth child or more</li>
<li>Birth interval &lt;2 years</li>
<li>Sibling deaths</li>
<li>Chronic illness</li>
<li>Single parent household</li>
<li>Domestic violence or alcohol use in the family</li>
</ul>

<h2 class="pccm-section-heading-blue" id="at-first-visit">At First Visit</h2>
<ul class="pccm-content-list">
<li>Build rapport with the mother or caregiver</li>
<li>Explain the purpose of child health visits and growth monitoring</li>
<li>Take a full history and perform a thorough examination, including the hips and umbilical stump</li>
</ul>

<h2 class="pccm-section-heading-blue" id="at-each-visit">At Each Visit</h2>

<h3 class="pccm-section-heading-orange" id="history">1. History</h3>
<ul class="pccm-content-list">
<li>General health and recent illnesses</li>
<li>Feeding, sleeping, bowel and urinary patterns</li>
<li>Mother’s nutrition, rest, and wellbeing</li>
<li>Answer any questions or concerns</li>
</ul>

<h3 class="pccm-section-heading-orange" id="weigh-the-child">2. Weigh the Child</h3>
<ul class="pccm-content-list">
<li>Record on the growth chart</li>
<li>Assess for growth faltering or failure to thrive</li>
</ul>

<h3 class="pccm-section-heading-orange" id="examine-fully">3. Examine Fully</h3>
<ul class="pccm-content-list">
<li>Perform full physical examination:
<ul>
<li>Eyes, ears, mouth, chest, heart, abdomen, skin, genitals</li>
</ul>
</li>
<li>Developmental assessment
<ul>
<li>Every 3 months in the first year</li>
<li>Every 6 months thereafter</li>
</ul>
</li>
</ul>

<h3 class="pccm-section-heading-orange" id="advise">4. Advise</h3>
<ul class="pccm-content-list">
<li>Promote good nutrition and age-appropriate feeding</li>
<li>Encourage extra feeding during and after illness</li>
<li>Provide family planning counselling as appropriate</li>
</ul>

<h3 class="pccm-section-heading-orange" id="prevent-disease">5. Prevent Disease</h3>
<ul class="pccm-content-list">
<li>Check immunisation status at every visit</li>
<li>Immunise unless the child is acutely unwell or has a fever &gt;38.5°C</li>
<li>Follow the national Vitamin A supplementation protocol</li>
</ul>

<h2 class="pccm-section-heading-blue" id="immunisation-schedule">Immunisation Schedule</h2>
<div class="pccm-guideline-panel">
<h2 class="pccm-section-heading">Recommend</h2>
<ul class="pccm-content-list">
<li>Ensure all children are vaccinated according to the national immunisation schedule.</li>
<li>Administer vaccines at the correct age whenever possible. If delayed, vaccinate as soon as possible.</li>
<li>Do not give vaccines earlier than recommended.</li>
<li>Aim to fully vaccinate all children before their first birthday.</li>
<li>Always check the child’s immunisation status at every visit.</li>
<li>Give any missed doses as soon as they are due.</li>
<li>Observe the minimum interval between vaccine doses (refer to schedule).</li>
</ul>
</div>

<h2 class="pccm-section-heading-blue" id="growth-monitoring">Growth Monitoring</h2>
<div class="pccm-guideline-panel">
<h2 class="pccm-section-heading">Recommend</h2>
<ul class="pccm-content-list">
<li>Record the child’s weight on the growth monitoring chart at every visit.</li>
<li>Always review the chart before examining the child.</li>
<li>Weight should follow an upward curve.</li>
</ul>
</div>

<h3 class="pccm-section-heading-orange" id="growth-key-points">Key Points</h3>
<ul class="pccm-content-list">
<li>The top line (100%) indicates expected (normal) weight.</li>
<li>The bottom line (80%) is the cut-off for underweight.</li>
<li>A flat or downward-sloping weight curve indicates possible malnutrition or illness.</li>
</ul>

<h2 class="pccm-section-heading-orange" id="consultation-referral-growth">Consultation/Referral</h2>
<p>Consult a doctor about any child who:</p>
<ul class="pccm-content-list">
<li>Shows no weight gain over time (flat line).</li>
<li>Has a falling weight (line slopes down).</li>
<li>Is at or below the 80% line on the chart.</li>
</ul>
<p>Refer to the WHO Weight-for-Age Charts for detailed guidance on expected weight ranges by age and sex.</p>

<h2 class="pccm-section-heading-blue" id="postnatal-examination">Postnatal Examination of the Infant</h2>
<p><strong>Physical Examination</strong><br />Adapt the sequence of the physical examination to the infant’s condition (awake, sleepy, or fretful).</p>
<ul class="pccm-content-list">
<li><strong>Weight</strong> – Compare the current weight to the birth weight and record both. Healthy infants typically gain about 28.5 grams (1 ounce) per day during the first six months of life.
<ul>
<li>If there is inadequate weight gain, inquire carefully about feeding practices.</li>
<li>Monitor more frequently (e.g., weekly) until weight gain is satisfactory.</li>
</ul>
</li>
<li><strong>Skin</strong> – Inspect the umbilicus for proper healing. Check for rashes, especially in the diaper area, and look for any skin sores.
<ul>
<li>Assess for jaundice.</li>
<li>If no BCG scar is visible by 3 months of age, repeat BCG vaccination – this is very important.</li>
</ul>
</li>
<li><strong>HEENT (Head, Eyes, Ears, Nose, Throat)</strong> –
<ul>
<li>Examine and palpate the head, fontanelles, neck, and clavicles.</li>
<li>Measure and record head circumference.</li>
<li>Inspect ears and eyes (use otoscope/ophthalmoscope if available).</li>
<li>Examine the mouth and palate. Palpate the neck for masses.</li>
</ul>
</li>
<li><strong>Lungs and Heart</strong> –
<ul>
<li>Auscultate the lungs.</li>
<li>Examine the back, spine, and buttocks.</li>
<li>Palpate the precordium and auscultate heart sounds.</li>
</ul>
</li>
<li><strong>Abdomen</strong> –
<ul>
<li>Palpate the abdomen for liver, spleen, kidneys, masses, or distension.</li>
</ul>
</li>
<li><strong>Femoral Pulses</strong> –
<ul>
<li>Carefully palpate femoral pulses bilaterally.</li>
</ul>
</li>
<li><strong>Genitalia</strong> –
<ul>
<li>Examine the genitalia. In male infants, palpate scrotum for testicles and check for hydroceles or hernias.</li>
</ul>
</li>
<li><strong>Extremities / Musculoskeletal</strong> –
<ul>
<li>Inspect femurs, thighs, and feet for deformities (e.g., torsion, metatarsus adductus, clubfoot).</li>
<li>Perform hip exams for clicks, restricted abduction, or subluxation.</li>
</ul>
</li>
<li><strong>Neurological / Developmental</strong> –
<ul>
<li>Observe alertness and activity when awake.</li>
<li>Assess cranial nerves (e.g., facial movement, visual tracking, suck reflex).</li>
<li>Check primitive reflexes: Moro, tonic neck, stepping.</li>
<li>Evaluate tone by:
<ul>
<li>Letting baby grasp fingers and pulling gently to assess head control.</li>
<li>Holding the baby prone over your palm.</li>
<li>Supporting under the axillae.</li>
<li>Gently shaking a limb to assess tone.</li>
<li>Checking for clonus.</li>
</ul>
</li>
<li>Confirm general development is appropriate for age.</li>
</ul>
</li>
</ul>

<h2 class="pccm-section-heading-blue" id="referral-consultation">Referral / Consultation</h2>
<ul class="pccm-content-list">
<li>Refer any infant with the following to a Paediatric Doctor</li>
</ul>

<h3 class="pccm-section-heading-orange" id="prematurity-lbw">Prematurity or Low Birthweight</h3>
<ul class="pccm-content-list">
<li>Born before 35 completed weeks</li>
<li>Birthweight &lt; 2.5 kg</li>
</ul>
