<h1 class="pccm-page-title">Calculating liquid doses when working with medications</h1>

<p><strong>Dose you want (mg) × Volume on hand ÷ Dose on hand (mg) = Amount (volume in ml) wanted or needed</strong></p>

<h2 class="pccm-section-heading-orange" id="example">EXAMPLE:</h2>
<ul class="pccm-content-list">
<li>Dose needed = FRUSEMIDE (LASIX) 15 mg IM</li>
<li>On hand you have Lasix 20 mg/2 ml</li>
<li>15 × 2 ml ÷ 20 = volume wanted</li>
<li>= 30 ÷ 20 = 1.5 ml (volume in ml needed)</li>
</ul>

<ul class="pccm-content-list">
<li>Parents are given a syringe (without needle) to measure liquid medicines administered by mouth—household spoons are not accurate for ml amounts of liquid medication</li>
<li>If tablets are to be crushed tell parents to cut tablet into 4 pieces with clean knife, then crush between 2 spoons. Medicine can then be mixed with banana, papaya or toddy</li>
<li>Parents should keep all medicines out of the reach of children</li>
</ul>
