<h1 class="pccm-page-title">Psychosis – Adult</h1>
<p class="pccm-page-subtitle" style="color:#ef6a4c; font-weight:700; font-size:1.05rem; margin-top:-0.35rem;">Drug-induced Psychosis, Schizophrenia, Post-natal Psychosis, Brief Psychotic Disorder, Bipolar Psychosis.</p>

<div class="pccm-guideline-panel">
<h2 class="pccm-section-heading">Background<sup>1</sup></h2>
<ul class="pccm-content-list">
<li>Psychosis is a general term used when a patient has lost some contact with reality and may have distorted thinking, perception and mood</li>
</ul>
</div>

<h2 class="pccm-section-heading-blue" id="may-present-with">1. May present with<sup>2</sup></h2>
<ul class="pccm-content-list">
<li>Delusions:
<ul>
<li>firm fixed belief of things that are not true e.g. the person must harm someone, their baby is the devil</li>
</ul>
</li>
<li>Hallucinations:
<ul>
<li>seeing or hearing things that are not there e.g. voices giving commands</li>
</ul>
</li>
<li>Disturbed sleep, walking or pacing at night</li>
<li>Very suspicious and paranoid of others</li>
<li>Family or friends report strange, disruptive or frightening behaviour</li>
<li>Women in the perinatal period</li>
<li>Intoxicated</li>
<li>Also consider <a href="/docs/Emergency/Delirium">Delirium</a> if:
<ul>
<li>disoriented to time and place</li>
<li>incoherent, loud, belligerent</li>
</ul>
</li>
</ul>

<h2 class="pccm-section-heading-blue" id="immediate-management">2. Immediate management<sup>2</sup></h2>
<ul class="pccm-content-list">
<li><a href="/docs/Emergency/Basic-life-support">DRSABCD</a></li>
<li>Do not leave patient alone</li>
<li>If person has survived an attempted suicide or a self-harm event, manage accordingly</li>
<li>Consider injuries consistent with self-harm attempt e.g. for hanging, see <a href="/docs/Emergency/Traumatic-injuries">Traumatic injuries</a>, <a href="/docs/Emergency/Spinal-injuries">Spinal injuries</a></li>
<li>If the patient is highly agitated, expressing an intent to suicide, aggressive or violent. See <a href="/docs/Mental%20health/Acute-severe-behavioral-disturbance">Mental health emergency</a></li>
</ul>

<h2 class="pccm-section-heading-blue" id="clinical-assessment">3. Clinical assessment</h2>
<ul class="pccm-content-list">
<li>Listen. Allow patient to speak without interruption</li>
<li>Enquire about but do not challenge their thoughts or behaviours</li>
<li>Develop and maintain rapport with the patient</li>
<li>Convey a sense of warmth, non-judgemental acceptance, and an interest in understanding the patient’s thoughts or behaviours</li>
<li>Be respectful and empathic
<ul>
<li>Avoid minimising their experiences</li>
</ul>
</li>
<li>Seek information from patients family, friends, support person or others</li>
<li>Get history, including:<sup>2,3</sup>
<ul>
<li>current mental health management plan and related presentations</li>
<li>behaviour and personality prior to the current presentation</li>
<li>adverse medication event</li>
<li>substance use including cigarette use</li>
<li>head injury, trauma or seizures</li>
<li>STIs</li>
<li>other psychiatric disorders</li>
<li>diet and exercise</li>
<li>if a woman in the perinatal period, assess for:
<ul>
<li>capacity to safely care for her infant (if delivered)</li>
<li>quality of mother-infant relationship e.g. cuddling, eye contact, responding to cues</li>
</ul>
</li>
</ul>
</li>
<li>Do physical examination +
<ul>
<li>vital signs</li>
<li>BGL + SpO<sub>2</sub> to exclude glycaemic or hypoxic causes of behaviour change</li>
<li>bloods – FBC, TFT, PTH, UE, calcium, vitamin B12, folate, niacin, CHEM20</li>
<li>urine for MCS and drug screen</li>
<li>pregnancy test if female of reproductive age – STI/BBV tests – HIV and syphilis</li>
</ul>
</li>
<li>Do Mental State Examination</li>
<li>Do risk assessments:<sup>3</sup>
<ul>
<li>depressive symptoms. See <a href="/docs/Mental%20health/Depression-Bi-polar-disorders">Mood disorders</a>,</li>
<li>suicidal intent. See <a href="/docs/Mental%20health/Suicidal-behaviour">Suicidal behaviour</a></li>
<li>neglect or victimisation by others</li>
<li>harm to others especially children in patient’s care</li>
</ul>
</li>
</ul>

<h2 class="pccm-section-heading-blue" id="management">4. Management<sup>2</sup></h2>
<ul class="pccm-content-list">
<li>Patients with psychosis are unlikely to be managed in the community. Consult MO who will:
<ul>
<li>plan immediate management and coordination</li>
<li>arrange evacuation for comprehensive management</li>
<li>order antipsychotic and sedative medicine as required. See <a href="/docs/Mental%20health/Acute-severe-behavioral-disturbance">Mental health emergency</a></li>
<li>if psychosis is due to an adverse effect to antipsychotic medication, may advise to cease medication</li>
</ul>
</li>
<li>Monitor closely if intoxicated</li>
<li>For women in the perinatal period consult the MO or midwife for further management options</li>
<li>Provide family support and education</li>
</ul>

<h2 class="pccm-section-heading-blue" id="follow-up">5. Follow up<sup>2</sup></h2>
<ul class="pccm-content-list">
<li>Consult MO, Mental Health Team/psychiatrist who will plan ongoing management and coordination</li>
<li>For Mental Health Team follow up response time and action, see Follow up under <a href="/docs/Mental%20health/Acute-severe-behavioral-disturbance#follow-up">Mental health emergency</a></li>
</ul>

<h2 class="pccm-section-heading-blue" id="referral-consultation">6. Referral/consultation</h2>
<ul class="pccm-content-list">
<li>MO and psychiatrist</li>
<li>Mental Health, Alcohol and Other Drugs (MHAODS) if problem alcohol or drug use</li>
<li>Social worker if the patient requires counselling or support navigating services</li>
<li>Refer women in the perinatal period to a midwife</li>
<li>Consider child health services or Child and Youth Mental Health Services if concerned about welfare of children in care of patient. See <a href="/docs/Paediatrics/Child-protection">Child protection</a></li>
</ul>
</div>
