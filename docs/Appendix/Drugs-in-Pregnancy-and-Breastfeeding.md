# Drugs in Pregnancy and Breastfeeding

The route of administration often depends on the severity of the infection. Serious,
life-threatening infections require IV antibiotics. When a patient is seriously ill, the
body is very busy fighting the infection, blood supply to the gut and muscles may
not be sufficient for drug absorption.
Duration of therapy depends on the nature of the infection and the response
to therapy. Always explain to patients the importance of taking the full course
of antibiotic. Prolonged courses, however, are costly, wasteful and may lead to
side effects. Certain infections do require long courses of antibiotics—such as
tuberculosis and osteomyelitis.
