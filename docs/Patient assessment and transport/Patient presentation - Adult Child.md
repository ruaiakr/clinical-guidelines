<h1 class="pccm-page-title">Patient presentation - Adult/Child</h1>
<div class="manual-full-page pccm-flowchart" role="region" aria-label="Patient presentation decision flowchart">
<div class="pccm-flow-box pccm-flow-box--outline">
  <div class="pccm-flow-heading">General principles<sup>1</sup></div>
  <ul>
    <li>The first priority is to assess whether the patient is seriously ill + needs immediate management, or is less acutely sick giving time to get a full history</li>
    <li>Always ask open ended questions</li>
    <li>In children, pay particular attention to history from parent/carer where available</li>
  </ul>
</div>
<div class="pccm-flow-arrow" aria-hidden="true">↓</div>
<div class="pccm-flow-box pccm-flow-box--blue">
  <div class="pccm-flow-heading pccm-flow-heading--center">Rapid assessment</div>
  <ul>
    <li><strong>D</strong>anger</li>
    <li><strong>R</strong>esponse</li>
    <li><strong>S</strong>end for help if unresponsive</li>
    <li><strong>A</strong>irway - compromised</li>
    <li><strong>B</strong>reathing - not breathing, significant respiratory distress</li>
    <li><strong>C</strong>irculation - pulse absent, slow, rapid or profuse bleeding</li>
    <li><strong>D</strong>isability - <strong>A</strong>lert, <strong>V</strong>oice, <strong>P</strong>ain, <strong>U</strong>nresponsive</li>
    <li>Rapid history, allergies, alerts</li>
    <li>Vital signs - RR, SpO<sub>2</sub>, HR, BP, T - use appropriate Early Warning Tool</li>
    <li>Consider BGL</li>
  </ul>
