<h1 class="pccm-page-title">Allergic rhinitis - Adult/Child</h1>

<div class="pccm-guideline-panel">
<h2 class="pccm-section-heading">Background</h2>
<p>Is an immunoglobulin E (IgE)-mediated inflammatory nasal condition resulting from allergen exposure in a sensitised individual, often associated with atopic dermatitis (eczema) and asthma often begin in childhood and young adult.</p>
</div>

<h2 class="pccm-section-heading-blue">1. May present with</h2>
<ul class="pccm-content-list">
<li>Nasal pruritus, sneezing, rhinorrhoea, and nasal congestion.</li>
<li>Frequently, there is associated palate, throat, ear, and eye itching as well as eye redness, puffiness, and watery discharge.</li>
</ul>

<h2 class="pccm-section-heading-blue">2. Clinical assessment<sup>1</sup></h2>
<ul class="pccm-content-list">
<li>Get history, including:<br />
Identifying triggers, noting the onset and duration of symptoms, and assessing for seasonal variations. It's also important to ask about other allergic conditions (asthma, eczema), family history of allergies, and response to previous treatments.</li>
<li>Physical examination of the nose is necessary. Findings may include swelling of the turbinate and nasal mucosa, the presence of clear nasal mucus and/or a pale nasal mucosa, and nasal crease (a transverse crease resulting from repetitive nose rubbing and manipulation).</li>
</ul>

<h2 class="pccm-section-heading-blue">3. Management</h2>
<ul class="pccm-content-list">
<li>Give antihistamine e.g. Cetirizine, children ≥6 months of age: 2.5 to 5 mg orally once daily when required; children ≥6 years of age and adults: 5-10 mg orally once daily when required or Promethazine 10-25mg oral 2-3 times a day.</li>
<li>If mild to moderate symptoms: intranasal corticosteroid nasal spray: beclomethasone nasal spray: (50 micrograms/ spray aqueous) children ≥6 years of age and adults: 50-100 micrograms (1-2 sprays) in each nostril twice daily.</li>
<li>Allergen avoidance: reducing exposure to relevant allergens such as dander, dust mite, and pollen</li>
<li>Nasal saline irrigation twice a day or can use sterile water 500mls add with 1 teaspoon of salt and ½ teaspoon of baking soda…can use the syringe to flush each nostril. (Note to keep your solution clean and safe avoid contamination.)</li>
</ul>

<h2 class="pccm-section-heading-blue">4. Follow up</h2>
<ul class="pccm-content-list">
<li>In 2-4 weeks and find out how well the symptoms are controlled.</li>
</ul>

<h2 class="pccm-section-heading-blue">5. Referral/Consultation</h2>
<ul class="pccm-content-list">
<li>If patient symptoms are severe and not respond to drug therapy.</li>
</ul>
</div>
